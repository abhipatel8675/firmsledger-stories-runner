## About

Bruce began making cutting boards as Christmas gifts using only a table saw and a belt sander; a friend's request for eleven more boards sparked the business.

The shop produces handcrafted cutting boards, end-grain chopping blocks, charcuterie boards, specialty shaped boards, epoxy coasters, and desk accessories. All items are made by hand in the small workshop beside the Graybills' house, using domestic and exotic woods sourced worldwide along with epoxy resin. In its first full year (2016), the company sold nearly 400 cutting boards.

Sider's Woodcrafting sells directly to consumers through its online store, serving the kitchenware and home-goods markets. Customers include home cooks, gift buyers, and enthusiasts of functional wood art. The product line ranges from small cheese boards to two-foot-long "mega" cutting boards and custom epoxy-inlaid pieces.

The Graybills emphasize turning everyday objects into functional works of art that showcase the natural color and grain of the wood. The business remains family-owned, with Bruce as owner and Mary providing support, alongside a small team of assistants and specialists.
