# Sider's Woodcrafting

**Old description:** Please contact us if you cannot find what you are looking for!

## About
Sider's Woodcrafting operates from a home-based workshop in Brewer, Maine. The company was founded in October 2015 by Bruce and Mary Graybill after the birth of their first child prompted Bruce to start making cutting boards as holiday gifts using a table saw and belt sander. What began as a personal project quickly expanded when a friend requested eleven more boards, turning the effort into a family-owned business.

The workshop produces handcrafted end-grain chopping blocks, cutting boards in various sizes, charcuterie boards, specialty shapes such as cribbage boards and guitar-shaped boards, and epoxy-resin coasters and clipboards. Each piece is made from a mix of domestic and exotic woods chosen to highlight natural color and grain, with epoxy accents using SUPERCLEAR epoxy. In its first full year of operation (2016), the shop completed just under 400 boards.

The company sells directly to consumers through its online store, serving home cooks, gift buyers, and woodworking enthusiasts. Products range from small cheese boards to two-foot-long "mega" cutting boards, with designs that double as functional kitchen tools and decorative items.

The Graybills emphasize that every item leaves their shop as a functional work of art, backed by a commitment to stand behind their craftsmanship. The business grew from a desire to create everyday objects that showcase the beauty of wood while maintaining a hands-on, small-scale approach.

# Sierra Components Inc

**Old description:** Sierra Components adds Component Design, Packaging and more to our list of services.

## About
Sierra Components Inc. (SCI) was founded in 1992 in Minden, Nevada, as a small family-run business. The company began with only a fax machine, a flow hood, and several nitrogen boxes, and has since grown into a multi-million-dollar distribution and processing facility specializing in silicon wafers and bare die. The president and founder brings over 40 years of bare-die distribution experience and 42-plus years in the microelectronics industry.

Today SCI operates as a value-added processor and distributor for the microelectronics and semiconductor sectors. Its services include visual inspection to MIL-STD-883 Method 2010 and MIL-STD-750 Methods 2072/2073, element evaluation and lot qualification to MIL-PRF-38534D (Levels H and K) and MIL-PRF-19500, alternate sourcing for obsolete or unavailable components, and wafer processing such as SAW sorting, inspection, and wafer probing. The company also manages surplus inventory programs that guarantee customers at least 50 percent of book value. A partnership with Kooker Technologies extends the offering to component design, packaging, and substrate fabrication with fast turnaround times.

SCI serves customers in the microelectronics, semiconductor, and defense-related industries that require qualified bare die, silicon wafers, and rigorous inspection and qualification services. Its client base includes organizations needing MIL-STD and MIL-PRF compliant processing, alternate component research, and inventory recovery solutions.

The company emphasizes personal service—no automated attendants—and a commitment to the highest standards of quality, reliability, and customer attention. Its positioning as “large enough to accomplish the task, and small enough to deliver personal attention” reflects a deliberate focus on responsive, relationship-driven support.
