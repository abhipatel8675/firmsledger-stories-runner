# Sonoran Stitch Factory

**Old description:** Sustainability: Build a sustainable, profitable, and human-centered production model

## About
Sonoran Stitch Factory operates in Tucson, Arizona, founded by Erica Yngve, a serial entrepreneur who also owns the brands Bralessly, Postcraft Products, and Palmfree Sunwear. Yngve established the factory to provide a sustainable, human-centered production model for sewn goods, drawing on her background in manufacturing operations and her experience advising small businesses and educational programs.

The company specializes in small to medium batch production of knit apparel and other sewn goods, offering end-to-end services that include production flow management, inventory control, fulfillment, and shipping. Since launch, the factory has sewn more than 160,000 products for over 27 clients, while also running a workforce development program that has trained 209 students and sponsored two apprentices.

Sonoran Stitch serves growing brands in apparel, fashion, sun-protective wear, and industrial sewn products for sectors such as housing, healthcare, hospitality, and cruise lines. Its client roster includes Yngve’s own labels—Bralessly (women’s comfort fashion), Postcraft Products (industrial sewn goods with a 90-year history), and Palmfree Sunwear (fingerless sun gloves for outdoor sports)—as well as external brands ready to scale beyond startup volumes.

The factory’s stated mission rests on three pillars: sustainability, quality, and community. It aims to build a profitable production model that prioritizes human-centered practices, deliver high-quality domestic manufacturing, and create pathways into manufacturing careers for youth through apprenticeships and training partnerships with local colleges.

# Souhrada Manufacturing Inc

**Old description:** using innovative materials, equipment and efficient production techniques.

## About
Souhrada Manufacturing Inc. is a family-owned industrial sewing company based in Tyndall, South Dakota. The business has operated for more than 26 years, producing custom sewn products for a range of industries.

The company specializes in interior trim products for the heavy-truck market, including vinyl drapes, window curtains, mesh cabinet net assemblies, and custom straps. Beyond truck components, it sews soft-sided luggage, graduation gowns, pouches, industrial covers, heavy-duty nylon straps for agricultural use, and fly-rod cases. Services cover large and small production runs, OEM manufacturing, design and development, contract sewing, screen printing, embroidery, tensile testing, and FMVSS302 flammability testing. The facility houses a full-service production area with industrial sewing machines from Brother, Juki, and Mitsubishi, programmable bar-tack equipment, die-cutting presses, automated strip cutters, and an Eastman Brute vertical knife cutter on a 60-foot spreading table.

Customers span the heavy-truck, agricultural, consumer-goods, and industrial sectors. The company works with OEMs and other buyers needing custom sewn assemblies, prototypes, and finished goods, often providing material sourcing, warehousing, and drop-ship logistics.

Souhrada emphasizes a personalized customer experience over scale, aiming to be "the best sewing company" rather than the largest. Its mission stresses high-quality, competitively priced products delivered on time, backed by a safe, rewarding workplace and long-term employee retention.
