## About
Second Sunrise Solar operates from Mt Pleasant, South Carolina, serving residential and commercial customers across the region. The company describes itself as family-run, a detail echoed in customer reviews, though no founding year or founder names are disclosed publicly. It began with a focus on guiding homeowners through the full solar adoption process, from initial consultation to long-term system monitoring.

The firm provides turnkey solar installation, handling design, permitting, financing, and grid interconnection in-house. Its service includes a Gold Star Certified Building Performance Institute (BPI) energy audit, followed by efficiency upgrades such as insulation, air and duct sealing, smart thermostats, radiant barriers, solar attic fans, LED lighting, and hybrid water heaters. All panels, racking, and inverters carry a 25-year parts and production warranty, while workmanship is covered for 10 years.

Customers include homeowners seeking to reduce utility bills and businesses facing rising commercial electricity rates. The company cites residential and commercial solar as its primary markets, with an emphasis on maximizing available tax credits and local incentives. Reviews reference projects in the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic, suggesting a multi-state service area.

Second Sunrise Solar differentiates itself by managing the entire project lifecycle rather than subcontracting installation, a model it says ensures quality control and cost containment. The BPI audit-first approach aims to reduce overall energy demand before sizing the solar array, a sequence the company says improves long-term financial returns.

# SenseICs Corporation

**Old description:** SenseICs is dedicated to reimagining the status quo, designing and building next-generation integrated circuits, and delivering the best possible solutions to meet the needs of our customers.

## About
SenseICs Corporation is based in Columbus, Ohio, at 1275 Kinnear Road. The company was founded by Dr. Ramy Tantawy, who serves as CEO, and Dr. Dale Shane Smith, who serves as president; both hold PhDs from The Ohio State University and bring decades of mixed-signal integrated circuit design experience from Intel, Micron Technology, and academic research.

As a fabless semiconductor provider, SenseICs designs and delivers turnkey integrated circuits and custom microelectronic solutions. Its expertise spans readout integrated circuits (ROICs) for imaging sensors, trusted microelectronics, radio-frequency design, high-speed communication, clock and data recovery, and radiation-hardened ICs capable of withstanding over 100 Mrad total ionizing dose. The company also develops diamond particle detectors and medical imaging electronics. Prototyping and production leverage multi-project wafer runs through foundry partners such as SkyWater, GlobalFoundries, and Tower Semiconductor.

SenseICs serves defense and commercial markets. Government customers include NASA, the U.S. Navy, Air Force, and Army Research Laboratory, as well as high-energy physics collaborations at CERN and Jefferson Lab. Commercial applications target AI infrastructure, advanced medical devices, robotics, and autonomous vehicles. The firm maintains close ties with The Ohio State University’s ElectroScience Laboratory and Nanotech West Lab.

The company’s stated mission is to deliver advanced turnkey ICs that drive next-generation sensors and systems for mission-critical applications. Its STRIDE values — Staying Curious, Trust, Resilience, Innovation, Diversity, Excellence — reflect a culture built on multidisciplinary collaboration and proven technology validation.
