# Sapphire Systems Inc

**Old description:** Soon we will be delivering high-performance windows to both commercial and military customers.

## About
Sapphire Systems Inc. was incorporated in 2007 by Charles Allmon and John Outwater in a garage laboratory to commercialize crystal-growth innovations. The company soon moved to a larger facility in Bedford, Massachusetts, along the Route 128 corridor northwest of Boston, to develop larger systems and begin commercial production.

The company grows optical-quality sapphire plates using the Edge-defined, Film-fed Growth (EFG) method, which it considers ideal for large-area plates. Current production yields plates 15‑5/8 inches wide, with standard plates of 30 by 15‑5/8 inches by 3/8 inch thick planned for pilot production; larger plates are available on request. Sapphire Systems has also produced and sold optical sapphire fiber and sapphire ribbon for superconductor substrates.

Its customers span military and commercial markets. For defense, the firm supplies sensor windows, transparent armor, and bullet‑proof windows used on range finders, infrared cameras, and personnel protection systems. In semiconductor manufacturing, its sapphire components serve as windows, wafer carriers, and other hardware that withstand corrosive chip‑fabrication environments.

Sapphire Systems differentiates itself through its patented EFG growth technology and the scale of its plates, which it describes as among the largest commercially available. The company holds three U.S. patents, an R&D 100 Award, and an SBIR Award for its superconducting wire epitaxial growth process.

SMI operates as a full-service metal fabrication and machine shop, offering laser cutting (including a 6 kW fiber laser), CNC bending (press brakes up to 12 feet wide), CNC milling and turning (Haas equipment), turret punching (two machines), plasma cutting, robotic welding, powder coating (automated line accommodating parts up to 60 inches wide), sanding, deburring, and PEM hardware insertion. The company originally specialized in ATV and UTV accessories—front and rear racks, drop racks, floor boards, gun racks, stick shifts, and similar items—custom-fitted to new models each year. Today, SMI serves a broader range of clients as a job shop, manufacturing, modifying, and repairing prototype and production parts for various industries across the southern United States, leveraging a network of partner shops for processes not performed in-house.

SMI emphasizes a quality system built on hiring for attitude, quality, cooperation, and growth, with quarterly employee reviews and multi-stage inspections (welders, buffers, painters, packers, shippers).
