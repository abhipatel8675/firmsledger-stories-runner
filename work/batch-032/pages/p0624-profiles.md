## About
Rheem Manufacturing Company is headquartered in Montgomery, Alabama. The company traces its roots back over a century, evolving from steel drum production to a global manufacturer of air and water products.

The company manufactures a comprehensive range of residential and commercial water heating systems—including tank, tankless, heat pump, point-of-use, and solar models—as well as heating and cooling equipment such as air conditioners, furnaces, heat pumps, ductless mini-splits, and boilers. Rheem operates as a global enterprise with more than 50 brands serving markets across the Americas, Asia, the Middle East, and Oceania.

Rheem serves three primary customer segments: residential homeowners seeking energy-efficient comfort solutions, commercial businesses requiring high-capacity water heating and HVAC systems, and professional contractors, plumbers, and installers who specify and install the equipment. The company supports professionals through training programs, marketing resources, and technical documentation.

Rheem differentiates itself through a century-long focus on innovation and a stated commitment to sustainability, aiming to reduce environmental impacts and customer utility costs. The company highlights its engineering expertise and global brand portfolio as a single source for air and water solutions.
