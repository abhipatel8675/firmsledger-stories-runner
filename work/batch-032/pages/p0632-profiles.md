## About

Output is limited to small‑batch, made‑to‑measure seating rather than volume runs; no facility size or headcount figures are published.

## About
Seaside Casual Furniture operates from Coventry, Rhode Island, where the brand was officially established in 1996. Its roots trace back to 1911 when Carold "Kit" Arnold opened the Arnold Lumber Company, a sawmill that evolved into a multi-generational family business. In the 1990s, "Art" Arnold Jr. began selling handcrafted mahogany Adirondack chairs from the lumber company's door shop, turning a hobby into a dedicated furniture line. The company was acquired in 2025 by The Prestwick Companies, a family-owned, Made-in-USA furnishings group based in Sussex, Wisconsin, with manufacturing remaining in Rhode Island.

The company engineers and manufactures luxury outdoor furniture using its proprietary EnviroWood, a non-porous, UV-stabilized recycled HDPE (also called Marine Grade Polymer) developed in 1999 to withstand coastal New England conditions. The product range spans over 400 SKUs, including Adirondack chairs, deep seating, dining and bar sets, fire tables, and planters. All pieces are built at the Rhode Island facility, and the company offers complimentary design services that provide layout planning, finish selection, and 3D renderings before production.

Seaside Casual serves residential homeowners, hospitality operators at high-end resorts and private estates, and commercial projects through a dealer network. Its furniture is specified for environments that demand resistance to salt spray, chlorine, oils, and extreme weather, with substantial weight to stay grounded in high winds.

The brand differentiates itself through a zero-maintenance material that runs UV-inhibited pigment through the entire profile, ensuring color retention for decades. The 1999 material innovation was driven by the need for furniture that could endure corrosive sea air, harsh winters, and relentless sun without sanding, oiling, or covering.
