Sana Packaging operates from Denver, Colorado, specializing in full-service cannabis packaging. The company does not publicly disclose its founding year, ownership structure, or the names of its founders.

Greg Sander has said the business exists to continue his father’s vision of keeping the Sander name on Main Street while providing a hands‑on resource for the local sewing community. Sandridge Crafted Foods traces its roots to 1960 when Vincent R. Sandridge began selling vacuum-packed deli meats in Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio. The company produces chef-crafted fresh foods including deli salads, soups, sauces, sous vide proteins, and assembled meal kits. A 2023-opened Center of Excellence in Medina, Ohio, houses a culinary R&D team that runs a “bench-to-box” development process. Facilities also feature blanching systems, vegetable prep lines, and cold-chain logistics managed through sister company MDS Inc.

Sandridge serves college and university dining, deli and prepared-food retailers, foodservice distributors, and healthcare operators. Its branded lines—Grandma’s®, Melch’s®, and the new Simply Crafted™—appear in retail and foodservice channels, while private-label and co-pack programs supply custom formulations for national and regional brands.

The founding vision centered on fresh, authentic food that fosters community. Leadership emphasizes culinary innovation backed by food-science rigor, a GFSI/SQFI-certified safety program, and a vertically integrated cold chain to deliver “remarkably fresh” products without artificial preservatives.
