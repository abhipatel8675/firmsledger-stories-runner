## About
SMR-Metal Technology operates from South Windsor, Connecticut, where it produces metal fabrications and assemblies for a range of industrial sectors. The company emphasizes that its parts are professionally and completely made in the United States.

The shop provides the entire manufacturing and fabrication process under one roof. Starting from CNC laser cutting, the team cuts, punches, bends, sands, deburrs, and mills parts, then joins them with both MIG and TIG welding before final assembly. Special finishes such as powder coating, painting, and plating are available on request. Materials handled include steel, stainless steel, aluminum, brass, and copper.

Its customer base spans automotive, locomotive, aerospace, medical, energy, marine, defense, communications, restaurant customization, and original equipment manufacturing (OEM) sectors. The company works from customer sketches or samples at any stage of the design process.

SMR positions itself as a single-source partner that can take a project from concept through finished part, offering both fabrication and finishing services without requiring the customer to manage multiple vendors.

# STI Electronics, Inc.

**Old description:** STI Electronics, Inc. is a leading provider of comprehensive solder and electronic assembly training, designed to enhance skills and meet the stringent requirements of the industry.

## About
STI Electronics, Inc. is headquartered in Madison, Alabama. The company was founded in 1982 by Jim D. Raby, P.E., in San Dimas, California, as a family-run business. It relocated to Madison in 1993 and has since grown to employ over fifty professionals.

The firm provides electronic contract manufacturing, engineering services, analytical testing, and IPC-authorized training. Its manufacturing capabilities include PCB assembly (surface-mount and through-hole), rapid prototyping, new product introduction builds, design-for-manufacturing review, rework and repair, conformal coating and encapsulation, and both leaded and lead-free soldering. Operations are supported by cleanroom facilities, failure-analysis labs, and materials-testing equipment at sites in Madison and Houston, Texas.

STI serves customers in aerospace, defense, medical, industrial, and energy markets, focusing on safety-critical and military-grade applications where precision and reliability are paramount. Its client base includes original equipment manufacturers and prime contractors requiring engineering-driven, low-to-medium volume production.

Raby’s background in developing NASA and Department of Defense soldering schools established a culture of technical rigor that remains central to STI’s differentiation. The company combines advanced manufacturing, analytical laboratories, and one of the industry’s most respected training programs under one roof to help customers reduce risk and solve complex manufacturing challenges.
