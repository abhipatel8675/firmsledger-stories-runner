# River Valley Woodworks

**Old description:** Every single day, old barns and old trees perish and fade from the earth. We give them new life.

## About
Based in Wilder, Idaho, River Valley Woodworks is a family-owned woodworking business that reclaims urban trees and salvaged barn wood to create live-edge slabs and custom furnishings. The company serves the Treasure Valley region, including Boise, from its shop on Hoskins Road.

The shop specializes in milling live-edge slabs and processing reclaimed lumber using a slow-drying kiln process and specialized equipment. It transforms logs harvested within a 50-mile radius into a range of products: conference tables, sliding barn doors, flooring, shelving, countertops, tables, structural beams, and home furnishings. The business also sells raw slabs to DIY woodworkers and partners with tree services to remove hazardous urban trees, often offsetting removal costs for homeowners.

Customers include residential homeowners, construction contractors, commercial property owners, and professional woodworkers seeking unique hardwood materials. The company's reclaimed inventory comes from dismantled barns, corrals, and log houses, providing a variety of specialty wood species.

River Valley Woodworks was founded on the premise that old barns and trees should be given new life rather than sent to landfills. The team emphasizes the natural character of live-edge slabs and aims to deliver reliable, distinctive wood products for both modern and rustic designs.

Robco works across residential, commercial, and industrial construction, with early roots in agricultural feed-mill projects. ## About
Robling Medical, LLC operates from Youngsville, North Carolina, where it has accumulated over 35 years of medical device manufacturing experience. The company focuses exclusively on medical devices, with more than 75 percent of its work in interventional and minimally invasive surgical applications.

As a full-service contract manufacturer, Robling handles design for manufacturing and assembly, prototyping, and benchtop performance characterization, with early emphasis on material, tooling, and process selection. Its capabilities span sterile disposable manufacturing — including bonding, tipping, heat sealing, ultrasonic welding, and kitting — as well as electro-mechanical assembly, box builds, cable and harness assembly, PC board flashing, and functional testing. The firm also provides vertically integrated packaging services, running high-speed Multivac lines with inline printing on Tyvek 1073B, paper, and poly film, and manages contract sterilization via ethylene oxide, gamma, and electron beam. Annual output exceeds 10 million sterile devices, from prototype runs to full-scale production.

Robling serves medical device and life sciences OEMs across a broad range of clinical markets: women’s health, urology, fluid management, cardiology, orthopedics, neurology, oncology, gastrointestinal, ENT, vascular, and respiratory. Customers include large, mid-sized, and newly commercialized MedTech original equipment manufacturers.

The company differentiates itself through cross-market experience that it says yields proven processes, faster ramp-up, and fewer surprises. Its quality system is described as flexible and collaborative, offering clients the option to use Robling’s QMS or work within their own. Quality certifications include MDD 93/42/EEC, Japan PMDA Foreign Manufacturer Accreditation, Brazil ANVISA certification, compliance with SOR/92-282, and Korean GMP compliance.
