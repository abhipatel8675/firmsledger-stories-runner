# River Valley Woodworks

**Old description:** Every single day, old barns and old trees perish and fade from the earth. We give them new life.

## About
River Valley Woodworks operates from Wilder, Idaho, as a family-owned woodworking business. The company was established to give new life to old barns and trees that would otherwise perish, though a specific founding year is not publicly disclosed. The shop serves the broader Treasure Valley region, including Boise.

The shop specializes in live-edge slabs and reclaimed wood, using a slow-drying process and specialized equipment to transform urban logs and dismantled barn timber into hardwood products. They source trees within a 50-mile radius and partner with tree services to reclaim urban trees, often offsetting removal costs for homeowners. Their output includes conference tables, sliding barn doors, flooring, shelving, countertops, tabletops, tables, beam construction, and home furnishings, sold both as custom pieces and as DIY slabs for other woodworkers.

Customers range from residential homeowners and DIY enthusiasts to construction contractors and commercial properties throughout the Treasure Valley and Boise area. The company also supplies inventory to other woodworkers who serve their own clients.

The business was started on the premise that every day old barns and trees fade from the earth, and they aim to give them new life by turning potential landfill waste into distinctive, reliable wood products.
