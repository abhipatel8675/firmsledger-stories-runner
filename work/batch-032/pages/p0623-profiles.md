# Renick Valley Meats

**Old description:** Your trusted source for commercial and custom processing and quality local meats in Renick, WV.

## About
Renick Valley Meats operates from Renick, West Virginia, where in 2022 Jesse Zimmerman and Anna Gajewski acquired the existing Countryside Meat Processing facility. The family-owned business rebranded to reflect its ties to the valley and its commitment to serving area farmers and families.

The company provides custom and commercial processing for beef, pork, lamb, goat, and yak, offering services that include commercial slaughter, on-farm slaughter, hand-blended sausage seasoning, and custom labeling. A West Virginia Department of Agriculture inspected facility handles the work, and a retail shop sells seasonal cuts sourced from the owners' farm and neighboring producers.

Primary customers are West Virginia and Virginia livestock producers who need state-inspected processing to sell meat within the state, as well as local households purchasing retail cuts or curated meat boxes. The 2024 commercial license expanded the firm's ability to process animals for resale, strengthening the regional food chain.

The founders frame the venture around connecting communities with locally raised meat through ethical handling, eco-conscious operations, and personalized butchery that accommodates rare or custom cuts. Their stated mission emphasizes sustainability, humane treatment, and keeping the food supply close to home.

# Restaurant Barn

**Old description:** From Commercial Kitchens to Home Kitchens, we have what you need, For Every Cook, For Every Kitchen

## About
Restaurant Barn operates from Ellsworth, Maine, as a locally owned restaurant supply house serving Downeast Maine and beyond. The company does not publish a founding year or founder names, but it positions itself as a community-focused supplier that treats each Maine town as its own hometown.

The business stocks thousands of smallwares and large equipment items across categories that include cooking equipment (griddles, fryers, ovens, ranges), commercial refrigeration and ice machines, sinks, dishwashing and bar fixtures, and tabletop supplies. It carries product lines from brands such as Turbo Air, Imperial, Town Food Service Equipment, Nemco, Winco, Cuzco, Hatco, and Tomlinson. Through its supplier network the company says it can access millions of additional SKUs, and it offers kitchen planning, financing options, and free on-site estimates.

Customers range from professional chefs and commercial kitchens to home cooks. Testimonials cite Acadia Provisions in Ellsworth and a bakery in Bar Harbor that drives to the store for boxes and other supplies. The store is open to the public and the team also travels to project sites to assess layout and workflow.

The company was started to be an integral local support for Maine food-service businesses, emphasizing neighborly service and a willingness to “bend over backwards” for customers.
