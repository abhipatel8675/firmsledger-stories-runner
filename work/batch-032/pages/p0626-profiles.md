## About
Since the 1980s, Rockland Foodservice has operated in Rockland, Maine, under the ownership of a local family. The business traces its roots to an earlier incarnation as Rockland Wholesale, which occupied a building now home to J. Edward Knight Insurance, and before that a location next door to where CarQuest Auto Parts now stands. The family also operated Shore Save, a former grocery store that once stood where a Rite Aid pharmacy is today.

The company runs a foodservice store at 195 Park Street, just outside downtown Rockland, offering a wide variety of foodservice and drink items for takeaway. It sources from numerous local and out-of-state distributors and can procure specialty items usually within a few days. The operation handles bulk and party orders, allowing customers to place advance orders a week early for weddings, parties, or boat trips, with items set aside for pickup. Curbside service is also available.

Rockland Foodservice serves local restaurants, hotels, and individual customers throughout the Midcoast region. Its customer base includes anyone needing foodservice supplies, from professional kitchens to residents planning large gatherings.

The business was founded to fill the gap between large national food distributors and conventional retail chains, aiming to provide exemplary service and competitive prices to the Maine market. The company emphasizes a willingness to accommodate each customer, whether a first-time buyer or a long-time patron.
