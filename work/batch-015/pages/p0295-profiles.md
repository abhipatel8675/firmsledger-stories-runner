## About
Founded in 1923 in Bally, Pennsylvania, Bally Ribbon Mills (BRM) has operated as a privately held manufacturer of engineered narrow fabrics for over a century. The company began producing specialized woven webbing, tapes, and specialty fabrics for critical-use applications.

The business distinguishes itself through its patented thermal-spray technique, a commitment to artisan-level detail on one-of-a-kind commissions, and a willingness to take on challenging, non-standard jobs that combine aesthetic and utilitarian requirements.
