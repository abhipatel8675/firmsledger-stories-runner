## About
Bauer Fabrication & Art Metal operates from Waterbury, Vermont, as a metal fabricating and machine shop specializing in architectural metal work, custom furniture, and original artwork. The company serves architects, designers, contractors, and homeowners throughout New England and beyond, with installations in New York City, Boston, Florida, Mississippi, California, and the Virgin Islands.

In its Waterbury facility, the team combines traditional hand-and-eye craftsmanship with a patented thermal-spray-and-wire-mesh process branded as Bauer Art Metal. This proprietary method builds freestanding objects by spraying molten metal, enabling sculptural artwork, memorials, and household items such as sinks and cookware that would otherwise require casting. The shop also provides design services and installation, coordinating with other trades when needed.

Clients include Gossens Bachman Architects, which has commissioned complex stairways and custom sinks, and Planeta Design Group of Boston, a long-term collaborator on boundary-pushing projects. Residential customers such as Howard and Linda Awand have received one-of-a-kind garden fences. The firm works across construction, architectural, residential, and art markets, delivering custom stair rails, staircases, furniture, memorials, and decorative objects.

The company distinguishes itself by welcoming challenging, non-repetitive jobs and by integrating its patented thermal-spray technique with conventional fabrication to create unique textures and forms. Its stated intention is to deliver a finish of excellent and uniform quality on every custom piece.
