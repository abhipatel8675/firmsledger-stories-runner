The technology addresses depression, anxiety, cognitive impairment, PTSD, concussions, and attention disorders.

Zarreii's motivation traces to the loss of his daughter Samira, after whom Intellig-EYES is named. The company invested 19 years and millions of dollars without government grants to develop non-invasive, objective diagnostics that generate clear reports in a single extended visit. Multiple patents have been issued with more pending, covering optics, stimuli generation, and measurement standards for autonomic nervous system assessment.
