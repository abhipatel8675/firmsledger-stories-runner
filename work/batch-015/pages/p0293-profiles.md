## About
Avon Lake Sheet Metal Company, Inc. has been family-owned and operated since 1953 in Avon Lake, Ohio, about 25 miles west of Cleveland. The business began in a one-car garage when founders George Wightman and Carl Wetzig Sr. established the shop on principles of honesty and hard work. Second-generation owners Carl Wetzig Jr. and Gary Wightman continue to lead the company today.

The company runs a 32,000-square-foot facility on a five-acre industrial site equipped with modern machinery including a Trumpf TruLaser 2030 4,000-watt fiber laser, a Trumpf TruBend 2100 press brake, a Wysong 12-foot 175-ton hydraulic press brake, a Lockformer Vulcan 2900 dual-table CNC plasma cutter, and a Fasti FP60 CNC panel former. Core capabilities span sheet metal fabrication (CNC laser, plasma, and shear cutting; CNC press brake forming and panel folding; rolling), structural fabrication (sawing, punching, drilling), certified welding (MIG, TIG, stick, flux core, pulsed MIG, spot, field), assembly of mechanical components and modules, finishing (painting, laser etching, glass beading, grinding), and 3D modeling with Inventor and AutoCAD. Field services cover equipment installation, field welding, plant maintenance, project management, and coordination drawings. The shop also provides turnkey HVAC system design, duct fabrication, and installation, plus custom machine guarding fabrication and installation.

Avon Lake Sheet Metal serves a diverse customer base across automotive, steel and foundry, chemical, plastics, construction, food processing, general manufacturing, retail, education, and health care industries. The company also works with medical groups, industrial mills, and bulk powder material handling systems.

The founders' original emphasis on honesty, hard work, and lasting value remains central. The company states its core values as quality, efficiency, and integrity, and highlights a safety-first culture recognized by multiple SMACNA Zero Injury Awards (2015, 2017, 2018, 2020, 2022). AWS-certified welders and a drug-free workplace certification further underscore its operational standards.

Zarreii’s original vision was to solve complex neurological challenges through non-invasive, objective, and automated testing. B & M Sheet Metal Shop, Inc. has been a family-owned fixture in Roanoke, Virginia since 1955. The business was founded by Wallace Leedy Martin, Edith Martin, and Marion Bartley, then led for decades by Wallace Lee Martin before passing to the current owner, Luke McVey Martin.

The shop provides custom sheet metal fabrication services including laser cutting, bending, shearing, plasma cutting, welding, and press brake forming. It produces ductwork, commercial kitchen hoods, and refurbishes stainless steel elevators, and maintains an inventory of stainless steel, carbon steel, aluminum, galvanized, galvanneal, copper, and brass sheet as well as flat bar, angle, tubing, and solid rod.

B & M serves commercial and industrial customers who need measured, fabricated, and installed sheet metal assemblies, offering turnkey service from on-site measurement through final installation and providing quotes before any work begins.

The company emphasizes a hands-on approach, handling the entire project lifecycle in-house — from taking field measurements to fabricating and installing the finished product — which allows it to control quality and scheduling for each job.
