## About
Asahi Kasei Plastics North America operates from Fowlerville, Michigan, as the North American subsidiary of Japan's Asahi Kasei Corporation, serving as the regional hub for the group's engineering plastics business.

The portfolio centers on high-performance engineering plastics: polyamide (LEONA™), polyacetal (TENAC™), modified PPE (XYRON™), polypropylene compounds, and advanced materials such as engineering particle foams, continuous glass- and carbon-fiber reinforced thermoplastics, cellulose nanofiber nanocomposites, and purging compounds (ASACLEAN®). The company also provides CAE analysis—including topology optimization and structural simulation—molding guidance, extrusion grade recommendations, and binder proposals for powder injection molding, supporting customers from product design through mass production.

Customers span AI data centers, automotive, solar energy, electrical/electronic, and medical device sectors. For AI infrastructure, grades address power connectors, intelligent PDUs, battery backup units, server fans, and cooling pumps. Automotive applications include millimeter-wave radar antennas requiring high dimensional stability and plating performance, plus in-vehicle battery peripherals. Solar products cover photovoltaic connectors and junction boxes with weather resistance. Medical-grade POM resins meet ISO 10993-5 and USP Class VI biocompatibility standards.

Asahi Kasei emphasizes customized solutions backed by resin expertise and CAE-driven design optimization, alongside carbon neutrality and circular economy initiatives. In 2026, the North American operation received DENSO's North America Business Partner Award, reflecting its collaborative approach with major automotive suppliers.
