## About
Asahi Kasei Plastics North America operates from Fowlerville, Michigan, as the North American arm of Japan’s Asahi Kasei Corporation. The subsidiary was established to bring the parent company’s portfolio of engineering plastics and technical services to the U.S. market, though a specific founding year is not publicly disclosed.

The company supplies a range of high-performance resins including polyamide (LEONA™), polyacetal (TENAC™), modified polyphenylene ether (XYRON™), polypropylene compounds, engineering particle foam, continuous fiber-reinforced thermoplastics (CFRTP), cellulose nanofiber (CNF) nanocomposites, and the ASACLEAN® purging compound. Beyond material supply, it offers CAE analysis services — featuring topology optimization and molding simulation — and provides technical support covering material selection, molding conditions, and powder injection molding (PIM) binder systems from design through mass production.

Its customer base spans automotive, where materials are used in millimeter-wave radar antennas and EV battery peripherals; AI data centers, for power connectors, cooling pumps, and server fans; medical devices, with POM grades meeting ISO 10993-5 and USP Class VI; and photovoltaic installations, supplying connector and junction-box components. The division was recognized by DENSO with a 2026 North America Business Partner Award, underscoring its role in automotive supply chains.

Asahi Kasei emphasizes customized material solutions backed by simulation-driven design, aiming to reduce weight, improve heat resistance, and meet flame-retardancy requirements. The group also highlights its commitment to carbon neutrality and circular economy initiatives across the engineering plastics business.
