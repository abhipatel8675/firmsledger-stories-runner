## About
Atlas Precision Inc traces its roots to April 1979 when Johann W. Hofschuster founded Atlas Mold, Inc. in Arden, North Carolina, to design and build injection molds. In 1988 the company added a custom injection molding division, Atlas Precision Plastics, Inc., installing its first molding machines in 1989. The two operations merged under one roof in 2006, and the business now operates as Atlas Precision, Inc.

The company provides full-service plastic injection molding with in-house tool design and construction. Its Arden facility houses 33 molding machines ranging from 80 to 360 tons, each equipped with a robot or sprue picker, and runs 24/7 across four crews. Process technicians use RJG/eDart systems for process control. The on-site tool shop handles design, engineering changes, repairs, and maintenance, while a 3D printing capability supports prototyping. Services span product design, material selection, mold flow analysis, and packaging.

Atlas Precision serves healthcare, automotive, telecommunications, industrial, and electrical markets. Its tooling division specializes in molds for electronics, electrical connectors, medical devices, packaging, and single-use healthcare products. Long-standing customers include Johnson & Johnson (Personal Care and Vistakon divisions), S.C. Johnson, Maxxim Medical, Bic Corporation, Robert Bosch Automotive, Tyco Electronics, Superior Modular Products, Trex, and Endura Products.

The company emphasizes that it has historically built some of the fastest running production tooling in the U.S., particularly for large manufacturers of single-use medical and consumer products. Its operating philosophy centers on assigning a personal account manager to each customer, empowering staff to remove obstacles quickly, and maintaining ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 13485:2016 certifications as a framework for continuous improvement.
