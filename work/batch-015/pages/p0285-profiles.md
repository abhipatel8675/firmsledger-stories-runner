# Americor Electronics Ltd

**Old description:** Manufacturing power cords, cable & wire harnesses and other electric and electronic components for the world since 1988.

## About
Americor Electronics Ltd. was founded in 1988 in Elk Grove Village, Illinois, by Tom Pross, who brought 22 years of experience in power cord, cable, and wire harness production. Pross leveraged his supplier relationships worldwide to establish the company as a privately held supplier of electrical and electronic components.

The company manufactures custom and standard power cords, cable assemblies, and wire harnesses, along with a range of electronic components including solenoids, relays, axial fans, terminal blocks, piezoelectric devices, EMI/RFI filters, transformers, and adapters. It provides prototyping, testing, documentation for approval, and post‑order inventory management and shipping. Americor also serves as the U.S. manufacturer’s representative for Germany’s Adels Contact, supplying terminal blocks, connector systems, and distribution boxes for high‑power LEDs.

Americor sells exclusively to original equipment manufacturers and distributors, serving the appliance, electronics, and medical device sectors. Longtime customers include Pulsafeeder, an Idex Corporation business, which named Americor its Supplier of the Year in 2019. The company has supported OEM product development for over three decades.

Pross started the business to offer custom‑engineered products that guarantee maximum function and cost efficiency, backed by a commitment to stay current with certifications such as ISO 9001:2015 and RoHS compliance. The firm emphasizes ongoing service, managing inventory and logistics for clients after the initial order.
