# Americor Electronics Ltd

**Old description:** Manufacturing power cords, cable & wire harnesses and other electric and electronic components for the world since 1988.

## About
Americor Electronics Ltd. was founded in 1988 by Tom Pross in Elk Grove Village, Illinois. Pross brought 22 years of experience in power cord, cable, and wire harness production to the venture, establishing a supplier network that spans the globe.

The company manufactures custom and standard power cords (including locking, medical, and international configurations), cable assemblies, and wire harnesses. It also produces a range of electronic components such as solenoids, relays, axial fans, terminal blocks, piezo electric devices, EMI/RFI filters, and transformers. Beyond fabrication, Americor provides prototyping, testing support, documentation for approval, and post-order inventory management with scheduled shipping. Standard items are sourced from overseas partners, while custom designs are built to customer specifications.

Americor serves original equipment manufacturers and distributors exclusively, with a focus on the appliance, electric, and electronic industries. Longtime customers include Pulsafeeder, an Idex Corporation business that named Americor its 2019 Supplier of the Year. The firm also acts as the U.S. manufacturer's representative for Germany's Adels Contact, supplying terminal blocks, connector systems, and high-power LED distribution boxes.

Pross founded the business to leverage his deep supplier relationships and deliver custom products that guarantee maximum function and cost efficiency. The operation maintains ISO 9001:2015 certification and RoHS compliance, and holds membership in the Wiring Harness Manufacturer's Association.
