The company designs and supplies pre-driver integrated circuits for brushless DC fan controllers, offered in both open-loop (AGV8011, AGV8012, AGV8018, AGV8019) and closed-loop (AGV8001, AGV8002, AGV8008, AGV8009) configurations. Agave’s products target data center operators and equipment manufacturers that rely on fans and blowers for thermal management. By optimizing the pressure-airflow operating point, the technology aims to reduce the electricity consumed by cooling systems while preserving required airflow. The company also serves applications in electronics cooling where motor efficiency and vibration reduction are priorities.

Agave states that its Smart-Drive approach can achieve energy savings of up to 80 percent depending on fan pressure and airflow conditions, and that it reduces motor vibration as a secondary benefit. The company describes its mission as improving electric motor efficiency and performance through intelligent control.

The shop offers insert molding, over-molding, rapid tooling, prototyping, 3D modeling, and proof-of-concept development, along with secondary services such as ultrasonic welding, clam-shell packaging, packaging design, and direct-to-distribution shipping. A staff of 14 handles production runs from low-volume prototypes to high-volume orders.

Aire Plastics serves a mix of long-standing clients — including the original six accounts from 1989 — as well as inventors, entrepreneurs, and product developers seeking manufacturing support. The company emphasizes intellectual property protection, requiring non-disclosure agreements before project discussions, and provides resources for product development stages from ideation through manufacturing.

The business was built on Terry Post's determination to preserve the Simms family legacy and his precision-manufacturing discipline. That foundation, combined with a focus on long-term service records and on-time delivery, continues to differentiate the operation under the leadership of Vice President and General Manager Sean Post.
