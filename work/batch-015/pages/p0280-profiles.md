## About
Agave Semiconductor is a Phoenix, Arizona-based semiconductor company focused on motor control integrated circuits. The company was founded to address energy efficiency in electric motors, though its exact founding year and ownership structure are not publicly disclosed. Its founder has been featured in local business media discussing the Arizona startup ecosystem.

The company designs and supplies Smart-Drive pre-driver ICs for brushless DC fans, offering both open-loop (AGV8011, AGV8012, AGV8018, AGV8019) and closed-loop (AGV8001, AGV8002, AGV8008, AGV8009) product families. These devices implement real-time efficiency optimization algorithms that adjust power delivery to maintain mechanical workload while minimizing electrical input. The AGV8009 closed-loop pre-driver, for example, provides 40 V MOS output pre-drivers capable of sourcing or sinking 100 mA, along with soft-start, current-limit, locked-rotor, and thermal protections.

Primary markets include data-center cooling, where the technology reduces electricity consumed by fans and blowers in server enclosures, as well as broader electronics cooling applications. The company's literature highlights computer server enclosures as a key environment where the pressure-airflow (PQ) curve intersection determines operating points.

Agave states that its Smart-Drive technology can achieve energy savings of up to 80 percent depending on fan pressure and airflow conditions, while also reducing motor vibration. The differentiation rests on continuous, real-time efficiency optimization that adapts to changing system impedance without sacrificing speed or workload.

## About
Aire Plastics is headquartered in La Vernia, Texas, and has been family-owned since its founding in 1989. The business traces its roots to 1982 when mold maker Ken Simms established Aire Masters of San Antonio. After Simms' sudden death in 1989, Terry Post—who owned an automotive repair business on family property in Adkins, Texas—purchased the plastics division and converted his garage into a production plant. Despite having no prior injection molding experience, Post applied his background in high-precision boiler work to deliver on-time parts to the company's original six customers.

Today Aire Plastics operates a highly automated facility with nine injection molding machines ranging from 33 to 275 tons of press capacity. The shop offers rapid tooling, prototyping, 3D modeling, and proof-of-concept development alongside production services such as insert molding and over-molding. Secondary operations include ultrasonic welding, clam-shell packaging, custom packaging design, and direct-to-distribution shipping. The company employs 14 people and handles low- to high-volume runs.

The company serves its original six long-term customers while also working with inventors and product developers seeking plastics manufacturing solutions. Its customer base spans the plastics industry and includes clients needing product design, tool creation, assembly, and packaging services.

Aire Plastics emphasizes protecting intellectual property through NDAs and building long-term service records. The business was started to preserve the plastics division after its founder's death, and it differentiates itself through on-time delivery, family ownership, and a commitment to projects of any size.
