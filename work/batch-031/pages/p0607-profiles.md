## About
Pajaro Valley Fabrication Inc. (PVFAB) operates from a 10,000-square-foot facility in Watsonville, California. The company was founded in 2006 and employs at least four full-time fabricators. Ownership structure and founder names are not publicly disclosed.

The shop provides custom metal fabrication services including MIG, stick, flux-core, and TIG welding; waterjet cutting on a Flow Mach 2 machine with a 10-by-6-foot bed; shearing up to 3/8-inch steel and 1/4-inch stainless on two Amada hydraulic shears; bending up to 1/2-inch steel on two hydraulic presses; rolling of sheet metal, tubes, and solids; machining, drilling, and lathe work; hole punching and corner cutting; design assistance using current manufacturing software; and final assembly. The company also stocks and sells raw materials with value-added cutting, cleaning, and punching.

Agriculture accounts for roughly 65 percent of the business, with the balance split between commercial and residential customers. Typical projects range from agricultural equipment components and hydraulic system parts to truck beds with power lifts, trailers, lumber racks, sand-blasting tubes, custom gates, barbecue tables, fire pits, and stainless-steel countertops.

PVFAB emphasizes certified welders with more than 20 years of experience and a focus on minimal warping and extreme precision in waterjet cutting. The company states it builds industrial-grade products regardless of end use and aims to optimize productivity and streamline processes for both one-off and large-scale production runs.
