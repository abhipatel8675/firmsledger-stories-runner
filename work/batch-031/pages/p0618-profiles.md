## About
Q&D Wire Cable Assembly Inc. operates in Hillsboro, Oregon, in the Silicon Forest region just west of Portland. The shop provides custom wire and cable assemblies, electrical and mechanical box builds, injection/compression/transfer plastic molding, and ECN rework and fulfillment services. It specializes in high-mix, low-to-medium volume production, handling wire sizes from 14 to 32 AWG, coaxial and ribbon cables, and a range of thermoplastic and thermoset materials. Processes include hand and semi-automatic crimping, lead-free soldering, epoxy potting, 100% electrical testing, and hipot testing with reports. Molding capacity runs up to 12-ounce parts on presses up to 150 tons.

Customers span aviation, industrial, consumer, medical, robotics, aerospace, military, sensor, and UAV/UAS sectors. Q&D emphasizes a quality-first culture backed by AS9100D/ISO 9001:2015 certification and IPC/WHMA-A-620 workmanship compliance. Its differentiators include lean manufacturing expertise, quick-turn flexibility, engineering and design-for-manufacturability support, and a supply chain stocked with major connector brands.
