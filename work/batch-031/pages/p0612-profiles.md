## About
Plasman operates a manufacturing facility in Lawrenceburg, Tennessee, as part of a global automotive components company headquartered in Windsor, Canada, with a European headquarters in Gothenburg, Sweden. The business traces its roots back more than 45 years to a two-person tool shop and has grown into a multinational presence across nine countries including the United States, Canada, Mexico, Sweden, Norway, Belgium, the Czech Republic, Germany, and Japan. The company produces a range of automotive exterior and structural components, including window assemblies and regulators, rack lifts, door frames, roof rail systems, bumpers and fascias, grilles, and subsystem modules. Surface finishing technologies include Omniluxe®, a physical vapor deposition process positioned as an environmentally friendly alternative to traditional chrome plating, and Brilliance, a paint-over-chrome finish.

Plasman serves automotive original equipment manufacturers globally, delivering single-source solutions from concept through completion. Its customer base extends across North America, Europe, and Asia, with the company emphasizing collaborative partnerships and the ability to manage complex, high-volume programs for major vehicle platforms.

The organization highlights its collaborative mindset and single-source model as key differentiators, aiming to provide design, tooling, molding, finishing, and assembly under one roof. The development of proprietary finishes such as Omniluxe® reflects a stated focus on meeting evolving environmental regulations and OEM demands for sustainable surface solutions.
