# OXOS Medical, Inc

**Old description:** OXOS is transforming medical imaging with innovative, portable X‑ray solutions

## About
OXOS Medical, Inc. is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia. The company was founded in June 2016 as Micro C, LLC, later renamed OXOS Medical, Inc., by Evan M. Ruff, who serves as CEO, and Gregory P. Kolovich, MD, a reconstructive hand surgeon who became frustrated with traditional imaging processes while at Harvard Medical School. That frustration inspired the development of the Micro C, the world's first portable Dynamic Digital Radiography device for orthopedic surgery.

OXOS designs and manufactures portable X-ray systems, including the original Micro C and the newer MC2, which received FDA 510(k) clearance (K252068) in 2024. The devices are built around a low-radiation profile while maintaining high image quality, and incorporate the AiLARA system, an automated technology that optimizes radiation dose according to the ALARA principle. A team of engineers, designers, and clinical advisors supports development and commercialization, with the MC2 now available in the United States.

The company targets orthopedics, pediatrics, and sports medicine, serving clinicians and medical practices nationwide. Testimonials cite use by hand and upper extremity surgeons, orthopaedic surgeons for professional sports teams (including the Jacksonville Jaguars), foot and ankle surgeons, and at off-road racing events through the non-profit Safety On Site. The portable systems are designed for point-of-care imaging in diverse clinical settings.

OXOS states its mission is to allow anyone, anywhere to access radiographic diagnostics at the point of need, expanding availability and changing how healthcare is delivered. The company emphasizes three pillars: safety through low radiation exposure, simplicity via portable and modular design, and intelligence through real-time diagnosis enabled by optimized alignment and a no-fire positioning system.
