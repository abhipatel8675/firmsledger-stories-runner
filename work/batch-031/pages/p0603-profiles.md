## About
Origin Food Group LLC is a family-owned food manufacturer headquartered in Statesville, North Carolina. The company traces its roots to South America, where it pioneered functional foods for nearly four decades before expanding its operations to the United States in 2009.

The company develops and produces a range of dairy-based functional foods in-house, including Swiss-style drinkable and spoonable yogurts fortified with vitamins, minerals, and supplements. Its capabilities span contract packing, private label manufacturing, and proprietary product formulation using premium ingredients such as HOWARU® probiotics, TruCal® calcium, Benecol for cholesterol reduction, and iron for anemia prevention.

Origin Food Group serves the food and beverage industry with a focus on dairy, yogurt, and functional food segments. Its customer base includes private label brands and contract packing clients seeking better-for-you products, and it maintains a local supply partnership with Stamey Farms, a multi-generational dairy operation in Iredell County that provides milk for production.

Founded on the belief that nutrition should not be compromised for taste or convenience, the company differentiates itself through a history of category firsts — introducing Swiss-style yogurt to its home market, fortifying every product, and designing each item to deliver measurable wellness benefits such as cholesterol lowering and improved iron status.
