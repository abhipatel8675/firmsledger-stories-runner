# Origin Food Group LLC

**Old description:** It’s a given that “good food” should taste good. But good food should also be more nutritious.

## About
Origin Food Group traces its roots to South America, where a family-owned company spent nearly four decades pioneering functional foods—introducing Swiss-style drinkable and spoonable yogurts, fortifying every product with vitamins and minerals, and using premium ingredients such as HOWARU® probiotics and TruCal® calcium. The business expanded to North America in 2009, establishing its U.S. headquarters and production facility in Statesville, North Carolina.

At the Statesville plant, the company develops and manufactures a range of better-for-you dairy products, including yogurts enriched with probiotics, antioxidant-rich superfruits, cholesterol-lowering Benecol®, and iron for anemia prevention. Operations cover contract packing, private labeling, and full-scale dairy processing, with milk sourced locally from Stamey Farms, a multi-generational dairy partner in Iredell County. All formulation and production are handled in-house.

Origin serves food and beverage brands seeking private-label dairy and functional-food solutions, as well as companies needing contract packing for nutritional products. Its customer base includes marketers of on-the-go health foods, retailers building store-brand yogurt lines, and nutrition-focused brands requiring specific functional benefits such as digestive health, bone support, or cholesterol management.

The company’s founding premise—that no one should compromise nutrition for taste or convenience—continues to drive its approach. By combining dairy expertise with targeted fortification, Origin aims to deliver measurable wellness benefits in every product, a philosophy that began with its early innovations in South America and now extends to its U.S. manufacturing.
