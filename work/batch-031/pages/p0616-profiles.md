## About
Priority Plastics, Inc. is headquartered in Grand Prairie, Texas, and operates extrusion blow-molding manufacturing facilities strategically located across the United States. The company provides plastic packaging solutions with a focus on quality, timeliness, relationships, and sustainability.

The company produces HDPE and PET containers, including PriorityPour tight-head containers, canisters, and dry-pack towel wipes, using extrusion blow molding and injection stretch blow molding (ISBM) processes. Its GreenLite program incorporates post-consumer recycled (PCR) content — up to 80% in ECO multi-layer products — and lightweighting designs. Priority Plastics offers in-plant customer service, custom and stock tooling, labeling capabilities, and multi-plant sourcing from its coast-to-coast footprint, serving customers from startups to multinational firms.

Priority Plastics serves food and beverage, nutrition and wellness, industrial, personal care, agriculture, animal health, cannabis/hemp/CBD, hardware, hazardous materials, healthcare/nutraceutical, and specialty chemicals markets.

The company states its people-centric culture and sustainability commitments drive its differentiation: facilities recycle unused resin, redesign packaging to reduce gram weight, and leverage geographic proximity to lower freight emissions. Published goals include sourcing 60% of materials from recycled or renewable sources by 2035, making 80% of products recyclable by 2028, and cutting Scope 2 and 3 emissions 30% by 2030.
