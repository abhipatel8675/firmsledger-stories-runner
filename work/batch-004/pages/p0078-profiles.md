## About
McElroy Electronics Corporation is based in Shirley, Massachusetts, and was founded in May 1957 by John C. McElroy (1926–1994), the son of wireless communication pioneer Theodore Roosevelt McElroy. After a career in the U.S. Navy and years working at his father's McElroy Manufacturing Corporation in Littleton, John started his own company to focus on custom engineering and government contracts. The business remains family-owned and is now operated by John's three sons, Jack, Daniel, and Mark.

The company manufactures and distributes component parts for electronic coils and transformers. Its product line includes lug assemblies, nylon and fiber washers, lead holders, bobbins, custom headers and boards, Super Goop masking compound, and tinned copper wire assemblies. McElroy also provides custom engineering services, taking projects from design through prototype to production, and has a long history of fulfilling government contracts for specialized electromechanical equipment.

McElroy serves the aerospace, defense technology, technical education equipment, marine, and industrial sectors. Past customers include the U.S. Navy (Hawk and Polaris missile programs), the U.S. Army, the Army Security Agency, John Hancock Mutual Life Insurance Company, and the Saudi Arabian National Guard via Vinnell Corporation. The company's components have also been used in Operation Deep Freeze at the South Pole.

McElroy emphasizes its huge inventory as a guarantee of on-time delivery and uninterrupted production. The firm's founding was driven by John McElroy's desire to apply his naval and manufacturing experience to specialized electromechanical equipment, and the company highlights its decades of experience in custom timing devices, high-speed Morse systems, and audiovisual teaching systems as part of its technical heritage.
