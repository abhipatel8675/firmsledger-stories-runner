## About
Liberty Steel & Wire operates a large integrated steel mill in Peoria, Illinois. The business traces its roots back more than 125 years and today functions as part of Liberty Steel USA, a family of companies headquartered in New York that produces fabricated wire products, reinforcing bar, and prestressed concrete strand.

The Peoria facility melts, refines, and casts steel on site, producing roughly 700,000 tons annually. A continuous billet caster feeds a rod mill equipped with a Morgan No‑Twist finishing mill and retarded cooling, while 22 drawing machines handle wire drawing to customer specifications. Finished output includes billets, rod, rebar, industrial wire, PC strand, welded wire mesh, and the Red Brand line of agricultural fencing.

Customers span construction (cast‑in‑place and precast concrete), infrastructure, industrial OEMs, agriculture, and retail consumers. The company’s welded wire reinforcement is certified to ASTM standards for precast and cast‑in‑place applications, and its Red Brand fencing is marketed as the only agricultural fence made entirely in the U.S. from start to finish.

Liberty emphasizes its domestic supply chain: every step from scrap processing through final packaging occurs at its own U.S. facilities, making its products Buy America compliant. The mill offers customized steel grades — over 20 variations within the 1008 classification alone — and holds ISO 9001 certification and ISO 17025 (A2LA) accreditation for its testing laboratory.

**Old description:** Louisiana Plastic Industries, founded in 1973 by Sidney R. Wilhite, is located in West Monroe, Louisiana. Sidney’s philosophy was straight forward – Provide Economy through Service and Quality. Today, LPI is the leading supplier of high-density and low-density polyethylene film for the multi-wall bag industry. ## About
Louisiana Plastic Industries was founded in 1973 by Sidney R. Wilhite in West Monroe, Louisiana. The family-owned business began with a straightforward philosophy: provide economy through service and quality. Today, the company operates under the leadership of Wilhite's sons, John Mark Wilhite and Ross Wilhite Jr., who continue as owners.

The manufacturer runs an 80,000-square-foot facility on four acres, equipped with 13 advanced blown film extrusion lines. These lines produce high-density, low-density, and linear low-density polyethylene films, as well as polypropylene, in widths up to 80 inches. The operation includes custom film blending, pre-slitting, perforating, and tubing, delivering single-wound and two-ply sheeting tailored for multi-wall bag production and other industrial packaging needs.

Primary customers serve the multi-wall bag industry, with films also used in roofing, food service, and specialty applications. The company's strategic location alongside the CPKC Railroad, supported by eight on-site silos and a dedicated truck fleet, enables direct delivery across the United States.

Louisiana Plastic Industries emphasizes rigorous quality controls, including FDA-compliant virgin resins, gluability treatment, and continuous monitoring of gauge, width, moisture vapor transmission, and oxygen transmission rates. Every order is inspected during production, and the company guarantees on-time delivery backed by its own logistics network.
