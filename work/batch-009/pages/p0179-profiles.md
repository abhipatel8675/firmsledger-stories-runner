## About
Grace Technologies is headquartered in Davenport, Iowa. The company traces its roots to 1993 when founder Phil Allen, while working on a friction welder retrofit at John Deere, identified a hazardous electrical access issue and launched Grace Engineering to develop the GracePort panel interface connector. The business evolved into Grace Engineered Products and was renamed Grace Technologies in 2020 to reflect its expanded portfolio. Leadership remains in the family: Phil Allen serves as Chairman of the Board, and his son Drew Allen is President and CEO.

Grace manufactures three core product lines: GracePort panel interface connectors that provide thru-door programming access to PLCs and control panels; GracePESDs, permanent electrical safety devices that combine presence-of-voltage LED indication with absence-of-voltage test points for lockout/tagout verification; and the GraceSense predictive maintenance system, IIoT smart sensors that continuously monitor vibration and temperature on switchgears, transformers, and rotating equipment. The company maintains roughly 16,000 active SKUs and ships orders worldwide within two to three business days through a distribution network spanning more than 60 countries.

Customers include industrial maintenance teams, electrical contractors, and original equipment manufacturers across sectors where incident energy exposure is a critical risk — such as manufacturing, power generation, and heavy industry. The devices are designed for low- to medium-voltage applications and are used to make maintenance safer, smarter, and more productive.

Grace emphasizes four differentiators: thought leadership in electrical safety, mass customization enabled by its large SKU count, fast global delivery, and a founding principle of being easy to do business with. Its stated mission is to pursue practical ways of keeping live voltage away from workers before, during, and after maintenance tasks, while empowering maintenance programs with IIoT sensor data.
