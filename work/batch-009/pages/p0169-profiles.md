# Electronic Specialties of Ct

**Old description:** Exceptional Manufacturer of Printed Circuit Board Assembly Solutions

## About
Electronic Specialties of Connecticut, Inc. (ESC) operates from a 14,500-square-foot, climate-controlled facility in Hamden, Connecticut. The company was established in 1981 by Rich Dumont. In 2012, Duc Nguyen, who had purchased the business with a partner, became the sole owner and has directed operations since.

ESC specializes in printed circuit board assembly, handling through-hole, surface-mount, and mixed-technology builds from prototype through full production. Its high-speed Juki pick-and-place lines can place up to 60,000 components per hour, supported by a Speedline OmniMax ten-zone reflow oven, a full aqueous cleaning line, and wave soldering for both RoHS and non-RoHS runs. The shop also provides automated optical inspection (Mirtec MV-3 and MVHTL systems), manual X-ray inspection (QUADRA3 PRO), functional testing, value-added unit assembly (cable harnesses, sheet-metal and plastic integration), box-build, design and layout services, and materials management for turnkey or consigned programs.

The company serves the broader electronics industry, working with customers who need prototype quantities as well as volume production runs. It accepts both full turnkey and consigned-material arrangements and uses an MRP system to coordinate component procurement from multiple vendors.

ESC states that its mission is to exceed customer expectations for quality, service, technology, and cost while maintaining a safe, healthy workplace. It emphasizes the use of current automation and IPC-certified processes (IPC-A-610, J-STD-001) to deliver reliable assemblies and offers design input aimed at maximizing production efficiency and reducing cost.

## About
ElringKlinger Manufacturing Indiana Inc. operates in Fort Wayne, Indiana, as part of the global ElringKlinger Group, a German-headquartered automotive supplier listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange (ISIN DE0007856023). The Indiana entity serves as a manufacturing arm for the group's portfolio of sealing, shielding, lightweight, and electrification technologies.

The facility produces components for all vehicle drive types—combustion, hybrid, and fully electric. Core product lines include cylinder-head and specialty gaskets, thermal and acoustic shielding, formed metal parts, and assembled modules such as lithium-ion battery systems and fuel-cell stacks. The group also maintains a dedicated toolmaking technology center in Lenningen, Germany, supporting global production. Scale specifics for the Indiana site—square footage, headcount, annual volume—are not disclosed in the source material.

Primary customers are automotive OEMs and tier-one suppliers across passenger-car and commercial-vehicle segments. The company also supplies non-automotive sectors, leveraging its sealing, shielding, and lightweighting expertise for industrial applications. Concrete examples from the group's portfolio include electric drive units, underbody systems, chassis and brake components, and aftermarket repair kits sold under the "Elring – das Original" brand.

ElringKlinger positions itself as an independent, globally networked system partner that combines technological depth with a sustainability agenda aimed at decarbonizing mobility. The group's SHAPE30 strategy emphasizes CO2 reduction, energy efficiency, and the development of hydrogen and battery-electric solutions as key differentiators.
