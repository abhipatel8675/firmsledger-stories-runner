## About
Electronic Specialties of Connecticut, Inc. (ESOCI) operates from a 14,500-square-foot climate-controlled facility in Hamden, Connecticut. The company was established in 1981 by Rich Dumont. In 2012, Duc Nguyen became the sole owner after purchasing the business with a partner.

ESOCI provides printed circuit board assembly services covering through-hole, surface-mount, and mixed-technology processes. The shop handles prototype builds—either by hand or on a dedicated prototype line—through full production runs, with high-speed Juki placement lines capable of 60,000 components per hour. It offers both turnkey and consigned-material programs, supported by an MRP system, and adds value through box-build unit assembly, cable and harness integration, and design services including circuit layout. The facility also houses aqueous cleaning, wave soldering, and automated optical and X-ray inspection equipment.

The company serves original equipment manufacturers and electronics developers across sectors that require PCB assembly, though specific industry verticals are not detailed in its public materials. Customers range from prototype-stage innovators to established firms needing full-scale production.

ESOCI states its mission is to exceed customer expectations for quality, service, technology, and cost while maintaining a safe, growth-oriented workplace. Its vision emphasizes satisfying every customer need with the highest quality circuit board assembly service.
