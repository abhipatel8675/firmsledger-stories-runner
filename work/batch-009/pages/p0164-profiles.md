Today the company extrudes, injection molds, and precision machines a range of ultra high-performance polymers—including Torlon PAI, PEEK, Ryton R-4 PPS, AvaSpire PAEK, and Ultem PEI—into stock shapes, molded parts, and machined components. Drake serves aerospace and defense programs (notably the Javelin missile), OEMs, government agencies, universities, distributors, and fabricators worldwide. Sales offices in Germany, France, and Taiwan support global customers. The company’s stock shapes are supplied to machine shops globally, while its molded and machined parts go into applications such as electrical components, fluid handling, transportation, and oil and gas equipment.

The business was built on producing what larger suppliers could not or would not: niche grades in efficient sizes that minimize machining waste. Quance’s vision combined technical intuition with a commitment to long-term supply assurance, exemplified by the development of Drake 4200 PAI after Solvay discontinued Torlon 4200 resin.
