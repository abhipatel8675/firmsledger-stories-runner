# Bluffton Woodworking Company

**Old description:** Founded in 1989 by Stephen Z Bischoff, one of the Lowcountry’s original millwork craftsmen.

## About
Bluffton Woodworking Company operates from Bluffton, South Carolina, where it was founded in 1989 by Stephen Z. Bischoff, one of the Lowcountry's original millwork craftsmen. What began as a small local shop has grown into a multi-generational operation now led by Bischoff's sons and a team of craftsmen who continue the same hands-on approach under one roof.

The company produces high-end custom woodworking and architectural millwork across five core service areas: custom millwork (including trim, molding in stock and custom profiles, paneling, coffered ceilings, and mantels), architectural woodworking (staircases, beams, doors, windows, shutters, and railings), cabinetry and built-ins (kitchens, bathroom vanities, libraries, wine cellars), restoration and preservation (historic replication, profile matching, door and window restoration, refinishing), and specialty projects (furniture, outdoor structures, marine woodwork, feature pieces, and installations). All work is executed from their Bluffton facility by a skilled team combining decades of shared experience.

Bluffton Woodworking serves homeowners, architects, builders, and artisans throughout the region. Their projects span residential construction, architectural millwork, historic preservation, and marine woodworking, with concrete examples ranging from custom kitchen cabinetry and coffered ceilings to historic door replication and marine-grade wood structures.

The firm's stated distinction rests on handcrafted quality and longevity — summarized in the phrase "Built by hand. Built to last." The founder's intent was to pass down not just a business but a way of working, emphasizing expert restoration that revives historic details and one-of-a-kind commissions that bring unique designs to life in wood.

# Brown's Auto Salvage

**Old description:** Pardon our dust! We're working on something amazing — check back soon!

## About
Brown's Auto Salvage was founded in 1976 by Mark and Joan Brown in Bomoseen, Vermont, a rural community in the state's central green mountains. The business began with a mission to supply high-quality OEM used auto parts to New England and the broader U.S. market, leveraging Vermont's self-sufficient, environmentally conscious culture.

The company operates as a full-service auto salvage and recycling facility, acquiring late-model, low-mileage vehicles, draining fluids, and dismantling them in an indoor facility. All parts are cleaned using a water-based bio-remediation system, inspected, and tested—mechanical components for functionality, body panels for damage—before being inventoried. Brown's maintains thousands of late-model parts in stock across two locations and, through the PRP Northeast network, shares combined inventory with 15 other yards within a 6.5-hour radius, enabling immediate availability for items such as Audi engines, Honda transmissions, and Jeep wheels.

Customers include independent repair shops, collision centers, fleet operators, and individual vehicle owners across the United States. The company ships nationwide and emphasizes accessibility via phone, email, and instant messaging. Its inventory spans a wide range of makes and models, with recent arrivals including 2021 Ford F-250 pickups, 2015 Jeep Grand Cherokees, and 2024 Chevrolet Trax models.

Brown's differentiates itself through its ARA Gold Seal certification, which requires strict quality, service, and environmental standards, and its receipt of the Vermont Governor's Award for Environmental Excellence in Pollution Prevention. The company highlights a 100% money-back guarantee, a 90-day warranty on all parts, and a commitment to green practices such as reusing fluids for heating, recycling plastics and rubber, and filtering wash water through a live bacteria culture.
