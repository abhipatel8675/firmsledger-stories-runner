# Bluffton Woodworking Company

**Old description:** Founded in 1989 by Stephen Z Bischoff, one of the Lowcountry’s original millwork craftsmen.

## About
Bluffton Woodworking Company operates from Bluffton, South Carolina, where it was founded in 1989 by Stephen Z. Bischoff, one of the Lowcountry's original millwork craftsmen. What began as a small local shop has grown into a multi-generational operation now led by Bischoff's sons and a team of craftsmen who continue the same hands-on approach under one roof.

The company produces high-end custom woodworking and architectural millwork across five core service areas: custom millwork (including trim, molding in stock and custom profiles, paneling, coffered ceilings, and mantels), architectural woodworking (staircases, beams, doors, windows, shutters, and railings), cabinetry and built-ins (kitchens, bathroom vanities, libraries, wine cellars), restoration and preservation (historic replication, profile matching, door and window restoration, refinishing), and specialty projects (furniture, outdoor structures, marine woodwork, feature pieces, and installations). All work is executed from their Bluffton facility by a skilled team combining decades of shared experience.

Bluffton Woodworking serves homeowners, architects, builders, and artisans throughout the region. Their projects span residential construction, architectural millwork, historic preservation, and marine woodworking, with concrete examples ranging from custom kitchen cabinetry and coffered ceilings to historic door replication and marine-grade wood structures.

The firm's stated distinction rests on handcrafted quality and longevity — summarized in the phrase "Built by hand. Built to last." The founder's intent was to pass down not just a business but a way of working, emphasizing expert restoration that revives historic details and one-of-a-kind commissions that bring unique designs to life in wood.

# Bright Medical Technology, Inc.

## About
Bright Medical Technology, Inc. operates from Bensalem, Pennsylvania. The company is family-owned and has served patients and providers across the Philadelphia region for three generations; no founding year is disclosed.

The firm supplies durable medical equipment and pharmacy solutions, offering a comprehensive inventory that includes nebulizers, ambulatory aids, blood pressure monitors, electrotherapy devices, bathroom safety equipment, bracing, incontinence supplies, diabetic products, compression wear, enteral nutrition, pediatric and women's health items, wound care, and urological supplies. It also runs a full-service pharmacy providing prescription and over-the-counter medications. Services include insurance coordination (accepting automobile and workers’ compensation plans), same-day or next-day delivery with equipment tutorials, 24/7 emergency answering for equipment-related issues, and a showroom open weekdays 9 a.m.–5 p.m. The company collaborates with hundreds of healthcare providers across Pennsylvania.

Customers include individual patients, physicians, and other healthcare providers. The company works with a wide range of insurance plans and partners with platforms such as Parachute Health and Leading Reach to streamline ordering.

Bright Medical emphasizes its family-owned heritage and a mission to treat every patient like family, delivering timely, compassionate care. It differentiates itself through dedicated account managers for each patient, round-the-clock emergency support, and a combined equipment-and-pharmacy offering under one roof.
