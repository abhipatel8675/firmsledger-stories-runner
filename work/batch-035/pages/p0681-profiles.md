# Agiliti Manufacturing

**Old description:** We provide industry leading medical and surgical technologies wrapped in a full range of services that help keep necessary equipment — and the clinicians who need it — patient ready.

## About
Agiliti Manufacturing, doing business as Agiliti Health, Inc., is headquartered in Hays, Kansas. Under the leadership of CEO Tom Leonard, the company delivers medical and surgical technologies paired with comprehensive equipment management services.

The company manufactures a portfolio of hospital beds, therapeutic surfaces, and mobility equipment, including the Capillaire™ alternating pressure overlay, the Essentia™ multi-acuity bed frame, and the Adapt Air Pro™ pressure injury treatment system. Beyond manufacturing, Agiliti provides medical equipment rental, onsite equipment management, clinical engineering, surgical equipment services, and logistics support for government and original equipment manufacturers. The operation supports more than 10,000 U.S. acute care facilities through a network of local service centers and mobile service labs.

Agiliti serves acute care hospitals, surgical centers, government agencies, and medical device manufacturers. Its customer base includes biomedical engineering departments and clinicians who rely on the company for movable medical equipment, biomedical and imaging equipment repair, and onsite equipment management.

Agiliti emphasizes a “Nationwide Support. Local Responsiveness.” model, aiming to keep equipment and clinicians patient ready. A published case study notes that the company helps bridge communication gaps between departments, improving staff productivity and enabling nurses to spend more time at the bedside while reducing hospital-acquired conditions.

# Al's Automotive Supply

**Old description:** We take pride in distributing quality parts that exceed original equipment standards.

## About
Founded in 1954, Al's Automotive Supply Inc. is headquartered in Lenexa, Kansas. Over the decades, the company has grown to 16 locations across the St. Louis metropolitan area, establishing a strong regional footprint.

The company distributes automotive replacement parts that meet or exceed original equipment standards. Its operations include an online ordering platform for registered accounts and a nationwide warranty program covering all products sold. The 16-branch network supports a broad inventory and same-day availability for many items.

Al's serves the automotive aftermarket, supplying independent repair shops, dealership service departments, and fleet maintenance operations throughout the St. Louis region and nationally via its e-commerce channel. The company focuses exclusively on the automotive sector.

The business emphasizes parts quality that surpasses OE specifications and backs every item with a nationwide warranty, positioning itself as a reliable source for professional technicians and commercial buyers seeking consistent availability and support.
