## About
AudioScience Inc. is based in New Castle, Delaware. The company designs and manufactures advanced DSP-based digital audio peripherals for OEM, broadcast, installed sound, and entertainment markets.

Its product line includes network audio interfaces such as the ASI5752 Dante sound card and the ASI2816 streaming tuner, along with ASIControl software for device configuration. The devices support PCI Express, MPEG audio compression, Livewire, Dante, and AVnu AVB networking standards, and run on both Windows and Linux platforms. A 2022 catalog lists additional models for various channel counts and form factors.

Customers span original equipment manufacturers, broadcast facilities, installed-sound contractors, and entertainment venues that require interoperable, standards-based audio networking. The company emphasizes compatibility with third-party AVB equipment and provides drivers for major operating systems. Products are marketed directly to system integrators and end users in professional audio installations. AudioScience's focus on DSP-based design allows low-latency, multi-channel audio transport over standard IT networks.
