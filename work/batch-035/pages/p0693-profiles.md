## About
Bailey Metal Fabricators, Inc. has operated from Mitchell, South Dakota since its founding in 1960. The family‑run business began as an ornamental metal shop and has grown into a full‑service fabrication house serving customers across the United States.

The company provides custom metal fabrication and ornamental ironwork, producing structural steel components, metal stairs, gates, railings, and stainless‑steel countertops. Its shop runs a CNC plasma cutting system, a 300‑ton Cincinnati CNC press brake, a Hyd‑Mech CNC band saw with material bundling, and a Marvel CNC vertical band saw capable of 45‑degree miter cuts. The operation handles projects from single pieces to larger production runs, though specific volume figures are not published.

Bailey Metal Fabricators works with commercial, industrial, and residential clients nationwide. Typical projects include architectural metalwork for buildings, custom stair systems for contractors, and countertop fabrication for design firms.

The firm emphasizes a low incident rate and ongoing employee training as markers of its safety culture. Its longevity and family ownership are cited as reasons clients turn to the shop for difficult or non‑standard fabrication challenges.

# Bakery Equipment & Controls, Inc.

**Old description:** Let us help set you up for production growth and jump all the inefficiencies that come with machinery.

## About
Bakery Equipment & Controls, Inc. (BEC) is based in Billings, Montana. The company was founded by Mike Ninichuck, who brought over 40 years of bakery industry experience after roles at Consolidated Biscuit and Ralcorp where he helped build and consolidate facilities. Ninichuck owns and operates the business today.

BEC designs and builds custom bakery equipment, specializing in complete line installations for cookie, cracker, snack, and pet treat production. The shop provides equipment removal, installation, relocation, refurbishment, repair, inspection, and tear-down services, along with process consulting and efficiency assessments. The company reports a staff of 30-plus, more than 200 clients served, and over 2,000 pieces of equipment supplied from a large used-equipment inventory.

Customers range from small bakeries expanding operations to large-scale producers such as Ralcorp and Consolidated Biscuit. BEC works across the United States and internationally, handling projects of any size in the cookie, cracker, snack, and pet treat sectors.

The firm emphasizes integrity and problem-solving on the spot, drawing on a combined 50 years of hands-on equipment experience. It states that its bids come in well below competitors while delivering higher-quality results, and it offers complete tear-down services to lower the cost of new equipment purchases.
