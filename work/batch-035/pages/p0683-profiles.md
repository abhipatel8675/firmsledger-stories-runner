# All-New Stamping Company

**Old description:** None

## About
All-New Stamping Company operates from a 40,000-square-foot facility in El Monte, California. Founded in 1962, the company has spent over six decades building a reputation for quality metal stamping, evolving through industry downturns and technological shifts while remaining independently owned.

The shop specializes in custom precision metal stamping and progressive die stamping, complemented by laser cutting, sheet metal fabrication, and tooling services. Its process portfolio includes blanking, coining, bending, drawing, forming, piercing, turret fabrication, and assembly. Equipment ranges from stamping presses up to 150 tons and automated turret punch presses with 36-by-48-inch beds to computerized press brakes and a fiber laser cutting system. A staff of approximately 150 personnel runs a 24-hour production schedule capable of delivering quantities from single prototypes to over one million parts.

Customers span aerospace, medical, automotive, electronics, energy, marine, defense, nuclear, telecommunications, and recreation sectors. Aerospace work involves exotic alloys such as titanium, Inconel, Hastelloy, and Waspaloy. Medical device clients receive prototyping, material selection guidance, and design-for-manufacturing support. The company also serves automotive, electronics, and energy markets with high-volume stamping and value-added services.

All-New Stamping emphasizes what it calls "Power Satisfaction" — a blend of personalized attention, rapid prototyping flexibility, and die maintenance that can modify existing tooling. The firm highlights its problem-solving approach, on-time delivery track record, and integration of computer-enhanced tracking across operations.

The company designs and manufactures mixed-signal integrated circuits, including Hall-effect current sensors, brushless DC motor controllers, and automotive-grade power management ICs (PMICs) that meet ASIL-D safety standards. Its product portfolio emphasizes ultra-low primary conductor resistance (50 µΩ) for high efficiency and thermal reliability. Scale metrics such as facility size, headcount, or annual volume are not specified in the available information.
