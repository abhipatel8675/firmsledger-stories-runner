## About
Bluefin Energy Solutions Inc (Bluefin ESI) operates from Providence, Rhode Island, as a manufacturer's representative for engineered power equipment. The firm is owned by a team of licensed professional engineers and sales engineers who collectively bring over 100 years of experience in the electric power delivery industry.

The company represents manufacturers of high- and medium-voltage substation equipment, transformers, switchgear, testing equipment, substation automation, and asset monitoring technology. It also provides engineered packaged solutions and a dedicated customer service team that handles quotes, project schedules, and technical support. Additional services include power systems studies (short circuit, coordination, arc flash, harmonic analysis), retrofit and remanufacture of existing switchgear, start-up and testing of equipment packages, seismic certification, and NYC Advisory Board submissions.

Bluefin ESI serves utilities, data centers, and industrial customers across the Northeast, focusing on the electric power delivery market. Its product portfolio spans high-voltage distribution (steel structures, circuit breakers, control houses), medium-voltage distribution (metal-enclosed switchgear, pad-mounted switches, busway), low-voltage distribution (drawout switchgear, busway, network protectors), and transformers ranging from 5 MVA to 650 MVA.

The ownership team emphasizes integrity, ethics, and long-term relationships as the foundation of its business. The company positions itself as a partner that leverages rapid technological advancements to address challenges such as an aging grid and evolving supply chains.
