# Ag Technologies Inc

**Old description:** Send your excess energy back to your utility for a credit on your account when you need it – ask us about net metering today!

## About
Ag Technologies Inc. is based in Rochester, Indiana. The company traces its roots to 1984 when the Straeter family opened a New Holland farm equipment dealership in Rochester. The solar business began in 2012 with a prototype tilt-adjustable ground mount at the local USDA Service Center. The company remains family-owned.

The company designs, installs, and services the SolarCAM® system, a patented steel ground mount that can be re-angled seasonally by a single person. The frames are engineered by Ag Technologies and fabricated by Craig Welding & Manufacturing with engineer-certified wind loading. The company handles site assessment, energy modeling, permitting, interconnection, and ongoing monitoring. As of 2026, they have installed over 1,000 systems across Indiana, Illinois, Michigan, and Ohio, with a largest single project of 2 MW.

They serve farms and agribusinesses (grain systems, livestock barns, irrigation), commercial and industrial clients (dealerships, manufacturers, funeral homes, churches, schools), and residential properties with acreage. Notable installations include the Curtis Creek Dairy (2024 AgriSolar Project of the Year), two 1 MW arrays for Beck’s Hybrids seed company in Atlanta, Indiana, and a system for Mentone Elementary School.

The company emphasizes its farm-equipment heritage: the SolarCAM stand is engineered like a piece of machinery, backed by a parts counter, service trucks, and a 30-year customer view. The seasonal tilt adjustment yields about 6% more annual energy than fixed arrays, and the cam-lock jack allows one-person adjustment in under a minute.
