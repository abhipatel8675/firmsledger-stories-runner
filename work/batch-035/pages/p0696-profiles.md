**Old description:** Spend more time growing your business and less time finding the right packaging.

## About
Located in Brooklyn, New York, Billie-Ann Plastics Packaging Corp. has operated for over 50 years as a family-owned business now in its third generation. The company was founded on a personalized approach to packaging, focusing on helping customers get products onto store shelves efficiently.

The company designs and develops custom plastic containers, offering a range of printing services including hot stamping, silk screening, four-color process lithographic, and four-color process flexographic printing. Customers can specify material, size, rigidity, and add-ons such as inserts. A stock packaging inventory is also available for online purchase. The Brooklyn facility enables shorter domestic supply chains.

Billie-Ann serves food and consumer packaged goods companies, with documented customers including Sweet Tooth Snacks and Melville Candy Corporation. The company emphasizes quick turnaround, aiming to provide samples and initiate orders within a few days, and coordinates direct delivery to eliminate transportation logistics for clients.

The company cites its domestic production, family-owned continuity, and responsive service as differentiators. Leadership highlights a commitment to reliability and on-time performance, with testimonials noting professional customer service and product quality.

## About
Birmingham Piston Warehouse, operating as Internal Engine Parts Group, Inc., is based in Birmingham, Alabama. The company runs an online storefront at EnginePartsCenter.com, offering a wide range of internal engine components.

The business distributes engine rebuild kits, rotating assemblies, timing components, pistons, rings, bearings, gaskets, valvetrain parts, and related hardware for automotive, marine, diesel, and agricultural applications. Its catalog spans domestic and import platforms, including Chevrolet, Ford, Dodge, Cummins, Power Stroke, Honda, Acura, Nissan, Toyota, and various tractor and marine engines. The company stocks parts from brands such as ACL, ARP, Clevite, Cloyes, Comp Cams, Dana, Dorman, Edelbrock, Eagle Specialty Products, Fel-Pro, Hastings, Mahle, Melling, Sealed Power, and Total Seal, among others.

Customers include engine rebuilders, performance shops, marine mechanics, diesel specialists, and agricultural equipment operators. The site organizes parts by engine family and application, serving both OEM replacement and performance upgrade markets.

The company emphasizes direct-to-customer pricing with a toll-free support line for ordering assistance and provides technical resources such as a video gallery.
