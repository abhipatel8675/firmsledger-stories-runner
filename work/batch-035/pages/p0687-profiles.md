# Arbor Medical Innovations

**Old description:** We provide a hassle-free way for clinicians to collect objective real-world measures of patient activity and sleep.

## About
Based in Saline, Michigan, Arbor Medical Innovations develops wearable medical devices and monitoring services for clinical use. The company focuses on providing clinicians with objective, real-world data on patient activity and sleep through a hassle-free, integrated platform.

Its flagship product, the FDA-cleared VERABAND™, is a low-profile, waterproof, elasticized wristband designed for long-term wear. The device automatically generates a standardized one-page report that summarizes patient function, enabling continuous monitoring without burdening the patient or clinical staff. The company also offers a burden-free clinical activity monitoring service that integrates the wearable with reporting and clinical workflow support.

The technology serves clinicians managing chronic diseases, rehabilitation programs, and personalized treatment plans. Target users include physicians, physical therapists, and healthcare organizations seeking objective measures of patient mobility and sleep patterns to inform care decisions.

Arbor Medical Innovations was founded to address the gap between episodic clinical assessments and continuous real-world patient data, aiming to improve engagement and decision-making in chronic care management.
