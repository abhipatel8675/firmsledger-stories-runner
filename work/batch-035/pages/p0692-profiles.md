# BK Plastics Industry, Inc.

**Old description:** BK Plastics specializes in the design, engineering and manufacture of vacuum formed plastic parts and assemblies.

## About
BK Plastics Industry, Inc. operates from Odessa, Florida, where it specializes in the design, engineering, and manufacture of vacuum-formed plastic parts and assemblies.

The company provides heavy-gauge thermoforming, including vacuum and pressure forming, with machines that accommodate sheets up to 54 by 108 inches and material thicknesses from 0.028 to 0.500 inch. It handles deep-draw parts exceeding 28 inches in depth. In-house tooling is built from wood, Ren board, epoxy, or aluminum using SolidWorks CAD/CAM and CNC equipment, and all tooling is maintained on-site. Seven CNC machining centers (up to 50 by 101 inches) perform trimming, routing, and secondary operations. Value-added finishing includes painting, EMI/RFI shielding, ultrasonic welding, die cutting, punching, riveting, assembly, and product fulfillment.

BK Plastics serves original equipment manufacturers in medical, electronics, point-of-purchase display, automotive, marine, transportation, packaging, recreational vehicle, and general industrial markets, delivering both prototypes and production runs.

The company emphasizes its in-house tooling capability, experienced machinists with over 22 years of hands-on experience, and a full-service approach from concept through finished product to reduce lead times and ensure repeatability.
