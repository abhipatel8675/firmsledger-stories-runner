# Automated Assembly

**Old description:** Our Pick-and-Place machines produce high-quality circuit boards.

## About
Automated Assembly LLC operates from Lehi, Utah, roughly halfway between Salt Lake City and Provo. The company was founded in 2007 to address a local gap for a printed circuit board assembly house that specialized in smaller projects. Ownership details and founder names are not publicly disclosed.

The shop provides PCB assembly services using two pick-and-place machines—one dedicated to smaller runs and the other to larger jobs—enabling runs from a single board up to 500 boards per batch. Core processes include solder paste stencil design, thermal profiling with thermocouple-instrumented boards, ESD-safe handling, a modified circuit board washer running custom controller software, and 100% inspection of every assembled board. Facility size and headcount are not published.

Automated Assembly serves customers needing prototype and low-volume PCB assembly, positioning itself as a contract manufacturer for electronics projects that might be overlooked by larger houses. The company does not list specific industry verticals or named clients in its public materials.

The business was started to focus on smaller production runs that larger facilities often treat as filler. Its stated differentiators include a first-come, first-served scheduling policy that prevents small jobs from being bumped, dedicated machines to keep small and large runs independent, a no-excess-materials-charge approach for turn-key builds, price matching against competitor quotes, and local UPS Ground shipping that typically delivers finished boards the next business day.

## Known for

- Two dedicated pick-and-place machines separate small and large production runs
- No excess materials charges on most turn-key builds
- Thermal profiling uses thermocouple-instrumented boards to optimize reflow profiles

# Automatic Coax & Cable

**Old description:** We take your complex designs and build to your specifications using New Product Introduction methodology for mil-spec and commercial applications.

## About
Automatic Coax & Cable, operating as ACC, is a contract manufacturer based in Sanford, Florida. The company has been building custom solutions for over 35 years, serving military and commercial markets with a focus on complex cable and electro-mechanical assemblies.

The shop produces cable assemblies, wiring harnesses, electro-mechanical assemblies, control modules, and custom assemblies, complemented by in-house injection molding and overmolding capabilities. Engineering services span design for manufacturing, overmold tool design, prototyping, design support, documentation, product testing, and qualification. Professional services include lean collaboration, depot repair, fulfillment, supplier management, product life-cycle management, global sourcing, and lead-time expedites, delivering a turn-key approach from design through sustainment.

Primary customers operate in military and aerospace sectors, with the company emphasizing mission-critical solutions built to mil-spec requirements. Commercial applications are also supported through the same New Product Introduction methodology.

ACC differentiates itself through its New Product Introduction methodology, which structures the transition from design to production for both mil-spec and commercial programs, and by bundling engineering, manufacturing, and professional services under one roof to reduce lead times and supply-chain complexity.
