## About
Based in Riverton, Utah, Ascent Diesel (formerly ASC Warehouse) operates under the Davis Distributing name. The company maintains two physical locations: a downtown Salt Lake City facility on South Major Street and a Bluffdale site on Concord Park Drive. The Salt Lake City location operates Monday through Friday 8:30am to 5:30pm, while the Bluffdale location runs Monday through Thursday 7:30am to 4:30pm and Friday 7am to 4pm.

The business focuses on supplying engine components for trucks and vans. It offers a dealer program that allows other businesses to become authorized resellers. Core services include customer support, order shipping, and a returns process. The company does not manufacture parts but distributes them through its dealer network and direct sales.

Primary customers are dealers and repair shops in the truck and van aftermarket. The product lineup centers on engine components, though specific part numbers or brands are not detailed in public materials.

## About
Atlantic Aluminum Products, Inc. was founded in 1996 by Dan Schlabach in Greenwood, Delaware. The company began as a garage-based startup after Schlabach, already a successful business owner in Florida, saw an opportunity to bring his vision for better railing and screen systems to the Eastern Shore. Harold Sylvester serves as co-owner and vice president.

The company manufactures aluminum railing, screen systems, prefabricated balconies, architectural railings, vertical 4-track systems, and fencing. Operating from a 100,000-square-foot facility consolidated in 2018, Atlantic Aluminum Products handles custom fabrication, CAD design, and extrusion using 6005-T5 and 6063-T5 alloys. All finishes meet AAMA standards, and the company emphasizes green manufacturing technologies that align with LEED specifications.

Atlantic Aluminum Products serves the construction, architectural, residential, and commercial building markets along the East Coast. Its projects range from single-family homes to community developments, including work for Habitat for Humanity in Seaford, Delaware, and the Operation Finally Home program in Milton, Delaware. The company also supplied materials for an Extreme Makeover: Home Edition episode in Wilmington in 2008.

Schlabach started the business to address a need for higher-quality railing and screen products while creating a better workplace. The company cites its integrity, innovation, and commitment to quality as differentiators, offering a limited lifetime warranty on its maintenance-free finishes and positioning itself as a single source for orders of any size.
