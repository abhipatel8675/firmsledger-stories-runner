# Bend Biomedical

## About
Bend Biomedical is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit global health pharmaceutical company based in Prineville, Oregon. Founded in 2019, the organization operates with a mission to deliver high-quality, low-cost medicines to prevent and cure serious diseases in neglected populations worldwide.

The company pursues three complementary aims: identifying and addressing shortages of essential global-health pharmaceuticals, developing and providing affordable standardized field medical kits for emergency and primary care, and building health informatics systems that track the availability, location, demand, and treatment gaps of essential medicines and supplies. Bend Biomedical states it can provide solutions through manufacturing, packaging, procurement, or distribution of medicines such as albendazole, azithromycin, ivermectin, praziquantel, benzathine penicillin, and essential vitamins and rehydration salts.

Its work serves governments, non-governmental organizations, pharmaceutical manufacturers, and frontline health workers in resource-limited settings. Target diseases include yaws, vitamin A deficiency, under-five mortality, neural tube defects, congenital syphilis, Chagas disease, onchocerciasis, lymphatic filariasis, and soil-transmitted helminths, among others.

Bend Biomedical differentiates itself by focusing on preventable or curable diseases where effective treatments exist but access remains inadequate, and where the organization can close meaningful gaps through direct manufacturing, kitting, procurement, or data-driven supply-chain coordination.

## About
Bend Steel Supply & Fabrication operates from Bend, Oregon, serving contractors, builders, and fabricators across Central Oregon. The company is part of the Empire Family, a group that includes Empire Truck Works and Empire Stone, though its founding year and ownership structure are not publicly disclosed.

The business combines a full-service steel yard with a custom fabrication shop. It stocks structural shapes such as I-beams, wide flange, HSS tube, pipe, plate, sheet, angle, channel, flat bar, and rebar, and offers cutting to size, plasma and torch cutting, and MIG, TIG, and stick welding. Orders can be picked up at the yard or delivered to job sites, and contractor accounts provide streamlined billing and priority service.

Customers range from general contractors and metal fabricators to specialized projects in signage and hospitality. Recent work includes structural fabrication for the Westgate Residence, custom signage and fire pits for On Tap Signs, monument signs for Westgate, and branding elements for Bevel Brewing.

The company emphasizes same-day cutting for most orders, no minimum order quantities, transparent pricing, and the backing of the Empire Family network to cover related needs such as trucking and stone.
