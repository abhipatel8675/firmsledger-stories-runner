# Bellevue Healthcare Central Oregon

**Old description:** Locally owned, operated, and committed to the communities we serve

## About
Bellevue Healthcare operates from its Bend, Oregon location as part of a network of showrooms spanning Washington, Oregon, and Idaho. The company was founded in 2000 in Bellevue, Washington, and remains locally owned and managed. From its inception, the business was built by its own team members with a focus on serving the communities where they live and work.

The company functions as a full-service home medical equipment provider, offering sales and rentals of mobility, respiratory, and daily-living devices. Its product range includes wheelchairs, power chairs, scooters, oxygen concentrators, CPAP and BiPAP units, hospital beds, patient lifts, and bathroom safety equipment. Services extend beyond distribution to include white-glove delivery with setup and instruction, complex rehabilitation technology customizing wheelchairs and pediatric assistive devices, respiratory therapy supported by on-staff therapists, and home modification installations such as stair lifts, ceiling lifts, and walk-in tubs performed through a partner firm. The organization describes itself as the largest local provider in the Pacific Northwest, with same- or next-day delivery across its service area.

Bellevue Healthcare serves individuals requiring home medical equipment, as well as hospice agencies and long-term care facilities. Its customer base includes patients with mobility impairments, pulmonary disorders, and sleep-related breathing issues, along with caregivers and facility managers seeking rental programs or facility-wide equipment support.

The company emphasizes its local presence as a differentiator, citing fast delivery, on-site service, and a hiring model that keeps staff in the community. It also highlights its assistive technology professionals (ATPs) and rehab technicians who provide clinical expertise for complex mobility prescriptions, and 24/7 on-call support for oxygen therapy patients.

# Bend Biomedical

**Old description:** None

## About
Bend Biomedical is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit global health pharmaceutical company based in Prineville, Oregon. Founded in 2019, the organization operates with a mission to deliver high-quality, low-cost medicines to prevent and cure serious diseases in neglected populations worldwide.

The company pursues three complementary aims: identifying and addressing shortages of essential global-health pharmaceuticals, developing and providing affordable standardized field medical kits for emergency and primary care, and building health informatics systems that track the availability, location, demand, and treatment gaps of essential medicines and supplies. Bend Biomedical states it can provide solutions through manufacturing, packaging, procurement, or distribution of medicines such as albendazole, azithromycin, ivermectin, praziquantel, benzathine penicillin, and essential vitamins and rehydration salts.

Its work serves governments, non-governmental organizations, pharmaceutical manufacturers, and frontline health workers in resource-limited settings. Target diseases include yaws, vitamin A deficiency, under-five mortality, neural tube defects, congenital syphilis, Chagas disease, onchocerciasis, lymphatic filariasis, and soil-transmitted helminths, among others.

Bend Biomedical differentiates itself by focusing on preventable or curable diseases where effective treatments exist but access remains inadequate, and where the organization can close meaningful gaps through direct manufacturing, kitting, procurement, or data-driven supply-chain coordination.
