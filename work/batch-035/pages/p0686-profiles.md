## About
Americo Vinyl & Fabric has been operating from its 78,000-square-foot facility in West Memphis, Arkansas, for over half a century. The company is family owned and operated.

The company manufactures custom vinyl table covers, laminated designer table covers, and roll goods, offering over 300 in-house colors and patterns with custom logo printing. It also supplies bulk vinyl films under the Nordic Shield and EPV Plastics lines, including flexible and rigid PVC films, double polished clear, frosted clear, expanded vinyls, oil cloth, and flannel backed vinyl. Production processes are fully controlled in-house, from making special ink concentrates and adhesives to applying finishes, and include converting services such as slitting, sheeting, interleaving, and put-up changes. All products are performance tested at an independent lab and inspected before shipment.

Americo serves restaurants, hotels, sporting venues, churches, nursing homes, schools, amusement parks, casinos, and conference centers, as well as retail distributors, institutions, manufacturers, variety stores, food stores, drug stores, fabric stores, lumber yards, and camps. Its industrial client base includes distributors, heat sealers, vinyl fabricators, book binders, laminators, window and clean room manufacturers, thermal formers, printers, and convention centers.

The company emphasizes its customer-friendly policies: custom cuts with no minimum order, free swatches, patterns stocked and ready to ship, and same or next day shipping for stock items that do not require converting. Its workforce, many of whom have been with the company for years, is cited as a key factor in maintaining rigorous quality standards and total customer satisfaction.

## About
Anchor Semiconductor is headquartered in Santa Clara, California. The company does not publicly disclose its founding year, ownership structure, or the names of its founders.

The company develops vendor-neutral image processing software and toolkits for semiconductor inspection and metrology. Its product line includes the Die-to-Database Pattern Monitor (D2DB-PM), Care Area Generator (YA-CAG), Anchor Hotspot Solution (YA-AHS), Defective Pattern Library (YA-DPL), Post-RET Verification (NS-PRV), and a suite of exploration and analysis tools such as Image Explorer, Target Finder, Result Explorer, and Image to Design Contour (I2DC). These toolkits are built on core engines that customers can integrate into their own automated workflows. Additional technology modules cover parametric rule editing, image processing, and NanoScope modeling and verification.

Anchor serves the semiconductor manufacturing sector, particularly advanced technology nodes, photomask inspection, e-beam inspection, and lithography manufacturing. Known customers include Huali Microelectronics (HLMC) in Shanghai, which has recognized Anchor with a Top 5 Supplier Service Award (2017) and an Excellent Service Supplier Award (2020), and KLA Corporation, with which Anchor announced a strategic partnership in 2021.

Anchor emphasizes its vendor-neutral image processing technology as a key differentiator, enabling custom solutions that integrate with existing production environments. The company also highlights its core engines that allow customers to develop proprietary automated workflows.
