## About
American Textile Systems operates from Long Beach, California, supplying institutional linens and textile products across the United States. The company manufactures and sources a range of textile goods including bed sheets, pillowcases, bath towels, blankets, pillows, fabrics, cleaning rags, footwear, and personal care items such as razors. It provides in-house manufacturing and custom finishing, along with value-added services such as screen printing, property marking, continuous imprinting, and color coding. Products are offered in specifications like T130 thread count for durability in high-use environments. Its customer base spans healthcare facilities, correctional institutions, hospitality venues, retail and consumer channels, shelters, and dormitory programs, serving high-volume institutional bedding programs. The product catalog includes items such as blue pillowcases sized 42 by 34 inches, T130 pillowcases, twin flat sheets, kitchen towels, bath mats, flour sack towels, pot holders, oven mitts, table runners, tablecloths, and placemats. American Textile Systems emphasizes competitive pricing, on-time delivery, and free nationwide shipping as core differentiators, positioning itself as a single-source textile supplier for institutional buyers.

# American Wholesale Furniture

## About
American Wholesale Furniture was founded in 1986 in Indianapolis, Indiana. The company began as a wholesale partnership serving the retail and rent-to-own (RTO) industries across the East and Central Midwest.

The company operates as a wholesale distributor of home furnishings, stocking a focused selection of high-turn items that represent roughly 20% of the product range but generate 80% of sales. It fulfills orders through a fleet of company-owned trucks and drivers, delivering to dealers on a weekly schedule. The operation ships between 600 and 800 stores each week, with most orders shipping the same week they are placed. An online ordering system is available for customers.

American Wholesale Furniture serves two primary channels: traditional retail furniture dealers and rent-to-own (RTO) operators. Its customer base includes thousands of partner stores across the East and Central Midwest regions.

The business was built on a simple model: deep inventory of core home furnishings combined with reliable, same-week delivery via company trucks. This approach has driven continuous growth for over three decades.
