# Bayne's Quality Custom Furniture

**Old description:** Specializing in Custom Cabinets, Furniture, and Architectural Millwork for nearly 30 years.

## About
Bayne's Quality Custom Furniture, Inc. was established in 1980 in Baltimore, Maryland, by woodworker Donald Bayne. The business began in the basement of his home before moving to a manufacturing warehouse and eventually settling at its current location.

The shop produces custom cabinets, furniture, and architectural millwork, including mantels, fireplace surrounds, libraries, built-in cabinets, bars, specialty wall units, kitchen cabinetry, niches, and hand-carved pieces. It also takes on non-standard projects such as staircases, gazebos, and display cases. Work combines traditional cabinetmaking with technical finishing, design support, and installation, carried out by a small team of personally trained staff and a network of skilled artisans.

The company serves homeowners, interior designers, and architects across Maryland and surrounding states, handling both residential and commercial commissions. Past projects range from a formal library and a grand staircase to a display case for a prized golf ball and a custom gazebo, illustrating a willingness to tackle varied challenges.

Founder Donald Bayne emphasizes personal oversight on every job, stating that nothing leaves the shop unless he is satisfied. The firm's differentiator is its blend of old‑world craftsmanship with a problem‑solving approach, aiming to build "furniture quality" into every piece and to exceed client expectations through direct involvement in design, manufacturing, and finish selection.

# Beekley Medical

**Old description:** Positively impacting patients' lives and improving clinical outcomes

## About
Beekley Medical is based in Bristol, Connecticut. The company does not disclose its founding year, ownership structure, or founder names in its public materials.

The company designs and manufactures a range of single-use medical products for imaging and procedural guidance. Its portfolio includes skin markers for mammography and radiation oncology, nasal malodor barriers, flavored beverages for small bowel distention, and semi-permanent tattoos for radiation therapy simulation. Beekley states its innovations are used in more than 10,000 medical facilities worldwide, though it does not publish facility size, headcount, or annual production volumes.

Beekley serves healthcare professionals across the clinical spectrum, with a focus on breast health, CT, MRI, X-ray, radiation oncology, and clinical aromatherapy. Its customers include hospitals, imaging centers, and clinics that perform mammograms, biopsies, lumpectomies, CT scans, MRI procedures, and radiation therapy planning. The company notes that many product ideas originate from the firsthand insight of clinicians who identify opportunities to improve everyday care.

Beekley emphasizes that its products should make a meaningful difference for both patients and clinicians. It differentiates itself through a 100% satisfaction guarantee, no restocking or return fees, and a commitment to ship in-stock orders the same or next business day. A portion of proceeds from its Mammography SPOT® skin markers supports breast cancer research and awareness organizations, and the company sponsors educational programs and advocacy groups for the imaging community.
