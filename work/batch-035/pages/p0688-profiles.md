**Old description:** Proudly Celebrating over 50 Wonderful Years of Quality, Service & Innovation

## About
Founded in 1969 in Tempe, Arizona, Arizona Manufacturing and Embroidery, Inc. (AME) has operated as a family-owned business for over five decades. The company began with a focus on golf head covers and has since expanded its product line while maintaining domestic manufacturing.

AME produces a range of golf accessories including head covers, putter covers, shoe bags, microfiber towels, drawstring bags, and utility pouches. Every item is custom-crafted to order at its Tempe facility, with the company offering six standard head cover styles that can be combined with various fabrics, colors, layouts, logos, and personalization options—yielding over 120,000 unique style configurations. A full-time in-house art staff translates customer artwork into production-ready embroidery designs using specialized design software.

The company sells exclusively through a network of manufacturer’s sales representatives to “green grass” accounts—private clubs, PGA professionals, tour players, junior golfers, and college coaches. AME also participates in the PGA Retirement Plus Program, providing benefits to PGA members. It does not sell directly to consumers.

AME emphasizes its Made-in-USA production, using materials such as genuine leather, UltraLeather, Promessa UltraLeather, Peloton, and Tech Canvas. The family ownership structure ties the company’s reputation directly to product quality, and the firm positions its custom-crafted approach and personalized service as its primary differentiators in the golf accessory market.

# Arkansas Industrial Fabricator

**Old description:** 40+ Years Of Design & Fabrication Providing Custom Solutions Across Multiple Industries​

## About
Arkansas Industrial Fabricator operates from Sheridan, Arkansas, where it has delivered design and fabrication services for over 40 years. The privately held company emphasizes domestic production, stating that its work is American-made.

The shop provides a range of welding and fabrication services, including weld inspection, welder and fitter qualification testing, development of pre-qualified and qualified weld procedure specifications (WPS), and third-party non-destructive examination coordination (PT, UT, RT, PAUT, HD, PMI). In-house quality project management covers weld mapping, inspection test plans, and WPS administration. Additional capabilities include cladding, lining, wear plastics and extrusion welding, industry-specific coatings, and equipment service-life extension. The company also offers design-build and custom fabrication, structural detailing with third-party PE stamps, and CAD drawing creation with part-number issuance for traceability and repeatable production. It does not hold ASME U-, S-, or R-stamps.

Customers span aviation and defense (Raytheon Systems, BF Goodrich, Day & Zimmerman, U.S. Navy P-3 Orion project), paper and pulp (International Paper, Georgia Pacific, Evergreen Corporation), industrial manufacturing (ALCOA World Alumina, 3M Corp, Army Corps of Engineers, U.S. Vanadium), heavy construction (HGL Construction, Nabholz Industrial Services, CDI Contractors), manufacturing support (Ryerson, Central Moloney, Boyd Metals), and oil, energy, and gas (Siemens Energy, Marathon Petroleum, Baker Hughes, Koch Heat Transfer, Apache Corporation, Cameron).

The firm differentiates itself through its AWS QC1-certified welding inspector on staff, in-house quality project management, and the practice of issuing part-numbered CAD drawings to ensure traceability and repeatable fabrication. It coordinates third-party NDE to meet client inspection test plans and provides design-build services from concept or from customer-supplied drawings.

## About
Arnott Inc operates from Merritt Island, Florida, where it designs and manufactures aftermarket air and adaptive suspension components for the global automotive market. The company does not disclose its founding year or ownership structure in the available materials.

The firm produces replacement air springs, struts, compressors, and coil-spring conversion kits, covering approximately 93 percent of vehicles worldwide. Its manufacturing approach emphasizes meticulous engineering, premium material selection, and rigorous testing at every production stage. A global distribution network supports parts availability across North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia.

Customers include independent repair shops, dealership technicians, and vehicle owners servicing brands such as Audi, Mercedes-Benz, Range Rover, and light trucks. Arnott also runs a free, industry-recognized certification program that trains technicians on air suspension diagnosis and repair in 90 minutes or less.

The company states its differentiation lies in precision manufacturing combined with expert training and seamless support, aiming to reduce comebacks and dealer referrals while unlocking new revenue streams for service providers.

The company operates as an independent distributor with a corporate facility in Chino, California, and additional stocking locations in Hayward, California, and Phoenix, Arizona.

The firm distributes wire, cable, connectivity components, and security products from a broad roster of manufacturing partners. It maintains millions of feet of category cable in stock and offers custom cable assembly, same‑day shipping on stocked items, will‑call pickup, on‑site customer training, and continuing‑education certification programs. Three strategically placed West Coast locations support rapid fulfillment across California and Arizona.

Arrow serves commercial, industrial, communications, OEM, government, and security markets. Its customer base includes contractors, end users, system integrators, and design consultants who rely on the distributor for low‑voltage and telecommunications infrastructure.
