## About
Applied Molding Technologies, LLC was founded in 2006 in Frederic, Wisconsin. The company operates as a custom molder with no proprietary product lines, serving customers globally from its single facility.

The shop provides thermoplastic injection molding on all-electric presses ranging from 55 to 850 tons of clamp force, and thermoset compression, transfer, and injection molding on presses from 150 to 300 tons. It also offers value-added mechanical assembly including heat-staking, ultrasonic inserts, sub-assemblies, kitting, and ship-direct packaging. Production runs 24/7/365 on a four-shift rotation designed to minimize start-up and shut-down scrap. Tooling is sourced both domestically and off-shore, with in-house maintenance and repair.

Applied Molding serves a global customer base with varying technical, regulatory, and commercial requirements. Its thermoset capabilities target electrical and dielectric components as well as large or thick-section parts, while its thermoplastic work spans commodity and engineering-grade resins. The company scales engineering, quality, and program-management support to each customer's needs.

The business was built on the premise that domestic manufacturing could compete through a fundamentally different operating model rather than low-wage competition. Its founders designed the company for efficiency, customization, and adaptability—retaining core capabilities in-house while partnering for ancillary functions, and continuously revisiting processes through structured improvement.
