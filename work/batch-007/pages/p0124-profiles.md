The shop provides a full range of timber frame services, including design, milling, joinery, and on-site installation. Clients include homeowners seeking custom timber frame residences, as well as commercial and institutional projects such as the restoration of the historic Glacier Park Lodge in East Glacier, Montana. The company’s work has been installed on-grid and off-grid in Montana, Wyoming, Washington, British Columbia, and Alberta.

Wild Mile Woodworks emphasizes a blend of traditional old-world craftsmanship with modern design techniques, positioning itself as a full-service timber frame shop that controls the process from design through milling and joinery.
