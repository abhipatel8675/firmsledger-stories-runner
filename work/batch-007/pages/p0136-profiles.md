## About
American Bean operates from its headquarters in Petersburg, North Dakota, with additional facilities in Angus and Oslo, Minnesota, and Sharon, North Dakota. The company was founded on the principle that quality seed products drive higher yields and profitability for growers and buyers, though the exact founding year and ownership structure are not publicly disclosed.

The company specializes in producing and packaging a wide variety of dry bean certified seed, including pinto and cranberry beans. Its grower network adheres to rigorous standards to ensure seed quality, and the company's food safety program is audited and certified under the Primus GFS standard, an internationally recognized GFSI benchmark. American Bean also holds HACCP certification.

American Bean serves agricultural growers seeking certified seed for planting, as well as buyers and food processors worldwide who require high-quality dry beans. Its customer base includes farmers planning seasonal seeding and international buyers sourcing dry beans for food production.

The company states it was founded on the principle that quality products mean increased yields and higher profitability for customers, growers, and buyers alike, and that its products are held to a higher standard through each step of the process from farm to fork.
