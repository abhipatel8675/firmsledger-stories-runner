The JATC runs a five-year sheet metal apprenticeship combining on-the-job training with classroom instruction across four disciplines: general sheet metal fabrication and installation, testing adjusting and balancing (TAB), HVAC service, and residential sheet metal. At any given time roughly 500 apprentices are enrolled, and the program graduates approximately 98 journey-level workers each year. Instruction is delivered by seven full-time instructors supported by three administrators and two support staff, collectively holding nearly 210 years of industry experience. The DuPont campus hosts the SMART Heroes program, providing a direct entry route for active-duty military personnel and veterans. The program also partners with Bates Technical College for associate-degree credit.

The committee’s stated mission is to bring labor and management together to develop a highly skilled, competitive workforce for the evolving sheet metal industry. By offering tuition-free training, zero student debt, and a clear path to union wages and benefits — including retirement, family medical, dental, and vision — the JATC positions the apprenticeship as a career alternative to traditional college pathways.
