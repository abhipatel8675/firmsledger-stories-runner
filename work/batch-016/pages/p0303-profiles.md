## About

The shop produces custom thermoplastic extruded, injection molded, and blow molded parts, running all three processes under one roof. An in-house tool shop controls tooling design and fabrication, while secondary operations include fabrication, assembly, and special packaging. The facility also maintains a quality database for statistical process control and capability studies, though headcount and floor space are not disclosed.

The company serves a broad range of industries, with documented experience in products such as 45-foot-diameter circular curtain enclosures and non-standard blow-molded bottles requiring specialized head tooling. Customers receive engineering assistance and material selection guidance for filled and unfilled engineered resins.

C-K Plastics emphasizes its willingness to take on challenging geometries and smaller runs, a mindset traced to its founding. The 2018 ISO 2001:2015 certification, lean manufacturing practices, and just-in-time delivery programs are cited as mechanisms to reduce lead times and total cost.

# C.R. International Inc.

**Old description:** Providing our customers with quality electronic manufacturing services for over 40 years!

## About
C.R. International Inc. (CRI) is a woman-owned small business headquartered in Laurel, Maryland. Founded in 1981, the company began as a small engineering firm supplying communications and data-processing equipment to federal customers, including protocol adapters and specialized radio/telephone interface devices.

Today CRI operates as a full-service electronic manufacturing services (EMS) provider from an 18,000-square-foot, climate-controlled facility. The shop runs two surface-mount technology lines equipped with DEK screen printers and Mycronic pick-and-place machines capable of placing 01005 components through large BGAs, and offers paste printing, conformal coating (acrylic, silicone, epoxy), box-build electro-mechanical assembly, functional testing, first-article inspection, and component verification. Services span prototype development to volume production, supported by AEGIS manufacturing software for work instructions and process control.

CRI serves aerospace and flight programs, defense and military agencies, communications, industrial, medical, and commercial customers, as well as start-ups. Notable references include NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory—where CRI-populated boards supported pyrotechnic activation on a test flight—and Department of Defense prime contractors who rely on the company for tactical electronics.

The company emphasizes partnership and high-reliability manufacturing, maintaining IPC Class III as its standard process, with personnel trained to J-STD-001 and IPC-A-610, including flight-certified soldering and inspection. Certifications include ISO 9001:2015, ITAR registration, CMMC Level 2 C3PAO, and RoHS compliance, reflecting a focus on mission-critical quality and data security.
