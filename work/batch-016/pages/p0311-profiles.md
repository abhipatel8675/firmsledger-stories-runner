The business was acquired by the Martens family in 1936, shifting to stains and finishes for furniture makers. In the 1970s, the company added unfinished pine furniture at its Exchange Street location. The Levin family took ownership in 2014, refocusing the brand on exclusive furniture designs crafted from domestically sourced North American hardwoods. Today, Chilton operates as a family-owned furniture brand with a flagship showroom in Portland and a second location in Freeport.

The company designs and sells a range of hardwood furniture—including seating, tables, storage, beds, and accessories—using cherry, maple, walnut, and white oak sourced in the United States. Production is carried out by a network of independent makers in Maine and the Midwest, with Chilton coordinating design, material sourcing, and quality assurance. Chilton serves both retail customers and trade professionals through a dedicated trade partner program that extends discounts and benefits to interior designers and architects. Customer reviews highlight the company’s personal service and the ability to purchase without seeing pieces in person. The brand’s aesthetic centers on New England traditions and natural materials.

The Levin family’s acquisition was driven by a desire to honor Chilton Manufacturing Co.’s legacy while committing to the highest quality and paying tribute to New England’s history. The company emphasizes that its furniture is handmade in the USA from premium natural materials, positioning itself as a steward of enduring craftsmanship.
