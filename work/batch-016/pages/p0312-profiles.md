The company's stated mission is to provide global access to safe and effective prescription digital therapeutics. Its differentiators include a platform-based approach that integrates clinically proven therapies with software-driven neuromodulation, a track record of first-of-kind FDA clearances, and a commitment to rigorous clinical validation through randomized controlled trials.
