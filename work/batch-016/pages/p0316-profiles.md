## About
Contempora Fabrics Inc. was founded in 1972 by Lacy C. Nance in Lumberton, North Carolina. Originally a contract knitter producing fine-gauge interlock and single-knit fabrics, the company transitioned to an employee stock ownership plan (ESOP) in 1984 and became 100% employee-owned by 1988. Today it operates as a fully employee-owned circular knit manufacturer.

The company runs 175 circular knitting machines in a 150,000-square-foot facility on 29 acres, with a production capacity of 2 million pounds of fabric per month. It offers both coarse and fine-gauge knits, supplying greige goods and finished fabrics. Its lint-controlled, humidity-defined manufacturing environment allows simultaneous processing of all fiber types. An in-house research and development team provides fabric analysis and works with customers on the latest fiber and technology trends.

Contempora serves brands, retailers, and manufacturers across performance and activewear, athletic and team apparel, fashion and formal wear, casual and career wear, intimate apparel and shapewear, and swimwear and beachwear. A noted client is Boombah, which credited the company with helping its transition to CAFTA-compliant fabrics. The staff also manages weekly container shipments to Central America under USMCA and CAFTA-DR requirements.

The employee-owned structure is cited as a driver of quality and timely delivery. The company emphasizes competitive pricing, short lead times, and sustainability initiatives including energy-efficient lighting, upgraded HVAC, and recycling of cardboard, yarn scrap, plastic, and paper byproducts.

# Council Stainless & Sheet Metal

**Old description:** Custom items for commercial kitchen equipment, specialty enclosures, signage and more.

## About
Council Stainless & Sheet Metal operates from a 6,000-square-foot shop in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. The company was founded in 1979 and has spent more than 45 years serving the region with custom metal fabrication.

The shop focuses on stainless steel and sheet metal work, offering CAD design, CNC sheet metal fabrication, waterjet cutting, CNC cold forming, and 220-ton press brake forming. Capabilities extend from individual parts to sub-assemblies and complete systems, with field installation and repair services available for items the company fabricates as well as pieces made by others.

Primary markets include commercial kitchen equipment — restaurant tables, exhaust vent hoods, sinks, countertops, and cabinets — along with architectural accents such as fascia systems and sign components. The company also produces specialty enclosures, racks, and custom assemblies for oilfield equipment and industrial applications.

Leadership emphasizes a single-source model: design, fabrication, and field installation under one roof. The firm also highlights its ability to perform field repairs on existing installations, a service it says reduces downtime for customers.
