## About

It offers both greige and finished fabrics, supported by an in-house research and development team that provides fabric analysis and works with the latest fiber technologies. The lint-controlled, humidity-defined manufacturing environment allows simultaneous processing of all fiber types.

Contempora serves brands, retailers, and manufacturers across performance and activewear, athletic and team apparel, fashion and formal wear, casual and career wear, intimate apparel and shapewear, and swimwear and beachwear. Production volumes span from one-off prototypes to several hundred thousand units annually.

Corbitt serves OEMs in earth-moving, mining, agricultural, electrical, transportation, recreation, and food-service markets. In transportation it produces dock cleats, transmission casings, manifolds, and railroad-car parts as the industry shifts from iron to aluminum. For food service it re-engineered a motor housing that eliminated leakage and cut scrap rates. Electrical work includes explosion-proof receptacles and power-line connectors, while powertrain projects focus on light- and heavy-duty gearboxes held to super-close tolerances.

The company emphasizes a "what you need, when and where you need it" delivery guarantee backed by in-house testing protocols that fast-track part deployment.
