## About
C & T Fabrication, located in Sparta, Michigan, was founded in 2003. The company began as a specialist in stainless steel fabrication, laser cutting, process piping, and material handling.

The shop operates a 40,000-square-foot facility equipped with two Mazak CNC fiber optic lasers capable of cutting 1-inch hot-rolled steel and 3/4-inch stainless steel plate, three Accurpress CNC brakes with 3/8-inch by 12-foot bending capacity, plus a steel roller, shear, saw, drill presses, iron worker, tube bender, and scissor lift. Services include precision laser cutting, bending, rolling, welding (TIG, MIG, stick), engineering and design, and on-site installation of fabricated products.

C & T Fabrication serves manufacturers, food processing plants, and pharmaceutical companies, among other sectors. The company builds products for industry leaders and installs them at job sites throughout West Michigan.

The company emphasizes customer satisfaction, superior quality, and authentic relationships, leveraging over 30 years of sheet metal engineering experience to deliver cost-conscious solutions.
