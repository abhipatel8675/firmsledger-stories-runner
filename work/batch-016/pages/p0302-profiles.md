C & T Fabrication was founded in 2003 in Sparta, Michigan. The company operates as a metal fabrication shop specializing in stainless steel, laser cutting, process piping, and material handling.

The shop runs two Mazak CNC fiber‑optic lasers capable of cutting 1‑inch hot‑rolled plate and 3/4‑inch stainless plate, three Accurpress CNC brakes that bend 3/8‑inch plate up to 12 feet at 250 tons, plus a steel roller, shear, saw, drill presses, iron worker, tube bender and scissor lift. All work is performed in a 40,000‑square‑foot facility, where the team handles custom fabrication, precision laser cutting, bending, rolling, welding and engineering support.

C & T serves manufacturers, food‑processing plants, pharmaceutical companies and other industrial customers throughout West Michigan, delivering fabricated assemblies and installing them on‑site.

The firm emphasizes customer satisfaction, dependability and superior quality, citing over 30 years of combined manufacturing, design and engineering experience. It aims to build authentic relationships while providing quality products at a cost‑conscious price point.
