## About
Central Sheet Metal Products, Inc. has operated from Skokie, Illinois since its founding in 1912. The family-owned business, now in its fifth generation under the Silverstein family—Joel and Peter Silverstein representing the fourth generation and Ron and Rob Silverstein the fifth—began as a Chicago storefront supplying building materials such as copper, roof flashings, gutters, and downspouts. The company specializes in precision sheet metal fabrication, producing brackets, enclosures, housings, chassis, faceplates, panels, covers, rack-mount components, and custom OEM part housings. Core processes include fiber laser cutting with lights-out automation, CNC bending and forming on multiple press brakes up to 135 metric tons, CNC turret punching, welding and spot welding, drilling, tapping, countersinking, hardware insertion, shearing, and vibratory finishing. In-house engineering uses SolidWorks 3D solid modeling for design, reverse engineering, and reengineering. The shop also manages contract finishing—powder coating, e-coating, painting, silk screening, plating, and anodizing—through long-standing vendor relationships. Capabilities span small, medium, and large volume runs with multiple part-mix flexibility.

Customers span OEM medical and dental devices, electronics, rack-mount and audio/visual equipment, computer peripherals, industrial machinery, electrical and air-handling systems, display and point-of-purchase fixtures, gaming, food and beverage vending, and agricultural equipment. The firm serves as a contract manufacturer for OEMs across these sectors.

The company cites its unchanged philosophy of quality craftsmanship and complete customer satisfaction as its differentiator, emphasizing direct communication, troubleshooting, on-time delivery, and repeat business. Continuous improvement drives capital investment in manufacturing technology, while incoming material inspection, calibrated measuring equipment, first-article inspection reports, and certificates of conformance underpin quality assurance. A long-standing delivery record with few disruptions supports its business-continuity motto: “what is good for you, is good for us.”

# Cherry Brook Woodworks

**Old description:** Cherry Brook Woodworks creates custom designed furniture, historic reproductions, their own designs, and also repairs valuable antiques.  Working for individual homeowners, architects, interior designers, and museums, Cherry Brook Woodworks builds custom furniture that will fit in any environment and will last for generations.

## About
Cherry Brook Woodworks operates from Canton, Connecticut, where in 2001 founder Peter Aleksa and his family converted two historic apple barns on their property into a woodworking shop. The business remains family-run, with Aleksa’s wife and sons supporting the operation.

The shop produces custom furniture, museum-quality antique reproductions, and provides repair and restoration services for heirloom pieces. All work is performed by hand using traditional joinery and finishing techniques; the facility comprises the converted barns and serves clients nationally. No headcount or volume figures are disclosed.

Customers include individual homeowners, interior designers, architects, and museums. Projects have shipped locally and across the country, with examples such as period sideboards, Shaker-style tables, and reproduced museum pieces.

Aleksa emphasizes a collaborative process, listening closely to clients to deliver pieces that are “unique, meaningful and built to last for generations.” The company cites its master-craftsman expertise, including training under a German furniture maker, as a differentiator.
