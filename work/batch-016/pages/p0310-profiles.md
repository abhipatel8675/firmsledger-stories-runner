**Old description:** Cherry Brook Woodworks creates custom designed furniture, historic reproductions, their own designs, and also repairs valuable antiques.  Working for individual homeowners, architects, interior designers, and museums, Cherry Brook Woodworks builds custom furniture that will fit in any environment and will last for generations.

## About
Cherry Brook Woodworks operates from Canton, Connecticut, where in 2001 the Aleksa family purchased a home in the historic district and converted two antique apple barns into a woodworking shop. The business was founded by Peter Aleksa, a fine furniture maker who had spent years training under a master German craftsman and working in Connecticut shops specializing in period furniture and restoration.

The shop produces custom furniture, museum-quality antique reproductions, and repair and restoration services for heirloom pieces, all made by hand using traditional joinery and period-appropriate finishes. The workspace remains in the original barn structures on the family property, and the output ranges from one-off commissions to a rotating catalog of original designs such as Shaker tripod tables, sideboards, and chests crafted in tiger maple, birdseye maple, cherry, white oak, and walnut.

Clients include individual homeowners, interior designers, architects, and museums, with pieces shipped throughout the United States. The company has reproduced historic furniture from photographs or originals for institutional clients and restored family heirlooms for private owners.

Aleksa emphasizes a collaborative process, listening closely to each client to create furniture that fits specific spaces and styles, a level of personal involvement he contrasts with larger production shops. His approach stems from a belief that the relationship with the client is as important as the finished piece, and that honoring the materials and history behind each project results in furniture built to last generations.
