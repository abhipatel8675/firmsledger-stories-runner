## About
The Peterson Company operates from Auburn, Washington, with a second production facility in Moonachie, New Jersey. The business, also known as Peterson Foods, has built a reputation as a specialty food supplier and value-added processor for the Pacific Northwest and beyond. No founding year or ownership details are publicly stated.

The company maintains a broad catalog of domestic and imported artisan cheeses, charcuterie, bakery items, and specialty ingredients, available through a 24/7 online ordering system. Its core manufacturing service is custom cutting and packing: two third-party-audited facilities provide hand wrapping, shrink wrapping, cryovac vacuum packaging, cheese cubing, portioning into cups or bags, variety tray and gift box assembly, and custom labeling. The same lines also handle chocolate, candies, and nuts.

Customers include independent restaurants, hotel kitchens, specialty retailers, and pastry chefs such as the one at Seattle’s Ray’s Boathouse who has sourced from Peterson for 25 years. Buyers cite the one-stop-shop inventory, free delivery, and responsive customer service as reasons they consolidate purchasing through the company.

Peterson positions its cutting and packing service as a way for clients to eliminate in-house labor, trim loss, and liability while preserving freshness and upscale presentation. The monthly Provisions Flyer highlights seasonal items across cheese, charcuterie, bakery, and ingredient categories.

# The Sam Tell Companies

**Old description:** An award-winning kitchen design team, highly skilled equipment installers, and restaurant smallwares experts all under one roof

## About
The Sam Tell Companies traces its roots to 1956, when Sam Tell and his son Neil opened a two-person operation on Bleecker Street in New York City. The family-owned dealership began with a focus on dependable foodservice solutions, and by the 1980s the founder's grandson Marc Tell had joined the business alongside Dan Saltzman. Today Marc serves as chief executive, a team of 180 from two Long Island warehouses and a Manhattan design showroom.

The company operates as a full-service foodservice equipment and supplies dealer, offering design, build, and supply capabilities under one roof. Its award-winning design team creates commercial kitchen layouts, while skilled installers handle equipment placement and project execution. The supply division stocks tens of thousands of products ranging from smallwares to heavy equipment, and the firm provides shipping and delivery across the tri-state area and nationwide. Sam Tell ranks as the 30th largest foodservice equipment and supplies distributor in the United States.

Clients span restaurants and bars, sports and entertainment venues, hospitality, corporate dining, healthcare, education, country clubs, retail, and airport and travel lounges. Notable projects include the kitchen at Lure Fishbar, where chef Josh Capon credited Sam Tell with building the "heart and soul" of the operation.

The company emphasizes what it calls the "Sam Tell Difference": proactive customer service backed by a century of combined experience, tailored attention for each unique operation, and a culture that hires only passionate industry professionals. Those principles echo the founding values of dependability, integrity, and honesty that guided the original father-and-son team.

## About
The Sublimation Station Inc. operates from Orlando, Florida, where it began as a print shop driven by a vision to be different. The facility stocks more than 10,000 yards of fabric and has same‑day access to a major wholesale supplier, enabling a print‑on‑demand model with no minimum order quantity on sportswear.

Customers range from individual creators to brands and teams seeking custom merchandise, apparel, youth items, accessories, home‑and‑living products, and team sportswear. The Station also provides fulfillment and drop‑shipping so clients can focus on sales and marketing.

The business emphasizes sustainability through its print‑on‑demand approach, which reduces waste by producing only what is ordered, and by sourcing organic fabrics, recycled materials, and non‑toxic inks. Energy‑efficient equipment further lowers the carbon footprint. Direct‑to‑film printing, unlimited personalization, and rush‑order service round out the offering.
