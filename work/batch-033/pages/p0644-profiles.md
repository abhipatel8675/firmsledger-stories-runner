## About
Sturges Electronics LLC was founded in 1978 in Dryden, New York, as a woman-owned small business specializing in custom wire and cable harness assemblies. The company has operated continuously from its upstate New York location for over four decades.

The company provides custom "build to print" wire and cable harnesses ranging from simple two-circuit assemblies to complex designs with 50 or more circuits, using wire gauges from 30 AWG to 2 AWG. Its capabilities combine automated processes on a Schleuniger wire cutting, stripping, and crimping machine with semi-automated TE and Molex crimp presses, micro soldering under microscope, RF cable assembly, and IPC Class 2 and 3 testing for continuity, insulation resistance, and dielectric withstanding voltage. Sturges handles prototype and first-article builds as well as production runs from one or two units up to thousands, and offers box build assembly and overmolded cables through subcontract partners.

Sturges serves the industrial, defense, medical, transportation, and military markets, delivering harnesses for applications in those sectors. The company's customer base includes military and defense contractors, medical device manufacturers, and transportation equipment builders.

Sturges differentiates itself through its ISO 9001:2015 certification, IPC/WHMA-A-620 specialist certified inspectors, ITAR registration, and cybersecurity compliance, all within a woman-owned small business structure that emphasizes responsive service for both prototype and high-volume orders.
