A team of 30-plus technicians handles system refurbishment, cleanroom repair, sub‑assembly repair (particularly Lam Research components), installation, process qualification, and global field service. # Tezzaron Semiconductor Corporation

**Old description:** TSV-free 3D: Stacks the size of a single die. Better by design.

## About
Tezzaron Semiconductor Corporation is headquartered in Naperville, Illinois. The company entered the spotlight in 2004 when it demonstrated the world's first successful wafer-stacked 3D integrated circuits with through-silicon interconnects, including microprocessors, sensors, and SRAM devices. While the exact founding year and ownership structure are not publicly disclosed, the firm has since collaborated with dozens of customers to develop custom 3D-ICs for both prototyping and commercial production.

Tezzaron's core offering is its Di3D technology, a transistor-level 3D integration platform that combines wafer bonding, proprietary SuperContacts®, dis-integrated architectures, and BiSTAR® programmable self-test. The company handles the full development flow: adapting design tools, rewriting floor-planning rules, inventing and fabricating new structures, and creating novel test techniques and circuits. This end-to-end capability enables high-density, high-speed vertical interconnects in a footprint the size of a single die.

The technology targets high-performance semiconductor applications, including microprocessors, sensors, and memory devices. Tezzaron has participated in multiple multi-project wafer (MPW) runs for 3D-IC prototypes, partnering with organizations such as CMP (Circuits Multi Projets) in France. Its customer base spans companies seeking custom 3D-IC solutions for prototyping and volume commercialization.

Tezzaron was founded on the premise that conventional scaling had reached its limits and that a radical redesign of semiconductor component development was necessary. The company states that its approach "changes everything" about 3D integration, aiming to build a Di3D™ ecosystem of partners and licensees where the high-performance world can raise the bar for density and speed.
