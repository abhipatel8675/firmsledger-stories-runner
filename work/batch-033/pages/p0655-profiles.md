Its Shenzhen facility spans roughly 250,000 square feet and employs about 3,000 production workers, while the Providence headquarters houses an engineering team with over 100 years of combined design experience and an on-site laboratory where qualification testing is witnessed by UL and cUL representatives.

Tower serves the construction, commercial/industrial, and residential markets, and provides OEM safety products to Fortune 500 companies. The firm participates in standards bodies including NEMA, NFPA, AHAM, and NEC, and sits on UL’s 1699 STP committee for LCDI/AFCI.

The business was built on Shatkin’s pioneering work in GFCI technology, which has yielded more than 20 patents. Tower emphasizes safety-first design to UL and CSA standards, rapid custom development, and a vertically integrated model that keeps qualification testing in-house under UL supervision.
