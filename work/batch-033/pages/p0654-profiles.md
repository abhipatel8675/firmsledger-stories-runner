## About
Three Five Compounds, also known as III-V Compounds, operates a sales office in New York, New York, and a production facility in Asia. The company was established as a supplier of opto-electronic semiconductor materials, though the exact founding year and ownership details are not publicly disclosed.

The company manufactures a broad range of opto-electronic products, from epitaxial wafers and raw dice through packaged devices and finished modules. Its material portfolio includes GaP, GaAsP, AlGaAs, AlInGaP, and GaN for emitters covering 350–1100 nm, plus silicon PIN photodiode and phototransistor chips. Finished goods span LED display modules (full-color, 7-segment), LCD modules, OLEDs, discrete through-hole LEDs (3 mm and 5 mm) and surface-mount packages (1206, 0805, 0603, 0402) in single, bi-color, and tri-color configurations. Specialty packages such as flat-top and TO-header devices serve fiber-optic and sensor applications. III-V also supplies plastic, ESKA, and silica optical fiber, and provides printed circuit board assembly (PCBA/PWBA) and flexible printed circuit (FPC) assembly, including Kapton-based circuits. The Asian production floor employs over 400 workers.

Customers come from general lighting, display, automotive, sensor, medical, telecommunication, military, and commercial sectors. Medical and military companies worldwide, as well as traffic and automotive clients, use the company’s components. Examples include medical sensor and board assemblies, silica fiber for telecommunication and medical usage, and medical cable and wire harnesses.

The company emphasizes a "one-stop-shop" model, assisting customers from initial concept through production completion. It highlights flexibility, high-quality standards, just-in-time delivery, and special product selection to meet strict application requirements as key differentiators.
