## About
Summers Manufacturing, Inc. is based in Devils Lake, North Dakota. The company traces its roots to 1965 when Harley Summers purchased patent rights for Goebel truck and pickup hoists and began production in a small shop in Maddock, ND. He incorporated the business as Summers Manufacturing Company, Inc. in 1969. In 2007, the company became 100 percent employee-owned through an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP).

The company designs and manufactures a range of agricultural equipment, including tillage tools (vertical tillage, variable-rate tillage, disk rippers, chisel plows, harrows, and rolling choppers), land rollers (coil packers, hydraulic-fold and trail-type Super Rollers), application equipment (Spray Fill Xpress sprayer fill systems, grain handling conveyors, and field belts), mounted attachments (harrows, rolling baskets, rolling choppers), and rock pickers. Production takes place at its Devils Lake campus, which includes a 26,000-square-foot factory built in 1980 and expanded multiple times through 2010, and an Aberdeen, South Dakota facility added in 2013 for assembly, parts, and distribution.

Summers serves the agricultural sector, selling its equipment to farmers and through a dealer network across North America. The company collaborates with research and technology partners such as Grand Farm (Fargo, ND), AgRevival (Gibbon, MN), PTI (Pontiac, IL), and Mach to develop variable-rate tillage and precision agriculture solutions.

The company emphasizes field-tested durability, citing its 2000 introduction of the industry's first true vertical tillage tool (the Supercoulter) and a 10-year limited warranty on select land roller models launched in 2018. Its stated mission is to build field-tested tough equipment that helps farmers feed and fuel the world.
