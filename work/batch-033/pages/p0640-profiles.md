# Southern New Jersey Steel Co

**Old description:** The multifaceted capabilities of Southern New Jersey Steel enable us to provide an array of services to meet the most diverse project requirements.

## About
Southern New Jersey Steel (SNJS) operates from Vineland, New Jersey, tracing its roots to 1975. The company began as a steel contracting firm serving the construction industry across the Eastern United States.

SNJS provides structural and miscellaneous steel fabrication and erection services. Its partnering facilities hold American Institute of Steel Construction (AISC) certification and run CNC drill lines, CNC angle lines, and computerized plate burning equipment for oxygen and plasma cutting. The shop produces bolted shop connections, clips, lintels, one-piece trusses, and a range of miscellaneous items including stairs, rails, ladders, grating, spiral stairs, monumental stairs, glass railings, and bronze railings. Pipe rails are formed on a mandrel bending machine to eliminate extra components. The company also supplies steel joists and metal decking, manages erection through experienced subcontractors, and uses Tekla Structures for 3D drafting, BIM modeling, and engineering calculations.

The contractor works directly with public entities and general contractors on projects valued from hundreds to several million dollars. Its portfolio spans public construction such as schools, libraries, prisons, and municipal buildings; industrial work at refineries and manufacturing plants; private commercial projects including supermarkets, retail centers, and warehouses; medical facilities such as Shore Memorial Hospital and Kennedy Health; and higher education projects at Rowan University and the University of Pennsylvania. SNJS is also licensed for casino construction in New Jersey and Pennsylvania.

The company emphasizes performance and precision, aiming to deliver maximum customer satisfaction regardless of project complexity or timeline. It maintains bonding resources that meet mandatory criteria and holds approved-contractor status in multiple states for public works.
