## About
State Electronics Parts Corporation, based in East Hanover, New Jersey, has operated as a broad-line distributor of electronic components and hardware since its founding in 1956. The company remains privately held, though ownership details are not publicly disclosed.

The firm stocks and sells product lines from franchises including Honeywell Sensing and Control, Bourns, ETI Systems, Stego, and Mallory/Sonalert. In addition to distribution, State Electronics manufactures custom modular potentiometers under its Mod-Pots brand and is the sole source authorized by Honeywell to produce discontinued Honeywell potentiometer lines. The company also offers engineering-level support for product selection and technical inquiries.

Its customer base spans aerospace, defense, and space programs—evidenced by NASA-certified, hermetically sealed precision thermostats—as well as commercial, industrial, and military markets. The product portfolio includes snap-action switches, toggle assemblies, proximity sensors, pressure sensors, and audible signal devices.

State Electronics differentiates itself through its unique Honeywell authorization for legacy potentiometer production, dual ISO 9001:2015 and AS9100D certifications, and a sales team with deep technical experience in electronics distribution.
