Contraxx manufactures custom hospitality furniture including guest room casegoods, beds, seating, banquettes, tables, lobby cabinetry, and bespoke furnishings for hotels, resorts, restaurants, casinos, senior living, and mixed-use properties. The company was founded on the belief that furniture manufacturing jobs should stay in America, and it highlights made-exclusively-in-America production, custom design flexibility, turnkey installation, on-site field measurements, and time-critical delivery as core value propositions.

# Conway Corp

**Old description:** Our commitment to the community where we work and live is the reason we were founded. We remain dedicated to enhancing the quality of life in our community through our services, education initiatives and economic development involvement.

## About
Conway Corp has operated from Conway, Arkansas since 1929, when city leaders signed a charter on May 6 to create the municipally owned corporation. The utility began by taking over the city's electric light plant and, within a year, assumed responsibility for waterworks and sewer operations. It remains a public, nonprofit entity governed by a local board, with all revenues reinvested in the community.

The corporation delivers a full suite of utility and telecommunications services: electric distribution, water treatment and distribution, wastewater collection and treatment, cable video, high‑speed internet over a fiber‑coax network, digital voice, and security and automation. It also runs a solar energy program and an IPTV streaming product, ConwayCorpTV. Crews work 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, maintaining substations, treatment plants, and a geographic information system that maps the infrastructure.

Customers are residential and commercial users within the Conway service territory. The utility serves households, local businesses, and municipal facilities, providing both basic utility needs and advanced broadband and entertainment packages.

The founding charter tied the organization directly to community well‑being, a mission reiterated in its core values of customer satisfaction, innovation, safety, reliability, responsibility, and community engagement. Profits are directed back into infrastructure upgrades, education initiatives, and economic development rather than distributed to shareholders.
