## About
The Valparaiso, Indiana facility began in 1979 as Custom Machining Services, establishing itself as a key domestic source for hydraulic hose crimpers, crimping dies, and related hose equipment. German technology group Continental acquired the plant on November 1, 2017, integrating it into a global automotive supplier network. The site employs approximately 100 people and sits about an hour southeast of Chicago in the community known locally as Valpo. Continental’s North American headquarters are located in Culpeper, Virginia.

The Valparaiso plant specializes in manufacturing hydraulic hose assembly equipment, including crimpers and dies, serving the fluid power distribution market. As part of Continental Corporation, the location operates alongside a broader U.S. manufacturing footprint that includes sites in Clinton, Mississippi; Fairlawn, Ohio; Fort Mill, South Carolina; Marysville, Ohio; Mount Vernon, Illinois; and Sumter, South Carolina. The company’s engineering and technology teams develop radar, sensor, and autonomous driving systems, with recent milestones such as 200 million radar units produced.

Primary customers include hydraulic hose distributors and original equipment manufacturers in the fluid power sector. Through its automotive division—soon to be rebranded as Aumovio—Continental supplies advanced driver-assistance systems to global automakers. Strategic partnerships with Aurora Innovation and NVIDIA aim to deploy driverless trucks at scale, while collaboration with trinamiX on biometric sensing displays earned a CES Innovation Award.

Continental states that its Valparaiso operation distinguishes itself as one of the U.S. producers of hydraulic hose crimping equipment. The corporation emphasizes a mission of advancing safety technologies to get people “SafelyThere,” guided by core values of trust, passion to win, freedom to act, and mutual accountability. The Indiana plant also runs a state-recognized machinist apprenticeship and an assembly technician seal certification program.

## About

The company emphasizes its integrated manufacturing approach, AS9100 and ISO 9001 certifications, and a team-based operation focused on fast turnaround and competitive pricing.

## About
Conway Corp has operated from Conway, Arkansas since 1929, when city leaders signed a charter on May 6 to create the Conway Corporation. The corporation provides electric, water, wastewater, video, internet, voice, and security services to residential and business customers across the Conway area. Its infrastructure includes the Stone Dam Creek Wastewater Treatment Plant, the James H. Crews operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, and the company launched one of the earliest broadband cable internet services in the country in 1997.

Customers range from individual households to local businesses and the broader Conway community. The utility serves the city's residential neighborhoods, commercial districts, and a designated Data District where gigabit internet was first rolled out to businesses. It also supports economic development initiatives and education programs as part of its community mandate.

The company states it was founded on a commitment to the community where its employees live and work. Its core values — customer satisfaction, innovation, safety, reliability, responsibility, and community — are framed as the reason it exists, with a focus on delivering safe, affordable, and environmentally sound services while maintaining fiscal stewardship.
