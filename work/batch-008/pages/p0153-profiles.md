The company draws together teams of project managers, engineers, and production and field service managers dedicated to sustainable solutions.

The firm produces ARMORFORM® fabric-formed concrete systems, which use double-layer woven geo-textiles engineered exclusively as forms for casting concrete erosion control revetments and linings. Installation involves positioning the fabric forms and pumping high-strength, fine-aggregate concrete, either underwater or on dry land. By 2002, more than 50 million square feet of ARMORFORM® had been installed worldwide, including projects inside the Arctic Circle and at the Olmsted Locks and Dam on the Ohio River.
