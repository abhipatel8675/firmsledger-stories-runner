## About
Barth Electronics, Inc. has operated from Boulder City, Nevada since 1976, after originating in Ohio in 1964. The company was founded to design and manufacture specialized test hardware for weapons programs at the Nevada Test Site, serving clients such as EG&G and national laboratories.

Customers span the semiconductor industry, physics and pulse‑power research laboratories, and government research facilities. The company traces its commercial ESD line to a 1997 breakthrough — the first commercial Transmission Line Pulser — which adapted weapons‑test hardware for semiconductor reliability. Management says the high‑reliability culture from defense programs carries over into every instrument, and that ongoing R&D targets emerging ESD and high‑voltage pulse‑power needs.
