# Barth Electronics

**Old description:** Please contact Barth Electronics, Inc. for general company inquiries and technical information on Pulse Power products or ESD test equipment. Complete our contact form with your information, requests and feedback.

## About
Barth Electronics, Inc. has been headquartered in Boulder City, Nevada since 1976, when the company relocated from Ohio to support weapons testing at the Nevada Test Site. Founded in 1964, the firm began by designing and manufacturing specialized test hardware for EG&G in Las Vegas and for national laboratories.

The company designs and manufactures sub-nanosecond high-energy pulse power instrumentation, including over 100 distinct pulse power products. In 1997 its engineers developed the first commercial Transmission Line Pulser (TLP) for the electrostatic discharge (ESD) industry. Today the product line encompasses the Model 4002 TLP+ for human-body-model testing, the Model 4012 VFTLP+ for charged-device-model testing, the Model 4702 HMM+ for IEC-equivalent gun discharge testing, and a 700-series of 3 kV and 5 kV pulse generators. All ESD testing services are performed in-house at the Boulder City factory, and a rental program is offered for the Model 4002 TLP+.

Barth serves the semiconductor industry with ESD device characterization and qualification testing, while its pulse power instruments are used daily in physics and pulse-power laboratories worldwide. Historical customers include EG&G and U.S. national laboratories involved in weapons testing.

The company traces its high-reliability standards to its weapons-testing heritage: “By beating our swords into plowshares, weapons testing hardware was changed into the development and manufacture of the highest quality ESD test equipment available today.” It continues to investigate new technologies for ESD and high-voltage pulse power as needs emerge.

**Old description:** Base Power is an Austin, Texas-based company that designs, assembles, and deploys home battery backup systems while operating as a retail electric provider in Texas.

## About
Base Power Company was founded in 2023 in Austin, Texas, by CEO Zach Dell and co-founder Justin Lopas. Lopas previously led manufacturing at Anduril and worked on rockets at SpaceX, while the broader team brings experience from satellite constellation launches and early electric vehicle production. The company was born after Winter Storm Uri left millions of Texans without power for days, highlighting the grid’s fragility and rising electricity costs.

Base designs, assembles, and deploys its Core home battery backup system — a 39.2 kWh unit that provides 36 to 72 hours of whole-home backup — and operates as a retail electric provider in Texas and Illinois. The company handles battery pack assembly, in-house engineering, UL-certified testing, and runs a virtual power plant that dispatches stored energy to the grid during peak demand.

The primary customers are residential homeowners in Texas and Illinois seeking affordable backup power and fixed-rate electricity. The company also serves grid-storage and utility markets by aggregating its distributed batteries to provide grid-balancing services. Testimonials cite installations in Houston, Waxahachie, Cypress, Arlington, Saginaw, and Pflugerville.

Base positions its model as a low-cost alternative to traditional generators or solar-plus-storage, installing the battery for a $695 one-time fee plus a $19 monthly membership, with an all-in energy rate around 13.9¢/kWh. The battery is tested for -22°F to 122°F and submersion-rated to three feet, and the company says it earns revenue from grid services rather than marking up energy, allowing it to keep rates low.
