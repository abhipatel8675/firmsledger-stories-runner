## About
Creative Hi-Tech Ltd. (CHTL) operates a 25,000-square-foot facility in Schaumburg, Illinois. The company was founded in 1998, beginning with a single pick-and-place machine in a 7,000-square-foot building. Over the following decades it expanded to two production lines equipped for both surface-mount and through-hole technology, adding advanced inspection and test equipment.

The shop provides full-service printed circuit board assembly, from quick-turn prototypes to high-volume production runs. Capabilities include SMT, PTH, and mixed-technology assembly; BGA and micro-BGA placement down to 0201 pitch; lead-free and RoHS-compliant processes; box-build and system integration; wire harness and cable assembly; and rework and repair. In-house testing covers AOI, X-ray, and flying-probe inspection. The facility also supports PCB design, fabrication, and turnkey procurement.

Customers span aerospace, military, medical, and electronic industrial products, as well as agricultural, automotive, pharmaceutical, wireless, telecommunication, defense, satellite, industrial electronics, computer, and business electronics sectors. Named references include Honeywell and several LED lighting manufacturers.

The company emphasizes flexibility, taking orders as small as a single board, and promotes a secure, cloud-based workflow for BOM upload and digital build-data validation. ITAR registration and compliance with UL, CSA, FDA, and IPC standards are cited as differentiators, along with rapid lead times ranging from one day for quick-turn work to four to eight weeks for standard production.

# Cristek Interconnects

**Old description:** Connectors and wire harness/cable assemblies for aerospace and defense

## About
Cristek Interconnects was founded in 1985 in Anaheim, California, by 23-year-old Cristi Cristich. Armed with a $25,000 contract from Litton, a fistful of credit cards, and a small loan from her first boss, she launched the company to serve the military and aerospace industries. Today, the business operates as a Qnnect Company with manufacturing facilities spanning Southern California, New England, and Northern Nevada.

The company designs and manufactures micro-miniature and nano-miniature connectors, complex cable and harness assemblies, twist capsules, precision mechanisms, hermetic assemblies, RF/microwave components, and machined parts. Its vertically integrated operation covers DC through 67 GHz RF frequency connector, cable, and electromechanical design and assembly. Cristek employs over 100 people across more than 56,000 square feet of production space.

Cristek serves aerospace, defense, and space programs, supplying prime contractors and other customers with mission-critical interconnects for harsh environments including vibration, temperature extremes, and vacuum. Its products are used in applications where failure is not an option, such as satellite constellations, laser communication terminals, and other high-reliability platforms.

Cristek differentiates itself through vertical integration, engineering expertise in nanominiature and microminiature connector technology, and the ability to add EMI filtering and hermetic sealing to custom high-speed, matched-impedance solutions. The company emphasizes process discipline, metric-based management, and rapid prototyping to accelerate development cycles while maintaining rigorous standards.
