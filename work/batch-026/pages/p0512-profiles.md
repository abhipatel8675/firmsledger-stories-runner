# Cold River Furniture

**Old description:** in 2019 and 2020 at the annual League of NH Craftsmen’s Fair, the oldest and largest craft show in the country.

## About
Cold River Furniture operates from South Acworth, New Hampshire, where master furniture maker Peter Maynard established his workshop in 1975 after completing an apprenticeship at the George Whitmore Furniture Company in Middletown, Connecticut. Maynard, who began his training at age 18 under second- and third-generation Connecticut River Valley craftsmen, moved to the upper Connecticut River Valley to start his own company. His wife, Marcie, has been integral to the business since its inception, handling communications and project coordination.

The studio produces original, handcrafted fine furniture using select North American hardwoods. Maynard combines antique water-powered machinery — including a 19th-century tenoning machine — with modern tools and extensive handwork. Joinery relies on full mortise-and-tenon and hand-cut dovetails, supplemented by Chinese and Japanese woodworking techniques. Each piece undergoes multiple sanding rounds, three coats of stain, and six coats of thin tung oil varnish. The workshop also handles log procurement, sawing flitch-matched lumber, and careful drying. Output is limited to signed, one-of-a-kind pieces shipped across North America.

Clients include private collectors, interior designers, and institutions such as the Metropolitan Museum of Art, which has commissioned work. The business serves the high-end residential and collector markets.

Maynard emphasizes a connection to the New England landscape and a commitment to integrity in craft. He describes the work as a deliberate alternative to mass production, where the mark of the human hand is central. The company's philosophy centers on creating heirloom pieces that gain value over time, rooted in a lifestyle of simplicity and focus.

CPE manufactures high-voltage power supplies with low stored energy, solid-state pulsers, TWT solid-state modulators, and ion thrusters including ion engines and Hall thrusters. The firm also offers custom analog and mixed-signal design solutions. In-house capabilities cover PCB design and layout, PCB assembly, system assembly, test and calibration verification, and a fully equipped R&D laboratory. The company emphasizes customized solutions tailored to specific application requirements.

CPE’s products are used by manufacturers of military and space TWTA test stations and ion implanters. The company also serves the thin-film industry, where its high-power DC and pulser products are described as the market leader for both U.S. and European sales. CPE differentiates itself through its focus on customized high-voltage solutions, supported by an in-house R&D and service laboratory.
