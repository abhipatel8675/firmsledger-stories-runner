# Columbia Packaging Group

**Old description:** Custom printed bags and rollstock for brands that demand performance

## About
Columbia Packaging Group is headquartered in Kansas City, Missouri. The company was established in 2017 with a strategy to acquire and consolidate regional flexible packaging manufacturers, beginning with M&M Printed Bag and Packaging Materials Inc. (PMI). In 2020 it launched Biolo, a proprietary line of certified compostable packaging.

The company operates as a full-service flexible packaging manufacturer with in-house extrusion, flexographic printing, and converting capabilities. It runs production facilities in California and Ohio; the California site supports up to 60-inch web width and 8-color registered printing, while the Ohio site offers 6-color inline printing on webs from 3 to 47 inches. Capabilities include custom film extrusion from raw resin, slitting up to 10 rolls across, and precision bag making with numerous style options such as wicketed, zip-lock, and tamper-evident configurations.

Columbia Packaging Group serves a range of industries including fresh produce, food service, retail, agriculture, industrial and automotive, and towel and tissue. Its packaging solutions are used for clear produce bags, food-safe compostable straws, high-graphic retail bags, UV-resistant agricultural films, heavy-duty industrial part protection, and high-speed overwrap film for tissue lines.

The company emphasizes its differentiation through certified compostability (TÜV home and industrial), food-safety compliance (BRC, AIB, BPI), and operational reliability from resin to finished rollstock. The Biolo brand was created to deliver sustainable packaging that integrates with existing converting lines without sacrificing performance.
