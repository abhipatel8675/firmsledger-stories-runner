## About
Component Packaging operates from an approximately 30,000-square-foot facility in Joplin, Missouri. The company was purchased in 2006 by Mike and Lori Magoulas, who brought more than 15 years of packaging industry experience to build a specialized service around fastener kitting, assembly, and inspection. Their son, Nick Magoulas, joined after military service and a management career at a Fortune 500 automotive firm, and now helps lead the business as a generational, family-owned operation.

The company provides fastener and component packaging services including kitting, nut-and-bolt assembly, master-kit creation, quality inspection for rust, mixed parts, thread defects, and contamination, as well as sorting, rework, relabeling, and repackaging. Additional capabilities cover receiving and storage, boxing and box-down work, heat-shrink sleeving, piecework, and hand packaging for jobs that don’t fit automated runs. Component Packaging primarily serves fastener distributors and industrial customers across 36 states, helping them add packaging capacity without investing in equipment or staff. Distributors send customer-owned inventory for custom kits, assembly, inspection, or rework, and the company coordinates shipping from its central location near major interstate routes.

The company emphasizes direct accountability—customers reach an owner or manager when exceptions arise—and a “whatever it takes” mindset that matches the method to the job, whether automated for repeatable volume or hand-packaged for complex combinations.
