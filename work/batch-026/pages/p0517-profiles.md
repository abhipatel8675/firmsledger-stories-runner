## About
Crown Wire & Cable Co. Inc. was founded in 1986 in Millville, New Jersey, located in the Millville Airport complex about 30 miles south of Philadelphia. The company began as a supplier to the oil, gas, and water industries and has grown into one of the largest stocking distributors of steel wire rope and submersible pump cable in the United States.

The company stocks a broad range of products including submersible pump cable in multiple constructions (flat jacketed, neoprene, PVC twisted, flat parallel, round double jacketed), steel wire rope in various configurations (6x7 sand line, 6x21 drilling line, 19x7 rotation resistant, 6x36 winch cable, 6x25 casing/tubing line, 6x19 rotary drill line), galvanized and Type 304 stainless steel aircraft cable, and manila rope. It also sources related electrical products such as URD, SER, overhead, triplex, quadraplex, and irrigation cables on request. Crown Wire ships nationwide and serves international markets in Central America, South America, and the Caribbean.

Primary customers operate in the oil and gas, water well, and electrical utility sectors. The company’s technical staff assists with product selection for applications ranging from submersible pump installations to drilling and winching operations. Crown Wire emphasizes 24/7 availability, with dedicated personnel reachable around the clock to handle inquiries and track shipments.

Crown Wire’s stated mission is to build long-term relationships through enhanced service, clear shipping communications, and superior products. The founders aimed to provide a pleasant, polite experience regardless of order size, a principle that continues to guide the company’s customer‑service approach.

## About
Crystal Farms Dairy Co. is headquartered in Edina, Minnesota. The company's roots trace to the 1920s when Nathan Friedell founded Nathan's Produce Company, and to the 1930s when Julius Rosenberg started an egg business after immigrating from Russia. The two operations merged in the 1970s, creating the Crystal Farms brand that sells cheese and other dairy products today.

The company produces a wide range of dairy items including shredded, block, sliced, and snack cheeses, ricotta, specialty cheeses, cream cheese, butter, English muffins, and bagels. Crystal Farms states that it sources its dairy from the Midwest and emphasizes a quality process that runs from ingredient selection through finished‑product inspection.

Crystal Farms primarily serves consumer retail and foodservice channels. The brand also engages with community partners: it supports the national FFA organization and its Minnesota, Wisconsin, and Iowa chapters; donates products quarterly to the Ronald McDonald House; and supplies nearly all cheese and butter for All Square, a Minneapolis non‑profit grilled‑cheese shop that employs formerly incarcerated individuals.

The company says its Midwestern dairy heritage and a culture of commitment — from high‑quality ingredients to careful final inspection — are what set it apart, allowing customers to "cheese like a Midwesterner."
