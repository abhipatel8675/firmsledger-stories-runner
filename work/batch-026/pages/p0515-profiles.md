**Old description:** CoolCAD Electronics is an innovative company that designs and fabricates semiconductor devices and chips made from wide bandgap semiconductor Silicon Carbide (SiC).

## About
CoolCAD Electronics operates from Greenbelt, Maryland, where it designs and fabricates semiconductor devices and chips from wide-bandgap silicon carbide (SiC). The company's founding year and ownership structure are not publicly disclosed.

The firm produces SiC MOSFETs, diodes, high-temperature integrated circuits, and deep-ultraviolet optical sensors. Its proprietary SiC formulations and manufacturing processes enable devices that operate continuously at temperatures exceeding 400 °C, well above the 200 °C limit of conventional silicon chips. CoolCAD also offers research and development services, computer-aided design and thermal simulation through its CoolSPICE software, cryogenic and radiation testing for space and nuclear applications, and engineering support for transitioning existing silicon-based power devices to SiC.

Customers span aerospace, automotive, data communications, green energy, military and defense, power generation, and water purification sectors. The company's high-temperature ICs and UV sensors are positioned for extreme-environment applications such as deep-space exploration, nuclear energy monitoring, and daylight UV imaging without optical filters.

CoolCAD holds multiple U.S. patents covering wide-bandgap SiC semiconductor advances. The technology was developed to accelerate the shift from fossil-fuel infrastructure toward renewable energy systems by delivering higher voltage, higher power, and smaller, lighter electronic systems.
