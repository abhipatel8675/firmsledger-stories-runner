# Cleveland Yutaka Corporation

**Old description:** Cleveland Yutaka Corporation is a precision machining manufacturer of the automotive industry and a subsidiary of Yutaka Industry Co., LTD.

## About
Cleveland Yutaka Corporation operates in Shelby, North Carolina, as a subsidiary of Japan's Yutaka Industry Co., Ltd. The company was incorporated in June 1995 and completed its initial 56,788-square-foot facility in February 1996, launching production with 20 employees. A 2015 expansion added 52,552 square feet, bringing the plant to 109,340 square feet.

The shop specializes in high-volume, high-precision machined components using CNC machining, centerless grinding, and centered grinding. It holds diameter tolerances to 0.008 mm (8 µm) and surface finishes to 3.2 Rz, with standard capability targets above 3σ and special-characteristic targets above 4σ. Materials processed include aluminum, hardened and non-hardened steel, stainless steel, and cast iron. In-line and off-line visual and dimensional inspection, backed by proprietary and outsourced equipment, enables 100% nonconformity detection.

CYC serves the automotive sector, supplying components for fuel pumps, blower fans, starters, alternators, power-window drive shafts, solenoids, and suspension systems. Its customer base includes Toyota, Ford, General Motors, Chrysler, Hyundai, Honda, and other major OEMs.

The company states its goals as zero failed deliveries, zero late deliveries, and 100% quality. It emphasizes continuous improvement, employee development, and environmental responsibility through waste reduction, regulatory compliance, and safe chemical management.
