## About
Cambero Metal Works was founded in 2003 by Virginia and Angel Cambero in a garage in San Gabriel, California. The family-owned and operated shop began with a set of tools and a commitment to craftsmanship, growing into a full-service custom fabrication facility serving clients across Southern California.

The company provides custom metal fabrication including precision CNC laser cutting, TIG/MIG/stick welding, sheet metal forming, rolling, and stainless steel finishing. The shop combines hand-sketching and CAD/Inventor design with modern fabrication technology.

Cambero serves industrial, commercial, and residential sectors, with specific experience in transit, rail, and government projects. Its client base includes architects, contractors, designers, restaurant owners, and kitchen designers throughout Southern California. Projects range from commercial kitchen equipment and ventilation systems to structural installations and ornamental metalwork.

The founders emphasize honest craftsmanship, personal attention, and a quality standard unchanged over two decades. The company states that its differentiator lies in treating each project with the same care as its own business, blending old-world craftsmanship with modern tools to deliver work that holds up long after completion.

**Old description:** Camgian was founded in Starkville, Mississippi, establishing its roots in advanced technology and defense innovation.

## About
Camgian was founded in 2006 in Starkville, Mississippi, by Dr. Gary Butler, establishing its roots in advanced technology and defense innovation. The company began as a venture focused on artificial intelligence and machine learning for high-stakes environments.

Today, Camgian develops AI-enabled kill chain automation software, most notably its REACTOR® platform, which provides cognitive decision aids for air and missile defense, counter-unmanned aircraft systems, and space defense. The software is hardware- and command-and-control-agnostic, built on a modular, open architecture that leverages reinforcement learning, multi-modal sensor fusion, and real-time sensor cueing. The firm operates from its Mississippi headquarters and additional offices in Tuscaloosa and Lawton, Oklahoma, with a new facility opened in Huntsville, Alabama in 2025.

Camgian serves primarily U.S. Department of Defense organizations, including the Missile Defense Agency and the U.S. Army. In 2024 it secured a $55 million contract to support the Army's Integrated Air and Missile Defense systems, and it holds a position on the MDA's $151 billion SHIELD IDIQ contract vehicle. Its solutions are also offered to select non-DoD government entities and commercial partners.

The company states its mission is to accelerate decision-making in high-stakes environments by reducing cognitive overload and delivering precision at the point of decision. Its stated differentiators include platform-agnostic deployment, open-architecture scalability, and a culture rooted in radical accountability and a "No Quit" mindset.
