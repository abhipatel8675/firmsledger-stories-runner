# City Hardwoods

**Old description:** In an industry filled with middlemen, our remote sawmill operation gives us the ability to supplement our wood room inventory with local hardwoods that have a unique story.

## About
City Hardwoods operates from Birmingham, Alabama, where it runs a sawmill, kiln-drying facility, and showroom The business was acquired in June 2022 by Caleb Gilbert, who runs the family-owned small business with his father. Gilbert’s path to woodworking began during his senior year at LSU when a shoulder injury sidelined him from baseball; he filled the time with weekend shop projects and soon shared the passion with his father, a lifelong hobbyist. Together they started milling and drying hardwood lumber for their own custom furniture projects.

The company’s remote sawmill salvages hundreds of urban logs each year from local tree services, diverting material that would otherwise become mulch. Logs are milled, stacked, and air-dried before finishing in one of two kilns—a dehumidification kiln and a vacuum kiln—that produce sterilized, kiln-dried lumber. Every board and slab is then surfaced on a planer or flattened on a slab CNC, a step that lets customers see grain, character, and color in the showroom. City Hardwoods builds custom furniture and retails the kiln-dried hardwood lumber, but does not offer milling or drying services to the public.

The primary customers are woodworkers, furniture buyers, and the broader Birmingham community seeking local hardwoods. The shop supplies lumber for custom furniture projects and sells boards directly to hobbyists and professionals through its retail showroom.

Gilbert says the operation was born from a desire to cut out middlemen and give each log a unique story. The dual-kiln setup ensures lumber is free of pests, and the slab CNC provides a showroom-ready surface that differentiates the buying experience. The acquisition of the existing City Hardwoods retailer in 2022 gave the family a platform to scale their sawmill output into a combined manufacturing and retail operation.
