## About
Bryce Corporation operates from Memphis, Tennessee, and Searcy, Arkansas, with its warehouse and manufacturing presence in Searcy. The company traces its origins to 1922 when William H. Bryce Sr. developed waxing technology at the Dixie Company in Dallas, Texas. He founded Dixie Wax Paper in Memphis in 1927, and in 1969 William H. Bryce Jr. established Bryce Corporation to convert polypropylene films for the snack food market. The business remains family-owned and operated, now in its fourth generation under Chairman and CEO Thomas J. Bryce, who took the helm in 1997, alongside his sons-in-law Sean Bowie and Stephen Perkins.

The company specializes in film conversion and flexible packaging production, offering flexographic printing, lamination, slitting, perforation, pouch making, and logistics. Through affiliate Cyber Graphics it provides design, prepress, and plate-making services, while minority-owned partner Johnson Bryce adds waxing and coating capabilities. Facilities in the Southeast support large-volume runs with automated quality systems including statistical process control, 100% web detection, and a closed-loop quality system. The operation is Safe Quality Food (SQF) certified.

Bryce serves the food, snack, pet care, household, and health and beauty sectors, with specific markets including salty snacks, confectionery, pet food, bakery, frozen foods, and personal care. Its packaging protects products for nationally recognized brands across these categories.

Founded on a legacy of technological innovation — from pioneering polypropylene film conversion to extended-gamut flexographic printing — the company emphasizes sustainable packaging solutions, generational commitment to employees and community, and responsiveness through its affiliate network.
