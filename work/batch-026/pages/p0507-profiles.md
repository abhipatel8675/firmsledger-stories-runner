## About
Caroba Plastics Inc is headquartered in Englewood, Colorado, in the Denver metropolitan area. The company was founded in 1983 by Barry Hart as a small, family-owned operation with two injection molding machines and a vision to provide high-quality plastic parts. Hart remains the owner and drives a hands-on philosophy emphasizing clean, organized facilities, systematic processes, and personal attention.

Today Caroba operates a fleet of 30 Nissei high-precision injection molding presses ranging from 20 to 750 tons, supported by in-house tooling design, maintenance, and repair. The facility includes an ISO Class 7 hard-wall cleanroom for medical molding and contract assembly, over 60,000 square feet of warehousing with lot and location control, and secondary operations such as assembly, packaging, and automated part removal. The company employs over 150 people and offers DFM assistance, mold validation, and ship-to-stock programs.

Caroba serves the medical, dental, high-technology, semiconductor, and consumer industries. It holds certified "dock-to-stock" status for medical OEMs and semiconductor customers, and its quality system supports IQ/OQ/PQ validation, measurement system analysis, and process capability studies. The management team brings over 100 years of combined thermoplastic experience.

The company's differentiation stems from its founding principles: a clean, organized plant; systematic processes; honesty; and hard work. Project teams of engineers, toolmakers, and polymer experts engage from design inception to reduce errors and cost. Caroba has maintained ISO 9001:2015 certification since 1998 and operates with a "do it right the first time" attitude.
