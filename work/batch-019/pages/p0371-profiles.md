Operations span manufacturing facilities on three continents, including a 640,000-square-foot plant in Lemoore, California, with over 1,100 employees, and a new $1 billion facility in Lubbock, Texas. Leprino supplies food and nutrition brands worldwide, including major pizza chains, with products sold in more than 70 countries. A strategic alliance with Dairy Farmers of America secures milk supply, and joint ventures in Europe, Latin America, and Asia extend its reach.

# Liberate Medical

**Old description:** VentFree contracts the expiratory muscles during mechanical ventilation to keep them active.

## About
Liberate Medical is based in Crestwood, Kentucky. The company develops the VentFree Respiratory Muscle Stimulator, a medical device designed to address respiratory muscle atrophy in mechanically ventilated patients.

The VentFree system uses proprietary non-invasive neuromuscular electrical stimulation to contract the abdominal wall muscles in synchrony with exhalation during mechanical ventilation. Therapy can begin from day one of ventilation while patients are sedated or delirious, and the device is described as quick and simple to use. No facility size, headcount, or production volumes are disclosed.

The company serves the critical care and respiratory care segments of the healthcare industry. Its customers include hospitals and ICU teams managing mechanically ventilated patients, particularly those requiring more than four days of ventilation who face high rates of weaning failure and post-discharge complications.

Liberate Medical states that its approach may reduce abdominal muscle atrophy and shorten the duration of mechanical ventilation. A pilot randomized controlled trial found that ventilation duration and ICU length of stay were shorter in patients receiving VentFree stimulation compared to placebo, potentially reducing morbidity, mortality, and healthcare costs.
