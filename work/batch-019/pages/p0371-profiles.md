**Old description:** We started in 1950 as a small family-run grocery store in Denver, Colorado. Today, Leprino is proud to be the world’s largest producer of mozzarella cheese and a leading manufacturer of whey protein, lactose, micellar casein and other high-quality dairy ingredients.

## About
Leprino Foods Company began in 1950 as a small family-run grocery store in Denver, Colorado, when Mike Leprino Sr. started making mozzarella cheese by hand in a back room for his family's corner market. The business incorporated as Leprino Cheese Manufacturing Company in the 1960s and adopted the Leprino Foods Company name in the 1970s, remaining family-owned throughout its growth.

Today the company is the world's largest producer of mozzarella cheese and a manufacturer of whey protein, lactose, micellar casein, and other dairy ingredients. Its proprietary processes include the patented "Same Day Dice" method that turns fresh milk into cheese in four hours, and Quality-Locked Cheese that is individually quick-frozen to preserve peak flavor. Operations span manufacturing facilities on three continents, including a 640,000-square-foot plant in Lemoore, California, with over 1,100 employees, and a global headcount exceeding 5,000. Products reach more than 70 countries.

Leprino serves food and nutrition brands worldwide, from major pizza chains and foodservice operators to consumer-packaged-goods manufacturers. A strategic alliance with Dairy Farmers of America secures milk supply, while joint ventures in Europe, Latin America, and Asia Pacific extend its reach. The company also entered the direct-to-consumer market with the Ascent® Protein brand.

The Leprino Difference—rooted in the founder's credo "Leprino Quality is a way of life"—emphasizes quality, service, competitive price, and ethics. The company's stated purpose is to sustainably feed and nourish families globally, guided by a climate-action commitment targeting greenhouse-gas neutrality by 2050.

# Liberate Medical

**Old description:** VentFree contracts the expiratory muscles during mechanical ventilation to keep them active.

## About
Liberate Medical is based in Crestwood, Kentucky. The company develops and manufactures the VentFree Respiratory Muscle Stimulator, a non-invasive neuromuscular electrical stimulation device designed to contract the abdominal wall muscles in synchrony with exhalation during mechanical ventilation.

The VentFree system uses proprietary stimulation technology that can be initiated from the first day of mechanical ventilation, even while patients are sedated or delirious, without requiring patient cooperation. The device is CE marked for sale throughout the European Economic Area and holds U.S. FDA Emergency Use Authorization to reduce disuse atrophy of the abdominal wall muscles, which may shorten the duration of mechanical ventilation in adult patients, including those with COVID-19.

Liberate Medical serves the critical care and respiratory care markets, targeting hospitals and intensive care units that manage mechanically ventilated patients. Clinical evidence from a pilot randomized controlled trial published in Critical Care (2019) demonstrated that patients receiving VentFree stimulation experienced shorter mechanical ventilation duration and reduced ICU length of stay compared to placebo.

The company positions its technology as a breakthrough approach to preparing patients for weaning from day one of ventilation, aiming to reduce abdominal muscle atrophy—a factor associated with weaning failure and delayed extubation—and potentially lower morbidity, mortality, and healthcare costs.
