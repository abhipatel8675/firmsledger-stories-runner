**Old description:** Majors Plastics is a leading provider of precision injection molding solutions, offering high and medium volume production of tightly-toleranced components with exacting quality standards, supported by decades of technical expertise and state-of-the-art manufacturing infrastructure.

## About
Majors Plastics Inc. was founded in 1953 by Ben Pfeiffer and Eli Dahir in Omaha, Nebraska. The company relocated to its current Plant One location in 1968 and became a 100% employee-owned ESOP in 2013 after expanding the plan from its initial adoption in 1993.

The company operates four molding facilities across the United States and Mexico, specializing in precision injection molding, overmolding, insert molding, two-shot molding, and ISO Class-7 cleanroom molding. Its Omaha campus spans over 300,000 square feet and houses more than 120 molding machines, while the Reynosa, Mexico facility adds 128,000 square feet with 36 machines. Approximately 500 employees support high- and medium-volume production, complemented by assembly and secondary services such as ultrasonic welding, heat staking, pad printing, and custom packaging.

Majors Plastics serves pharmaceutical, medical device, health and safety, energy, consumer electronics, and tableware markets. Longstanding customers include 3M, which has recognized the company multiple times with its Supplier of the Year Award, and Becton Dickinson (BD), which named it a Supply Chain Excellence Supplier of the Year. The firm secured its first medical products customer in 1985 and has since diversified into energy-related components and rigid computer disc preparatory operations.

The company emphasizes personalized customer relationships from initial design concept through finished product, providing engineering support throughout mold design and manufacturing. Its quality system is built on ISO 9001:2015 registration, statistical process control, and C=0 sampling plans to meet customer AQL levels as low as 0.040.
