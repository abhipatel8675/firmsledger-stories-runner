## About
Mark One Metal Works was founded in 1996 in Sparks, Nevada, by Mark, a fabricator whose career began in his teens as a transmission mechanic under his father John. After managing the fabrication department at a Peterbilt dealership in Reno, he launched Levco Transmission in 1991 and ran it for 15 years before selling to pursue off-road racing. He then led production at Torchmate, a CNC plasma cutting manufacturer, scaling its output significantly. That blend of machining, fabrication, CAD design, and artistic sensibility led him to establish Mark One Metal Works as a custom metal fabrication and design shop serving the Reno–Sparks area and beyond.

The shop provides a wide range of metalworking services: machining, welding (including TIG), tube bending, CNC plasma cutting on a Lincoln Electric table, and 2D/3D CAD design. It handles both one-off prototypes and production runs, producing items that span industrial fabrications, commercial architectural elements (railings, gates, stairs), residential furniture (desks, tables, doors, fixtures), off-road and hot-rod components, and custom signs, trophies, and awards.

Customers come from commercial construction, residential design, industrial manufacturing, off-road racing, hot-rod building, and the awards/recognition market. The company has fabricated everything from stair railings for commercial buildings to roll cages for UTVs and custom metal furniture for private homes.

Mark One emphasizes exceptional design, unmatched quality, and great customer service, positioning its “unique metal working concepts” and over 30 years of experience as differentiators. The founder’s rare balance of machining, fabrication, CAD, and artistic ability is cited as the reason the shop can take a project from concept to finished product in-house.
