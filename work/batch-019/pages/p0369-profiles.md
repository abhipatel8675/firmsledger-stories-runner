# Lakeside Trading, Sheds & Outdoor Furniture

**Old description:** None

## About
Lakeside Trading LLC has operated from Narragansett, Rhode Island since 1999, when founder Dave Harrigan liquidated his 401(k) to purchase a trailer load of hand-made clay chimineas from Mexico. The family-owned business began as a wholesale and seasonal market venture before Harrigan encountered Pennsylvania shed builders during East Coast buying trips. Recognizing a gap in the Rhode Island market, he acquired a shed-moving trailer and leased a former 19th-century fishing equipment warehouse on South Pier Road, establishing a permanent location that he purchased outright in 2010.

Today the company maintains a 5,000-square-foot showroom and an expansive display yard staffed by 20 full-time employees, several with more than 15 years of tenure. Lakeside Trading does not manufacture its own products; instead it procures, installs, and services American-made sheds, greenhouses, chicken coops, gazebos, garages, pergolas, playsets, and pavilions, along with outdoor furniture crafted from recycled plastic poly lumber and marine-grade hardware. The team installs more than 1,000 structures and sells over 2,000 furniture pieces each year, and also provides site preparation such as gravel or stone pads, rock pads, and shed relocation services.

The primary customer base is residential homeowners throughout Rhode Island seeking durable outdoor storage and living solutions. Projects range from simple garden sheds and Adirondack chairs to custom-designed pavilions, greenhouses converted into art studios, and chicken coops with epoxy floors and automatic doors. The company also handles used-shed purchases, moving and reinstalling them on prepared foundations.

Lakeside Trading differentiates itself through Amish-crafted structures backed by a 10-year warranty and a lifetime service guarantee, free on-site consultations before purchase, and a commitment to fair pricing. The business remains locally owned and operated, emphasizing personal service from sales through installation.
