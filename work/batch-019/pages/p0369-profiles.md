**Old description:** None

## About
Lakeside Trading LLC has operated from Narragansett, Rhode Island since 1999, when founder Dave Harrigan liquidated his 401(k) and life savings to purchase a trailer load of hand-made clay chimineas from Mexico. The family-owned business began wholesaling those chimineas to local garden centers and selling at seasonal markets before expanding into outdoor structures.

Today the company procures, installs, and services American-made sheds, greenhouses, chicken coops, gazebos, garages, pergolas, playsets, and pavilions from a 5,000-square-foot showroom and display yard A staff of 20 full-time employees — some with more than 15 years’ tenure — handles custom design, site preparation, delivery, and installation, completing over 1,000 structure installations and selling more than 2,000 pieces of furniture annually.

Lakeside serves residential customers throughout Rhode Island, offering both stock and customizable options. Its outdoor furniture line features marine-grade hardware and recycled plastic poly lumber backed by a 20-year workmanship warranty, while Amish-crafted structures carry a 10-year warranty and a lifetime service guarantee. The company also provides shed relocation, foundation preparation, and free on-site consultations.

Harrigan’s original leap of faith established a culture of fair pricing and personal service that remains the company’s stated differentiator: a locally rooted, family-run operation that stands behind every product it sells.
