EnaChip Inc. operates from Rocky Hill, New Jersey, where it develops high-frequency magnetic components for vertical power delivery in AI data centers. The company designs and fabricates bespoke magnetics that enable integrated voltage regulator (IVR) and transverse inductor voltage regulator (TLVR) topologies. Its core technology relies on an electroplated core stack with thick copper planar windings and high-flux-density magnetic materials, all processed on standard semiconductor equipment. The resulting components achieve low DC and AC resistance, support switching frequencies above 10 MHz, and fit within sub-millimeter, wafer-level packages that can be placed at the board, package, or die level.

EnaChip serves operators and hardware builders in AI data centers, high-performance computing, and power electronics, particularly those adopting 800 V DC architectures. The company also lists energy harvesting and energy conversion among its served markets.

EnaChip states that its differentiation lies in co-designing power, signal, sensing, and actuation functions rather than stitching together disconnected parts. By electroplating on standard semiconductor lines, the company says it dramatically reduces manufacturing time while delivering magnetics that suppress core loss at high frequencies, enabling low-inductance designs that drop into any level of the design stack.
