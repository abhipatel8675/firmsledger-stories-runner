The organization does not manufacture products but instead provides information, education, outreach programs, and special events covering topics such as enhanced geothermal systems, renewable fuels, sustainable development, and energy transmission and storage technologies. The Corridor's office is housed in the National Energy Center of Excellence (NECE) building on the BSC campus, a facility dedicated in September 2008 that incorporates a ground-source geothermal heat pump system, LEED-compliant lighting, a total energy heat recovery system, and Flexcrete—a construction material made with fly ash from coal combustion.

The Corridor serves partners across government, education, and the private sector, facilitating collaboration to promote energy-related activities through federal funding and other sources. Its audience includes energy industry professionals, students, policymakers, and researchers interested in North Dakota's energy landscape. The annual EmPowerND Energy Conference and the North Dakota Energy Report are among its key outreach vehicles, though the 2023 conference was canceled.

The Corridor's distinguishing feature is its integration within a college that operates a LEED-certified, energy-efficient facility purpose-built for energy education, giving it direct access to training infrastructure and a mandate to connect diverse energy stakeholders across the state.
