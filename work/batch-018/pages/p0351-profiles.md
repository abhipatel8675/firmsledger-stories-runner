HTDC offers co-working space, educational programs, funding access, mentoring, and workforce development through initiatives like the Holiday Tech Job Fair. The agency reports securing $62 million in federal funding, assisting over 300 companies, and helping create more than 3,000 jobs.

HTDC serves technology startups, manufacturers, and innovation-driven businesses in Hawaii. The agency works with small and medium-sized manufacturers seeking productivity improvements, tech entrepreneurs needing incubation and capital, and energy researchers focused on hydrogen technology.
