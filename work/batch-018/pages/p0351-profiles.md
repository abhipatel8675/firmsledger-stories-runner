## About
Hawaii Technology Development Corporation (HTDC) is a state economic development agency headquartered in Honolulu, Hawaii. Created legislatively in 1983, it operates as an attached agency to the Department of Business, Economic Development & Tourism (DBEDT). The corporation was established to develop Hawaii's technology sector and diversify the state's economy.

HTDC does not manufacture products. Instead, it provides a range of services to foster innovation and support businesses across the islands. As Hawaii's Manufacturing Extension Partnership (MEP) center, its INNOVATE Hawaii program enhances productivity and technical performance of small and medium-sized manufacturers. The agency also operates the Hawaii Center for Advanced Transportation Technologies (HCATT), which has over 20 years of hydrogen and fuel cell research in partnership with the U.S. Air Force. HTDC offers co-working space, educational programs, funding opportunities, mentoring, and workforce development initiatives. It reports assisting over 300 companies and helping create more than 3,000 jobs, with $62 million in federal funding secured and $72 million in funding obtained.

HTDC serves technology startups, manufacturers, and energy companies across Hawaii. Its manufacturing assistance reaches small and medium-sized manufacturers statewide. The energy tech program focuses on hydrogen and fuel cell research for national and state needs. The agency also supports aerospace and ocean technology entrepreneurs, as highlighted in recent news about efforts to stem outmigration of young talent. Customers include startups seeking incubation, manufacturers needing technical guidance, and research organizations pursuing clean energy integration.

HTDC was created to address the need for a dedicated state entity to build a sustainable technology and innovation ecosystem. Its differentiating role is as a public-sector catalyst that connects businesses with capital, infrastructure, mentoring, and federal partnerships to accelerate commercialization and job creation in high-value sectors.
