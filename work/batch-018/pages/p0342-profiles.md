## About
Frugll was founded in 2017 in Salt Lake City, Utah, by co-founders Jordan Folsom and Josh Giddings. The company began with a mission to help homeowners maximize solar savings through a direct-to-consumer approach that eliminates sales commissions and marketing overhead.

The firm designs and installs residential photovoltaic systems, handling everything from initial consultation and system engineering to permitting, installation, and ongoing maintenance. Its proprietary software automates quoting and project management, allowing the team to pass cost savings directly to customers. To date, the company reports more than 1,500 completed installations and a network of over 190,000 installs, with an average customer savings of 52 percent compared with traditional installers.

Frugll serves individual homeowners across its service territories, focusing on the residential solar market. Its customers are typically property owners seeking to reduce electricity costs and achieve a faster return on investment, with payback periods cited between two and five years.

The company differentiates itself by removing sales commissions from the pricing structure, using custom automation to lower overhead, and including full-service maintenance for the life of the system. Leadership emphasizes a referral-driven culture and a commitment to educating customers about the economics of solar power.
