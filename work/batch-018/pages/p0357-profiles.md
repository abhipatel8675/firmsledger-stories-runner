## About
Incap Electronics US, Inc. operates from Coal Center, Pennsylvania, as the U.S. arm of Incap Corporation, a global electronics manufacturing services (EMS) provider listed on Nasdaq Helsinki since 1997. The company emphasizes long-term partnerships, with some customer relationships exceeding 20 years.

The company says its differentiation lies in direct communication, fast decision-making, and a proactive approach that keeps production running smoothly. As part of Incap's global network, the U.S. site offers access to international manufacturing capacity while maintaining a local, customer-driven model focused on process control, testing, and total cost of ownership over short-term cost optimization.
