Goose Woodworking, LLC operates from Leesburg, Virginia, where owner and lead craftsman Wayne runs a small business built around custom, handmade furniture. Clients are primarily homeowners and interior designers seeking pieces that echo styles seen at retailers like Wayfair, West Elm, or Restoration Hardware, or on platforms such as Pinterest and Instagram. The company does not work with pine or construction-grade lumber, positioning its output above mass-produced imports. Each project is a one-off collaboration, with the design phase allowing minor changes at no cost before the signed drawing locks in the build.

Wayne started the business to ensure that every item leaving the shop reflects a personal stake in quality, a principle summed up by the 'Army of One' motto. The combination of in-house milling, customer-approved CAD drawings, and stain sampling from project-specific off-cuts is intended to eliminate surprises and deliver furniture that matches the client's vision in both material and finish.
