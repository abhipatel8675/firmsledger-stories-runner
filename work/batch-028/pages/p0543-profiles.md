## About
Fuji Foods USA operates from Browns Summit, North Carolina, as the U.S. subsidiary of Fuji Foods Corporation, a Japanese manufacturer with over 80 years of experience producing soups, sauces, and gravies for Asian markets. The American arm was established to bring that global flavor expertise to domestic food manufacturers and restaurant groups.

The company develops and manufactures custom savory flavors, including concentrated meat bases, broths, yeast extracts, spray-dried powders, and ramen soup bases. Its USDA- and FDA-inspected facilities produce everything from frozen chicken bone broth and shelf-stable beef broth to high-nucleotide yeast extract (HIMAX IG20 DFP) and a library of pre-developed beef, pork, poultry, seafood, and vegetable flavors. Teams also create custom formulas tailored to a customer's budget and target profile.

Fuji Foods serves the global food industry, supplying food-service operators, industrial processors, and restaurant chains that need authentic Asian, American, and fusion profiles. Examples include ramen bases for restaurant groups, marinades and seasonings for entrée producers, and flavor enhancers for soup and sauce manufacturers.

The company differentiates itself by building all meat bases in-house to guarantee batch-to-batch consistency, and by formulating high-intensity flavors that lower usage rates—reducing shipping and storage costs for clients. Collaboration between its U.S. and Japanese flavor teams expands the palette of available profiles, while FSSC 22000, Kosher, USDA, and FDA credentials support regulatory compliance.
