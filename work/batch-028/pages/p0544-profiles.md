# GE Vernova

**Old description:** Manufactures power generation, grid, and electrical equipment including transformers and switchgear (spun off from GE).

## About
GE Vernova, headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, began operations in 2024 as a spin-off from General Electric to focus on the global energy transition. The company carries forward the GE legacy while adopting a new identity—“Ver” for green and “Nova” for new—signaling its commitment to lower-carbon energy.

The company designs and manufactures a broad portfolio of power generation, grid, and electrical equipment, including transformers, switchgear, wind turbines, and gas turbines. Its installed base comprises roughly 59,000 wind turbines and 7,000 gas turbines, which together help generate about 25% of the world’s electricity. GE Vernova employs approximately 85,000 people across more than 100 countries, with its global headquarters in Cambridge serving as a hub for engineering and innovation.

GE Vernova serves the power generation, electrical grid, renewable energy, and nuclear energy sectors. Its technologies are deployed in utility-scale wind farms, gas-fired power plants, grid stabilization projects—such as a custom engineering effort in Brazil—and small modular reactor development. Customers include utilities, independent power producers, and industrial operators seeking to decarbonize their energy supply.

The company’s stated mission is to “electrify to thrive and decarbonize,” with a target of carbon neutrality in its own facilities and operations by 2030. Leadership emphasizes that the scale of its installed base and its digital capabilities, such as AI-driven turbine inspections using digital twins, differentiate its ability to accelerate the energy transition.
