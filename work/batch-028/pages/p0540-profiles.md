## About
Food Northwest operates from Portland, Oregon, where it was organized in 1914 as the Northwest Canners Association. Incorporated in Oregon in 1922, the trade association evolved through mergers and name changes — becoming the Northwest Food Processors Association in 1969 and rebranding as Food Northwest in 2018. The organization serves the food processing industry across Idaho, Oregon, and Washington from its office positioned midway between the three state capitals.

The association provides advocacy, training, technical resources, and networking for 350-plus member companies. Its professional staff manages government relations on food policy, environmental regulation, energy, and workforce issues; develops tools such as the Listeria Control Checklist and Sanitary Design Guide; and produces the annual Food Northwest Process & Packaging Expo, which marked its 50th edition in 2022. Five volunteer committees and task forces address sector-specific challenges, while the Education and Research Institute, incorporated in 2007, supports scholarships and workforce development.

Membership spans international brands, regional processors, and family-owned operations handling fruits, vegetables, seafood, dairy, bakery, specialty foods, and juices. Processor members represent nearly all food categories, while supplier members provide equipment, ingredients, and services to the industry. The association's Endowment Fund, established in 1989, funds food-science education at colleges in the three states.

Food Northwest was founded to advance the region's ability to produce and deliver safe, wholesome food to global markets. Its core priorities — food policy, government relations, environmental stewardship, energy, education, and sustainability — are set annually by the membership and affirmed by the Board of Directors, positioning the association as an authoritative voice for the Pacific Northwest food processing cluster.
