Today the firm is headquartered in Carmel, Indiana, with manufacturing facilities in Indianapolis, Indiana; Tultitlán, Mexico; and Amsterdam, Netherlands.

The company produces low-calorie sweeteners and liquid water enhancers under its own Splenda and Java House brands, while also serving as the U.S. private-label supplier in those categories. Its Indianapolis campus includes a 350,000-square-foot distribution center, a spray-dry plant with liquid blending, and an aseptic packaging line for shelf-stable protein shakes, ready-to-drink coffee, and creamers. A global workforce of over 1,200 supports R&D, market research, quality, creative design through the in-house agency The Workshop, manufacturing, distribution, and operations.

Heartland’s customers include retailers, foodservice operators, and brand owners across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Latin America, and Asia-Pacific. The company cites strategic co-manufacturing partnerships with category leaders and notes that a senior manager at a global foodservice retailer praised its responsiveness and target-matching expertise.

The organization emphasizes “Fast, Flexible, Innovative” as cultural pillars, attributing its speed to vertical integration that enables ideation-to-shelf execution. Its stated mission is to help people live happier, healthier, and longer lives by making it easier to reduce sugar.
