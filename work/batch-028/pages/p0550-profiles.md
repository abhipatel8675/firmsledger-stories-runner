# Hansa Plastics

**Old description:** Hansa Plastics is committed to providing our customers with the best quality molds at a fair price.

## About
Hansa Plastics was founded in 1959 in Queens, New York, by Peter Schmidt Sr., a master tool maker from Hamburg, Germany. The family-owned business relocated to Kings Park on Long Island's North Shore, where it operates today under the leadership of Schmidt's sons.

The company specializes in custom plastic injection molding, mold making, and mold design, with expertise in multi-action, hot-runner, 3-plate, two-shot, over-mold, and insert molds. Its Kings Park facility houses injection molding machines ranging from 30 to 385 tons, supported by a full-service mold shop, on-site tool room, and rapid prototyping capabilities. Secondary services include ultrasonic welding, hot stamping, pad printing, 3D printing, assembly, and packaging. Hansa Plastics has produced hundreds of distinct parts over its history.

Hansa Plastics serves a broad range of industries, including aerospace, automotive, communications, electrical, electronics, medical, military, marine, lighting, packaging, and consumer goods such as housewares, hobby products, and small appliances.

The company emphasizes on-time delivery to specification and offers in-house mold repair, refurbishment, or alteration at little or no cost. While most manufacturing is domestic, Hansa Plastics also leverages overseas partners for competitive tooling quotes.
