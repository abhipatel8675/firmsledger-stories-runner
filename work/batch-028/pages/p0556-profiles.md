**Old description:** Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama and Kia Motor Manufacturing Georgia.

## About
Hyundai Mobis Alabama, LLC was established in 2002 on nearly 83 acres in Montgomery, Alabama. The company operates as a subsidiary of Hyundai MOBIS, the parts and service arm for South Korean automakers Hyundai Motor Company, Genesis Motors, and Kia Motors. It was created to serve as the largest Tier 1 supplier to Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama (HMMA) and Kia Motor Manufacturing Georgia (KMMG).

The facility spans a 600,000-square-foot plastics plant where injection molding produces front and rear bumpers, instrument panel frames, and after-service parts. Bumpers are molded and painted in-house. A neighboring module plant assembles cockpit (dashboard) modules and front and rear chassis modules using parts from Tier 2 and 3 suppliers. The site also houses a Redistribution Center that ships genuine Hyundai and Kia service parts to dealerships, auto parts stores, and distribution centers across North America.

The company serves the automotive industry exclusively, delivering just-in-sequence modules to HMMA and KMMG for final vehicle assembly. Its Redistribution Center supports the aftermarket by supplying parts to dealerships and retailers nationwide. Customers include the two vehicle assembly plants and the broader North American dealer network.

Hyundai Mobis Alabama emphasizes just-in-sequence delivery, in-house molding and painting, and modular system integration to reduce vehicle weight, part count, and assembly complexity. The operation holds ISO 14001 and IATF 16949 certifications and follows the Hyundai Motor Group's core values of customer focus, challenge, collaboration, people, and globality.
