**Old description:** Hemlock Semiconductor celebrates 65 years of innovation and impact

## About
Hemlock Semiconductor (HSC) operates a chemical processing facility in Hemlock, Michigan. Founded in 1961, the company has spent over six decades producing hyper-pure polysilicon. Ownership details are not publicly disclosed in the source material.

HSC is the only polysilicon manufacturer headquartered in the United States and one of five major producers worldwide. Its polysilicon serves as the foundational material for semiconductor wafers — the "brains" behind virtually all electronic devices — and for the solar power industry. The company emphasizes low embodied greenhouse gas emissions in its polysilicon, enabling ultra low-carbon solar panels that can reduce project embodied carbon by up to 50 percent.

HSC serves the semiconductor and solar industries. Its polysilicon is used in computer chips found in nearly all electronic devices globally, and by solar project developers and owners seeking lower-carbon panels.

The company states its vision is to become the preferred supplier of silicon-enabled technologies to the low-carbon, digital world. Its values — teamwork, responsibility, excellence, and customer focus — are described as the foundation of a culture built for lasting impact.
