# Home Team Energy Company

**Old description:** Milwaukee & Madison’s Trusted Solar Panel Installation Company

## About
Home Team Energy Company is a residential solar installer based in Brookfield, Wisconsin, serving the Milwaukee and Madison areas. Founded in 2019 by Ryan Lettau, a Milwaukee School of Engineering graduate with a decade of solar industry experience, the company began as a family operation run from a garage. Lettau's wife, Shelly, managed permitting and logistics while their young sons assisted with site cleanup, establishing a deliberately small, family-owned structure that remains central to the business.

The firm provides turnkey solar energy systems for homeowners across Southern Wisconsin, handling design, permitting, and installation with its own in-house crews rather than subcontractors. Services include rooftop solar arrays, battery storage, and EV charger installation. Since launch, the team has completed more than 700 residential projects comprising over 13,000 installed panels, with typical installations ranging from two days to four weeks from contract signing to commissioning.

Home Team Energy serves exclusively residential customers, primarily homeowners of WE Energies territory who want to offset rising electricity costs through net metering. The company does not pursue commercial or utility-scale work, focusing instead on single-family homes where the founder can still meet personally with each client.

Lettau started the company after watching rapid growth at a previous solar employer erode customer service. He designed Home Team Energy to stay "intentionally small" so that quality and direct communication never give way to volume. The firm emphasizes transparent pricing, honest advice on battery ROI, and full project management from permit to permission-to-operate.

Drawing on a lifelong fascination with locomotive air horns, he began adapting that massive sound for everyday vehicles, initially operating out of his bedroom and selling kits from the back of his pickup truck before leaving his day job to grow the business full-time.

The company designs and assembles a wide range of air-horn and onboard-air products, including multi-chime train horn kits, standalone horns, spare-tire-delete mounting systems, 12- and 24-volt onboard air systems, compressors, air tanks, valves, fittings, pressure switches, and complete wiring kits. It also offers electric horns, marine horns, and public-address units, along with a library of instruction manuals, wiring diagrams, and an installer-locator service to support DIY and professional installation.

HornBlasters serves automotive enthusiasts, truck and SUV owners, commercial fleets requiring 24-volt systems, marine operators, and motorcycle riders. Its products are marketed to drivers seeking louder warning devices for safety, as well as to hobbyists building custom air-suspension or air-accessory setups. The installer locator connects customers with professional shops across the United States.

Heller has stated that the business was started to make roads safer by ensuring drivers are heard, turning a personal safety concern into a product line that now includes a two-year manufacturer's defect warranty on its branded kits.
