## About
PECA Labs is headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. The company was founded by a group of Carnegie Mellon University alumni, including pediatric surgeon Dr. Arush Kalra, along with Doug Bernstein and Jamie Quinterno. The trio, all recognized on Forbes 30 Under 30 lists, established the venture to develop cardiovascular implantable devices based on a proprietary polymer platform.

The company designs and manufactures a family of polymeric cardiovascular implants. Its product line includes radiopaque vascular grafts with embedded markers for noninvasive imaging, controllably expandable grafts that allow pre-implant customization and post-implant growth, anti-thrombogenic grafts with an inherent bio-inert surface, the MASA Valve polymeric pulmonary valved conduit for pediatric reconstruction, and the STEALTH transcatheter aortic valve with fully polymeric leaflets. PECA reports thousands of implants of its radiopaque graft to date.

PECA Labs serves the cardiovascular medical device market, with a focus on pediatric and adult congenital heart surgery. Its devices are used in hospital settings and clinical trials; the MASA Valve, for example, is being evaluated in an FDA early feasibility study at five U.S. sites, with the first human implant performed at Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia in June 2023.

The company’s differentiation rests on its tunable polymer platform, which enables radiopacity without added metals, controllable expansion for growth accommodation, and a bio-inert surface that does not rely on drug coatings that can wear off. The platform also allows fully polymeric leaflets that permit slimmer delivery systems for transcatheter valves.
