## About
Plastic Forming Inc. operates from Richland Hills, Texas, in the Fort Worth metro area. The company was founded in 1978 by Tom Stepp, a native Texan who spent over 20 years at Bell Helicopter before renting 2,500 square feet of industrial space with a single machine and two employees—one of them his wife, Dorothy, who handled bookkeeping. Their first projects included a children's swimming pool protector and a point-of-purchase display.

Today the firm occupies more than 45,000 square feet of manufacturing space and offers vacuum pressure forming (thermoforming), precision die-cutting, plastic fabricating, and packaging services. The thermoforming process heats plastic sheets and shapes them with vacuum or hydraulic pressure; molds are built in-house to customer specifications. Die-cutting handles materials such as paper, fabric, or thin metal for tight-tolerance components. Fabricating covers molding, machining, and forming of raw plastic into finished parts.

The company serves industries including automotive, electronics, packaging, and consumer goods, producing items such as disposable containers, custom packaging, and specialized components. Customers range from businesses needing short-run prototypes to those requiring ongoing production of formed plastic parts.

Stepp's founding values—transparency, integrity, and diligence—remain central to the operation. The company emphasizes a 5S workplace organization system to maintain efficiency and consistency, and its stated mission is to exceed expectations through innovation, quality, and sustainable practices.
