## About
R-K Electronics is based in Mason, Ohio, and traces its roots to February 1949 when Bill Keller and John Runge founded R-K Electric Company, Inc. as a panel builder serving steel mills, machine tool builders, and paper manufacturers across Ohio, Indiana, and Kentucky.

Today the company designs and manufactures a range of electronic automation control components, from intrinsically safe low‑voltage relays to 600‑volt three‑phase monitoring controls. In addition to its standard product line, R-K develops custom processor‑based embedded controllers—dedicated PLCs with hardware, firmware, and packaging tailored to OEM specifications—for equipment and machinery control systems.

R-K’s customers span the electrical controls industry and OEMs in sectors such as heating and refrigeration, pumping and level control, as well as the original steel, machine tool, and paper markets. The company supplies both off‑the‑shelf components and fully customized control solutions built to each OEM’s operating requirements.

The founder’s original decision to start the business because they “couldn’t do any worse working for themselves” evolved into a stated philosophy of customer‑driven innovation: R-K emphasizes listening to customers to create control components with unique features that simplify control requirements and make customers more successful. Ongoing development includes controls that interface with the company’s own color touch screens.
