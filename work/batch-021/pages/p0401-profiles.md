## About
PacMoore Products, headquartered in Hammond, Indiana, operates as a contract food manufacturer with additional production facilities in Mooresville, Indiana, and an extrusion innovation center in Illinois. The company does not disclose its founding year or ownership structure in the provided materials.

The company provides contract manufacturing services including extrusion, spray drying, dry blending, liquid blending, re-packaging, and consumer packaging. Its two Midwest facilities are BRC AA-rated and equipped with pilot-scale extrusion and spray drying equipment for research and development. PacMoore offers both toll processing and turnkey processing, manages ingredient procurement, and provides 15 days of free warehousing for inbound raw materials and outbound finished goods. Shipping capabilities include rail, less-than-truckload, and full truckload.

PacMoore serves the food industry, processing categories such as plant proteins, powder blends, homogeneous liquid blends, extruded inclusions, snacks, cereals, confectionery, flavors, nutraceuticals, and excipients. It also processes excipients for the pharmaceutical industry, requiring heightened care and precision.

The company emphasizes that it never competes with its customers' brands, maintains audit-ready facilities at all times, and designs plant layouts for scalable expansion. Its stated mission includes fostering whole-life transformation for employees and investing in the communities where it operates.
