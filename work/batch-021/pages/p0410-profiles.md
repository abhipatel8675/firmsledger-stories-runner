## About
Precision Molds & Molding Co. has operated from Van Nuys, California since 1985. The company is led by President Martin Danial and emphasizes long-tenured staff, noting that many employees have been with the firm since its inception.

The shop provides full-service custom plastic injection molding, handling projects from conceptualization and design through mold development and final production. Its capabilities cover mold design, tooling, and production runs, with experience in high-performance materials such as PEEK and elastomers for aerospace applications.

The company serves medical and dental device manufacturers, as well as aerospace customers. A testimonial from a medical device producer highlights the firm's ability to meet stringent specifications and deliver "Made in the USA" parts.

Precision Molds differentiates itself through decades of expertise, a focus on customization, rigorous quality assurance systems including inspection protocols and comprehensive testing, and a commitment to short lead times and responsive customer service. The president cites clear communication and reliable products as foundations of the business.
