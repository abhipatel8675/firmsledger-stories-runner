Its customer base spans the Mid-Atlantic region — Maryland, Washington D.C., Virginia, Pennsylvania, New York, and New Jersey — as well as national accounts.

The company emphasizes quality and customer service as its foundation, citing UL-approved processes, adherence to military and IPC specifications, and a commitment to on-time delivery. It also provides material selection guidance for substrates such as FR-4, PTFE, Rogers, Taconic, Arlon, and metal-core laminates to optimize signal integrity, thermal performance, and mechanical stability.
