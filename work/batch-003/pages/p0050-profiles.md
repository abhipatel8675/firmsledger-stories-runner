## About
Duro-Flex, a family-owned corrugated packaging manufacturer, was founded in 1956 in High Hill, Missouri. For over 70 years the company has served businesses across the Midwest from its facility along Interstate 70, growing into a regional supplier with nationwide reach.

The company produces single-, double-, and triple-wall corrugated boxes, custom foam inserts, die-cut packaging, printed boxes, industrial-grade pallet boxes, collapsible bulk shippers, hazardous-waste containers, corrugated pallets, and specialty wood crates. Its capabilities include custom corrugated design, foam fabrication, die cutting, multi-color printing, and full package assembly with inner partitions and dividers.

Duro-Flex serves the aerospace, construction, food and beverage, e-commerce, medical, and general industrial sectors. Customers range from local Missouri manufacturers to national distributors who rely on the company for both standard and custom packaging solutions.

The company cites its membership in the Association of Independent Corrugated Converters (AICC), complete packaging assembly services, local delivery throughout Missouri and the Midwest, and outsourced shipping nationwide as key differentiators. It emphasizes a hands-on, customer-service approach built on decades of family ownership.
