## About
Gus Willy Apparel was founded in 2017 in Metairie, Louisiana, by Craig Leaumont, who named the business after his third child, Gustave William Leaumont, known as Gus Willy. The company began as a custom apparel operation focused on screen printing and embroidery for teams and corporate clients.

Today the shop runs two automatic and four manual screen‑print presses alongside professional‑grade embroidery machines with 21 work stations, including 3D embroidery capability. An in‑house graphic design team prepares artwork for both decoration methods. Beyond apparel, the company supplies promotional items such as pens, koozies, water bottles, and custom etched tumblers, and has expanded into a full‑line safety distributor offering ANSI‑compliant high‑visibility vests, jackets, hard hats, helmets, gloves, and other job‑site essentials. Orders range from small crew outfitting to workforces of several hundred.

The customer base spans construction, manufacturing, utilities, oil and gas, youth sports leagues, and corporate accounts across the southeastern United States.

Leaumont started the venture to honor his son, who was diagnosed with non‑verbal autism in 2013, and the business emphasizes a one‑stop shop for apparel and safety, fast turnaround, durable materials, and local, responsive service.
