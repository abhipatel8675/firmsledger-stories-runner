## About
Dane Manufacturing operates from Waunakee, Wisconsin, where it was established in 1917 by a group of local investors to produce a below‑the‑frost‑line water pump. The company’s early success came with the Can’t Clog hog feeder, a staple in agriculture, before it transitioned into contract manufacturing. In 2001, engineer and CEO Troy Berg acquired the business, steering it toward innovation while retaining a focus on employee development and customer service.

Today the firm provides end‑to‑end metal fabrication and contract manufacturing services, including laser cutting, forming, welding, assembly and integration, packaging, order fulfillment, and finishing. Technical support covers process planning and material selection. Quality control relies on an InspecVision Planar P220.35 high‑speed 2D measurement system alongside staff inspections. Production takes place in a 510,000‑square‑foot smart factory that incorporates automated equipment and streamlined operations.

Dane primarily serves OEM manufacturers across various sectors, drawing on a history that includes agricultural equipment. The company positions itself as a partner that handles projects from initial design through final delivery, offering logistics support such as packaging and shipping.

The company’s stated mission is to enrich employees, customers, and the community through entrepreneurial spirit and innovative metal solutions. Core values — Invest, Innovate, Integrity, Excellence — guide its operations. Dane highlights its ISO 9001:2015 certification, six INC 5000 awards (2007, 2008, 2009, 2013, 2019, 2020), and a culture of continuous capability expansion as key differentiators.
