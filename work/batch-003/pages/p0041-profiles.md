Its capabilities span surface-mount and through-hole PCB assembly, automated cable processing (350 MCM to 28 AWG), conformal coating, potting, functional testing, programming, and design documentation. The plant runs three SMT lines with Panasonic chip shooters and fine-pitch placement, BTU 10-zone ovens, two CR Technology AOI machines, an X-ray unit, universal insertion equipment, two wave solder machines (one RoHS-compliant using SAC305 alloy), a BGA rework station, and Cirris cable testers. The floor includes 22 manual solder cells separated for RoHS and non-RoHS work, four slide lines, and three final QC stations. Production volumes range from prototype to high-volume runs.

The company serves original equipment manufacturers and other electronics businesses requiring contract assembly, though specific industry sectors are not listed in its public materials.

CEA states its mission is to be an industry leader in electronics manufacturing by continuously improving people, processes, and management to exceed customer expectations. It emphasizes quick turnaround, competitive pricing, and the flexibility of turnkey solutions, including reverse-engineering cable assemblies when drawings or bills of materials are unavailable.
