## About
Endicott Furniture Co. Inc. has operated in Concord, New Hampshire, since 1925, when the current owner’s great‑grandfather founded the business. The family‑owned retailer began as a modest furniture store and has grown into a multi‑building showroom spanning more than 20,000 square feet across three floors and seven show floors.

The company sells a broad range of home furnishings — bedroom, living room, dining, home office, and accessories — carrying brands such as New England Made, Amish Made, and Fjords, alongside a curated selection of imports from Canada and other countries. In addition to retail sales, Endicott provides complimentary in‑home decorating services, where staff assist with furniture selection, placement, fabric coordination, floor planning, and color‑flow consultation.

The store serves residential customers throughout New Hampshire and the broader New England region, targeting homeowners and families seeking traditional, country, and Shaker‑style furnishings. Its showroom is designed as a series of accessorized room displays to help shoppers visualize pieces in a home setting.

Endicott emphasizes a no‑pressure, personal shopping experience rooted in long‑term customer relationships; many patrons are described as acquaintances or friends. The business highlights its commitment to Made‑in‑USA furniture while offering imported options for variety and value, and it differentiates itself through free design services and a spacious, multi‑level showroom.
