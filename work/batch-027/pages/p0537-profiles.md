Today the 20,000-square-foot facility houses 19 injection molding machines ranging from 25 to 650 tons with shot sizes up to 7.5 pounds. The shop processes a wide range of thermoplastics including ABS, acetal, acrylic, HIPS, nylon, polycarbonate, polypropylene, polystyrene, polyurethane, PVC, and TPU. In-house tooling capabilities center on two Fadal high-speed machining centers, supporting mold production from simple mud sets to Class A tools, along with mold maintenance and repair.
