Delicious New Mexico operates from Albuquerque in Bernalillo County, New Mexico. While a precise founding year is not published, the team notes nearly 20 years of experience working with food businesses across the state.

The organization runs a fleet of refrigerated vehicles on weekly statewide routes, picking up from multiple farms on a single trip to consolidate loads and reduce costs. Services include scheduled pick-up and delivery for a flat fee, assistance with wholesale packaging and pricing, and retail placement coordination. Three staff members are listed as contacts: a director of finance and programs, a facilities manager, and a project coordinator.

Delicious New Mexico serves small and mid-sized farms, food producers, and retail outlets throughout New Mexico. Broadcast and audio applications span in-ear monitors, broadcast infrastructure, and alert systems. Test and measurement clients range from semiconductor chip makers to RF calibration labs. In the semiconductor sector, Delta's interconnects appear in etch equipment, deposition tools, and chip testers.

The company emphasizes its "Delta Difference" — vertically integrated facilities that control the entire manufacturing process from raw stock to finished product, enabling quick, cost-effective delivery of high-performance customized solutions. ## About
The Department of Food Science and Technology operates from Corvallis, Oregon, as part of Oregon State University’s College of Agricultural Sciences. A public institution, the department has no single founder but traces its roots to the university’s land-grant mission in agricultural research and education.

The department provides a suite of technical services for the food and beverage sector, including flavor analysis, food microbiology and safety testing, pilot-scale manufacturing, product and process development, quality and shelf-life evaluation, and sensory and consumer testing. Its pilot manufacturing facility supports small-batch production for product validation, while specialized service lines address beer, wine, dairy, seafood, and general food and beverage categories.

Clients range from startups to established processors in the Pacific Northwest and beyond. The department collaborates with industry partners such as Scott Labs on beer spoilage prevention and conducts research on smoke impact for Oregon wine grape growers. It also serves as the Western Regional Center to Enhance Food Safety (WRCEFS), delivering FSMA training to small and mid-sized farms, processors, and wholesalers across 13 western states and two territories.

What distinguishes the department is its integration of academic research, extension outreach, and fee-for-service testing under one roof. The WRCEFS designation, backed by a USDA-NIFA grant, positions it as a hub for food safety training, while its pilot plant and sensory labs allow companies to de-risk new products before full-scale commercialization.
