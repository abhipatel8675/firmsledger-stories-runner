# Factor 55

**Old description:** None

## About
Factor 55 is based in Boise, Idaho. The company began as an engineering design firm whose staff brought extensive experience from the aerospace, defense, and semiconductor sectors, applying that rigor to a shared passion for off-road vehicle recovery.

Factor 55 designs and manufactures a line of recovery hardware including winch-line shackle mounts (UltraHook, FlatLink, ProLink), vehicle recovery kits (Owyhee, Sawtooth, Borah, Baja), kinetic recovery ropes, and a range of rigging accessories. Products are developed in-house using 3D CAD and Cosmos finite-element analysis, then validated through destructive testing at an instrumented test facility. Components are precision CNC-machined from 6000- and 7000-series aluminum alloys and 6Al-4V titanium.

The gear serves recreational off-road drivers (Jeep, truck, SUV), powersports users (UTV, ATV), and military/industrial customers. Example product families include the UltraHook XTV for side-by-sides, the FlatLink MultiMount for winch mounting, and the HD Recovery Kit Teton for heavy-duty trucks.

The company differentiates itself through its Closed System Winching™ methodology, which replaces traditional hooks with closed-link hardware to eliminate latch failure.
