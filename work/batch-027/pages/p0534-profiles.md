## About
FDH Electronics operates from Naugatuck, Connecticut, as a division of FDH Aero. Its product range covers interconnect, wire and cable, harness management, and electromechanical components, including 1553 Data Bus interconnect products, custom harnesses, high-performance aerospace-grade wire and cable, and high-frequency RF connectors.

The company positions itself as a single-source supply chain partner for OEM and aftermarket programs, emphasizing its status as a certified QPL manufacturer and its ability to meet or exceed MIL-STD-810 and RTCA/DO-160 environmental standards. Its value proposition centers on supply chain excellence, global reach, and the breadth of franchised product lines.
