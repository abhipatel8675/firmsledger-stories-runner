# Enable Injections, Inc.

**Old description:** Founded in 2010, Enable Injections is a healthcare innovation company committed to improving the patient treatment experience through the development and manufacturing of enFuse.

## About
Enable Injections, Inc. was founded in 2010 by medical device entrepreneur Mike Hooven in Cincinnati, Ohio. Hooven, who previously founded AtriCure and Enable Medical, established the company to develop a wearable drug delivery platform that could administer large volumes of medication subcutaneously. The privately held company has since raised more than $300 million in capital funding to advance its technology.

The company designs, develops, and manufactures the enFuse® system, a purely mechanical, hands-free wearable injector that delivers pharmaceutical and biologic therapeutics subcutaneously. The platform accommodates drug product viscosities ranging from 1 to 100 centipoise and eliminates the need for intravenous infusion, allowing patients to receive treatment through a simple injection under the skin. All design, development, and manufacturing operations are based at the company's Cincinnati headquarters.

Enable Injections serves the pharmaceutical and biotechnology industries, partnering with companies such as Sanofi, Sobi, and Aptar Digital Health to integrate enFuse with specific therapies. Sanofi’s subcutaneous Sarclisa (iscatuximab) has received regulatory approval in the U.S. and EU for administration via enFuse, while Sobi’s Aspaveli (pegcetacoplan) has obtained CE certification for use with the platform. The company also supports clinical trials and commercial launch planning for partnered combination products.

The company states its technology was driven by patient insights to improve the treatment experience, increase safety, and reduce the time, cost, and burden of chronic disease management. By removing needle-stick hazards and manual mixing steps, enFuse aims to enable patients to perform light to moderate activities during administration. The platform has been recognized by industry publications including Fierce Pharma and PM360 for its novel approach to large-volume subcutaneous delivery.

## About
Enovix is headquartered in Fremont, California, and was founded in 2007 in Silicon Valley with the goal of reinventing the lithium-ion battery architecture. The company became publicly listed on NASDAQ (ENVX) in 2021 and operates as a manufacturer of advanced silicon-anode batteries.

Enovix designs and manufactures lithium-ion batteries using proprietary 3D cell architecture, offering 100% active silicon anodes as well as blended silicon-carbon and graphite platforms. Manufacturing spans multiple owned facilities: a high-volume Fab2 in Malaysia for advanced silicon-anode batteries, electrode coating and cell production lines in South Korea for blended platforms, an innovation hub in the U.S. for materials research and cell design, and an AI-driven R&D center in India.

The company serves consumer electronics, drones and autonomous systems, defense and public safety, and medical and industrial markets. Its defense-oriented cells are TAA-compliant with a secure, in-house supply chain. A recent 100,000-battery order for drones illustrates its scale in the autonomous systems sector.

Enovix says its differentiation lies in a fundamentally different battery design that delivers higher energy density without sacrificing cycle life or charging speed, backed by TAA-compliant, in-house manufacturing and flexible chemistries tailored to custom requirements for demanding applications.
