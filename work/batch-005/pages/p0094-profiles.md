Power Trans Inc. was established in 1967 in Bridgeville, Delaware, as a distributor of power transmission components and industrial supplies. The company maintains its headquarters on Fawn Road in Bridgeville and operates a second branch in Bear, Delaware, serving the Mid-Atlantic region.

The business distributes a range of power transmission products from manufacturers such as Carlisle, Daido, Rexnord, Browning, Lovejoy, Morse, Sealmaster, and 4B Components Limited. Its product lines include conveyor components and hydraulic hose assemblies, supported by a sales staff trained in product application and selection.

Power Trans serves industrial customers across sectors that rely on power transmission, conveying, and fluid power systems. The company positions its value on long-standing relationships with both suppliers and end users, leveraging decades of product knowledge to address production and process challenges.

The firm emphasizes quality customer relationships and access to superior brands as its primary differentiators, with a team that offers experience and technical knowledge to keep client operations running efficiently.
