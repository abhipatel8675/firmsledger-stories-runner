## About
Oregon Made Plastic Products, Inc. (OMPP) was founded in 2011 in Canby, Oregon, by a team with a few product ideas and deep molding know‑how. The company launched its own HITCH‑GEAR product line in 2013, which funded an expansion into custom injection molding services.

Today OMPP operates seven injection molding machines ranging from 25 to 90 tons (with experience up to 150 tons), specializing in small‑part and short‑run production. The shop offers in‑house pad printing, sonic welding, tooling, prototyping, CNC milling, and full mold design and sourcing. It works with both local and offshore mold makers and automation partners to handle projects from concept through production.

The company serves aerospace, automotive, medical, industrial, and electrical sectors. Its customers include manufacturers seeking alternatives to long overseas lead times, entrepreneurs needing a domestic molder, and other molders looking for overflow or specialty capacity.

OMPP emphasizes a quality‑first, low‑cost approach with flexibility to meet varied demand. Sustainability is a stated priority: the firm designs its own products to incorporate regrinds and reprocessed materials, and its tree logo reflects a commitment to reducing plastic waste. When a job falls outside its capabilities, OMPP refers customers to other molders in the region.
