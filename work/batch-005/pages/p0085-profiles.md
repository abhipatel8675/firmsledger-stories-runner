## About

## About
North Alabama Fabricating Company (NAFCO) is a structural steel fabricator headquartered in Birmingham, Alabama. Founded in 1954 by World War II veteran John Parrish, the business began as a structural detailing specialist and expanded into engineering, fabrication, and erection of complex steel projects. It remains a third-generation, family-owned operation under the Parrish family.

The company provides a full range of steel services — structural and industrial fabrication, erection, OEM subassemblies, in-house detailing (using Tekla and SDS/2), engineering, preconstruction design-assist, turnkey EPC for bulk material handling, sophisticated shop-applied coatings, and shop-applied fireproofing. NAFCO operates three fabrication sites in Alabama and a Chicago-based engineering division, Wolf Point Engineers & Contractors. It employs over 200 people and handles projects ranging from 20 to 20,000 tons, claiming to be the largest specialized conveyor fabricator in the United States.

NAFCO serves power generation, commercial construction, aviation, material handling, warehousing, industrial plants, waste recycling, forestry, construction equipment, pulp and paper, aggregate mining, and heavy minerals. Concrete project examples include Schenck Silos, Naheola Biomass Ductwork, Second Line Stages Expansion, CPS Energy, Cliff’s Oak Grove, Cliff’s Bloom Lake, Bryant Denny Stadium renovations, and the Birmingham News Building.

The company emphasizes a one-stop-shop model with all services in-house, a “Quality first. Production second. Safety always.” motto, and AISC-certified facilities with Sophisticated Paint Endorsement (P1) and AWS/CWB welding certifications. Its turnkey capability from detailing through erection and coatings is cited as a key differentiator.
