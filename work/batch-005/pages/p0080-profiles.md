Midian Electronics was founded in 1975 by Chuck Soulliard in Tucson, Arizona. The company also offers in-house custom electronic circuit design, PCB layout, and product development services. All products are designed and built at its Tucson facility and carry a three-year parts and labor warranty.

Midian's equipment serves public safety agencies (police, fire, ambulance), fleet operators (taxi, towing, fishing), government and military users, industrial and commercial businesses, and OEM radio manufacturers. Its interoperability solutions, for example, bridge LTE to VHF or connect Site Connect to P25 networks.

The company distinguishes itself by being the first to bring after-market MTS pulse tone decoders to market and by introducing the world's first voice scrambler small enough to fit inside a portable two-way radio. Midian states its mission is to continue providing innovative, after-market products that improve the safety and versatility of two-way radio networks.

The shop provides full-service automotive repair and maintenance for domestic, Asian, and European vehicles. Its ASE-certified technicians perform work ranging from oil changes and brake service to engine repair, transmission service, and ADAS calibration. The facility also stocks Carquest-branded parts and supplies, selling to both professional mechanics and DIY customers. Brands used in repairs include BG Products and Jasper Engine & Transmission.

Customers include Centre Hall-area drivers of makes such as Ford, Chevrolet, Toyota, Honda, BMW, and Audi, as well as independent repair shops and hobbyists purchasing parts over the counter.
