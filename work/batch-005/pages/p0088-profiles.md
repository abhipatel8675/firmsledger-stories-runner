## About
Ozark Trailer Sales & MFG operates from Harrison, Arkansas, where it was founded in 2009 as a family-owned venture rooted in Christian values. The business began with a vision built on faith, hard work, and service, growing steadily through dedication and a commitment to doing what is right.

The company manufactures its own line of Ozark-branded trailers — including deckover, cargo/enclosed, dump, equipment, hay hauler, roll-off, tilt, and utility models — and also fabricates custom builds. Beyond trailers, it sells truck beds, metal buildings, shipping containers, farm implements, and Bad Boy zero-turn mowers. A service shop handles repairs and maintenance, while rental and used-trailer options round out the offering.

Customers span agriculture, construction, transportation, and landscaping sectors. Farmers purchase hay haulers and implements; contractors use equipment and deckover trailers; landscapers buy Bad Boy mowers; and individual owners seek utility and tilt trailers. The shop has serviced everything from a 22-foot tilt-bed trailer to snow pushers and tractor-cutter combinations.

The founders say their Christian principles — honesty, integrity, and respect — guide every decision. They emphasize treating each customer like family, building relationships on trust and care, and standing behind their products with a "Built Ozark Tough" promise.
