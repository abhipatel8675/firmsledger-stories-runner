## About
Plastic Molding Technology (PMT) operates from El Paso, Texas, with four facilities in the Mountain time zone.

The company’s core processes include injection molding, thermoforming, and extrusion. It performs insert molding and overmolding using robotic automation cells, vertical presses, and rotary tables, as well as heavy-gauge thermoforming and vacuum forming. Mold design, mold building, and repair are completed in-house, and 3D printing alongside CNC machining supports prototyping and low-volume production.

PMT serves aerospace and defense, communications, energy, industrial, and automotive sectors. The company is IATF 16949 certified as a supplier to Tier 1 and Tier 2 automotive manufacturers and maintains ISO 13485 compliance for medical device manufacturing.

The company emphasizes its IATF 16949 certification for automotive supply chains and ISO 13485 compliance for medical work as key qualifications.
