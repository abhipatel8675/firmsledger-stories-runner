# A Vintage Wren

**Old description:** We are a family owned and operated custom furniture business located in beautiful Goffstown NH, where we work closely with you on your specific needs, creating each unique piece by hand for you.

## About
A Vintage Wren is a family-owned custom furniture shop based in Goffstown, New Hampshire. Founded by a husband-and-wife team—the woodworker and his partner—the business began in a small house on a rocky hillside where they crafted pieces by hand for local clients.

The company produces made-to-order furniture including farmhouse dining tables, trestle tables, Shaker-style pieces, modern industrial designs, beds, benches, chairs, and accent tables. Every item is handcrafted from domestically sourced hardwoods, with lumber harvested, sawn, and kiln-dried within New Hampshire. The shop offers full customization on dimensions, wood species, and finish.

A Vintage Wren serves residential customers across New England—New Hampshire, Massachusetts, Vermont, Maine, Rhode Island, and Connecticut. Its pieces are sold directly to homeowners and also through regional design professionals seeking locally made, heirloom-quality furniture.

The founders frame their work as an extension of the “farm-to-table” philosophy, emphasizing a local, sustainable economy. By keeping the supply chain within the state, they aim to deliver furniture that carries the story of New Hampshire’s forests into the homes where it will be used for generations.
