Waxoyl emphasizes that a single application of 120-4 can protect for the life of a vehicle, with active protection spanning -40°F to 300°F. The coatings are safe on paint, rubber, and electrical components, and they stop existing surface rust while lubricating moving parts such as window tracks. Touch-ups are needed only where the coating has been physically abraded.
