## About
SML Packaging Solutions, LLC was founded in 2005 in the Lynchburg, Virginia area and is now headquartered in Forest, Virginia. The company was established to design and build custom packing machinery for the glass and plastic bottling industry, drawing on what it describes as more than 150 years of combined engineering expertise among its team.

The company manufactures a line of case packers and inspection equipment, including the Glass House Packer, Drop Case Packer, Small Brewery Case Packer, Variety Pack Line, and Case Checker, along with drop grids and clamps compatible with major case packers. The Drop Case Packer is rated at up to 30 cases per minute (43,200 bottles per hour) and handles glass or plastic bottles and jars of various shapes. The Small Brewery Case Packer operates at 30 to 350 bottles per minute in a compact footprint suited for tight spaces. All machines are designed for 24/7 operation and can be customized for specific bottle sizes, pack configurations, and production requirements.

SML serves the craft brewing, glass packaging, and high-speed bottling sectors, with equipment used for liquor flasks, beer and wine bottles, pickle jars, and other round or non-round containers. Customers range from growing breweries needing cost-effective automation to high-volume bottling lines requiring heavy-duty, continuous-duty machinery.

The company emphasizes heavy-duty construction, long service life, and total customization as its distinguishing factors, stating that its machines are built for effortless operation, maximum efficiency, and durability in demanding production environments.

# SRC Automotive Inc

**Old description:** SRC Automotive® is owned by its workforce. This structure drives our employees to maintain the highest quality standards, as each has a personal stake in the company's success. Our employee-ownership model ensures excellence in every product we deliver.

## About
SRC Automotive operates from Springfield, Missouri, as an employee-owned manufacturer structured around an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP). The ownership model ties each worker's stake to the company's performance, a design intended to align daily decisions with long-term quality outcomes.

The company remanufactures gasoline, LPG, natural-gas, and marine engines, and also produces newly assembled and industrial engines. Its shop runs 5-axis CNC machining centers, and a dedicated R&D department operates dynamometer test cells for each fuel type. Every unit undergoes complete disassembly, with all components returned to specification using genuine OEM or OEM-validated parts before final testing.

Customers span automotive, marine, and industrial sectors. The remanufactured engines serve as lower-cost service replacements for OEM brands, while the newly assembled and industrial lines address original-equipment and aftermarket demand. The dynamometer capability supports validation across gasoline, LPG, natural-gas, and marine applications.

SRC Automotive positions its process as distinct from repair or rebuild: complete disassembly, specification restoration, OEM-grade parts, and full test cycles backed by warranty. The company also cites its certifications — IATF 16949:2016, ISO 9001:2015, and ISO 14001:2015 — and a circular-economy approach that reduces resource consumption and waste.
