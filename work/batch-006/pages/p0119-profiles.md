Unlimited Metal Fabricators operates from Stuart, Florida, where it was established in 1989 as a locally owned and operated shop. The shop provides custom fabrication, repair services, and engineering design support, including a dedicated marine air-conditioning division. Its process lineup includes CNC press brake forming, laser cutting with a Trumpf TruLaser system, waterjet cutting, saw cutting, plate and sheet metal rolling, shearing, welding (with SolidWorks and AutoCAD integration), grinding, polishing, graining, and CAD modeling. The company states it handles projects from single prototypes to high-volume batches.

Unlimited Metal Fabricators serves clients throughout south Florida, with noted involvement in the marine sector through its Marine A/C Division and membership in the South Florida Manufacturers Association. Its customer base includes businesses requiring custom metal parts, repairs, and design assistance.

The company emphasizes its AISC certification, on-staff engineer for design assistance, free quotes, and same-day or walk-in appointment availability as key differentiators. Bilingual service in English and Spanish is also offered.
