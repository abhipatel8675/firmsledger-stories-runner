## About
Shawmut Infinite operates from Burlington, North Carolina, as a subsidiary of Shawmut Corporation. The facility carries a legacy of over 50 years as a textile supplier, formerly known as Fairystone Fabrics before its rebranding to Shawmut Infinite in 2024.

The company specializes in warp knitting, tricot warping, and suedeing, with a focus on automotive interior and industrial fabrics. It runs a large array of Karl Mayer warp knitting machines capable of 168–210-inch widths and 24- to 36-gauge, 2- to 4-bar tricot constructions. Four tricot warpers handle fine denier filament yarns (15/1 to 300 denier) including nylon, polyester, textured and flat yarns, low-melt polyester, and polypropylene. A multi-million-dollar modernization is adding digitally controlled knitting, warping, and prototyping systems for rapid pattern development and real-time design sharing.

Shawmut Infinite serves automotive (including headliner fabrics), medical, filtration, military, protective apparel, hook-and-loop closure, and general apparel markets. Its customer base includes automotive and industrial designers who rely on rapid prototyping and digital production management.

The company emphasizes landfill-free manufacturing, reusing or recycling all materials, and maintains ISO 9001:2015 certification. It positions itself as an innovation partner offering design flexibility, precision manufacturing, and rapid iteration from prototype to mass production.
