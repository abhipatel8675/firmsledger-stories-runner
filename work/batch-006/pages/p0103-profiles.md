## About
SemiGen, Inc. was founded in 2009 in Londonderry, New Hampshire, to serve as a multi‑faceted partner for electronic device manufacturers. The company operates a 43,000‑square‑foot facility with 20,000 square feet of clean‑room space, housing RF/microwave assembly, automated PCB assembly, and testing under one roof. Now a subsidiary of Naprotek (an Edgewater Capital Partners portfolio company), SemiGen acts as an on‑demand extension of customers’ engineering and production teams.

SemiGen manufactures its own semiconductor and passive components — PIN, Schottky, and step‑recovery diodes, thin‑film circuits, filters, and attenuators — while also providing contract manufacturing: thermo‑compression and ultrasonic wire bonding, ball bonding, fine‑pitch and BGA PCB assembly, and box‑build. Its in‑house ion‑beam foundry offers thin‑film processing, metallization, wafer dicing, and ion‑beam milling. A test lab supports RF/microwave performance testing and high‑reliability MIL‑STD screening up to 50 GHz. Over $3 million in recent equipment investments supports capacity.

The company serves military electronics, commercial products, and medical instrumentation markets, with compliance to space‑level MIL‑PRF‑38534 and military MIL‑PRF‑19500, 750, and 883 specs. SemiGen holds CMMC 2.0 Level 2 certification for defense cybersecurity and earned a BAE Systems Partner 2 Win Silver Tier Award. Customers include prime defense contractors and OEMs needing high‑reliability RF/microwave hybrids and component‑level screening.

SemiGen combines product supply — via its RF Supply Store stocking bonding tools and materials — with manufacturing, testing, and foundry services. Its quality system is ISO 9001:2015 certified and built around military‑spec compliance. The firm emphasizes workforce training, cross‑training, and lessons‑learned reviews to reduce risk on repeat jobs, positioning itself as a supply‑chain link for time‑critical, high‑frequency programs.
