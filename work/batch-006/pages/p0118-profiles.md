## About
Tulsa Sheet Metal operates from Tulsa, Oklahoma, where it has been in business since 1949. The company does not publicly identify its founders or specify an ownership structure such as family, veteran, or woman-owned status.

The shop provides custom metal fabrication with a range of CNC and manual processes: shearing, punching, burning, forming, bending, pressing, stamping, welding, repair, rolling, sawing, cutting, drilling, grinding, polishing, and painting, plus assembly and subassembly. An in-house engineer assists with custom designs. The facility is described as fully equipped for high-capacity orders using larger, heavier, and thicker materials, and works with aluminum, stainless steel, copper, brass, and alloy in sheet, plate, or structural shapes. Materials can be supplied by the shop or shipped directly by the customer.

The company serves commercial and industrial clients across the United States. No specific industry verticals or named customers are disclosed in its public materials.

Tulsa Sheet Metal emphasizes its combined 90 years of metalworking experience and the goal of meeting each customer's fabrication needs as its primary differentiators.
