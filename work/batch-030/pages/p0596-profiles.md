## About
New England Table Company operates from a studio in Londonderry, New Hampshire. The business was founded by Ronald Lohse, a former software engineer who turned a woodworking hobby into a full-time practice after years of building digital systems. Lohse’s first shop stood on the shore of a small New Hampshire lake, where the rhythm of hand tools and the scent of walnut rekindled a childhood memory of watching his grandfather, watercolorist Henry Lohse, build paintings layer by layer.

The company produces custom hardwood tables — dining, coffee, console, desk, and conference pieces — along with matching chairs. Each piece is built by hand in the Londonderry studio using live-edge slabs, book-matched pairs, and river-table constructions. The workflow moves from an initial conversation and digital rendering through wood selection, joinery, and hand-applied finish, with clients receiving photographic updates via a private portal. To date, more than 150 tables have been delivered.

Clients include homeowners, interior designers, and trade professionals across New England. A dedicated trade program supports designers specifying pieces for residential and light commercial projects, while the Atelier Experience offers a higher-touch commission tier in which Lohse personally sources the slab and oversees every design decision.

Lohse started the company on the conviction that furniture should be kept for generations, not replaced. That philosophy drives the Zero Waste Collection, which repurposes offcuts into smaller objects, and the practice of personally delivering and installing each table throughout the region so the maker sees the piece settle into its home.
