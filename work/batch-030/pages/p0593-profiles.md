## About
The National Institute for Innovation in Manufacturing Biopharmaceuticals (NIIMBL) operates from Newark, Delaware, as a public–private partnership established under the Manufacturing USA network. It is funded through a cooperative agreement with the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) in the U.S. Department of Commerce, with additional support from its member organizations.

NIIMBL does not manufacture products itself; instead, it coordinates collaborative projects that advance biopharmaceutical manufacturing platforms, de-risk new technology adoption, and develop workforce training programs. The institute runs a structured solicitation process — Requests for Proposals, Information, and Applications — to fund technical projects in areas such as intelligent biomanufacturing, advanced process analytics, process intensification, and next‑generation biotherapeutic platforms. It also administers workforce initiatives including the NIIMBL eXperience for Educators and a Faculty Fellows sabbatical program.

Its membership spans large and small biopharmaceutical companies, academic institutions, non‑profits, and government agencies. As of June 2023, industry partners represented 51 % of members, academia 30 %, non‑profits 16 %, and government 3 %. Member companies cited in testimonials include GlaxoSmithKline, AstraZeneca, and Bristol Myers Squibb. The institute serves the broader biopharmaceutical manufacturing ecosystem, including medical countermeasure development and biodefense.

NIIMBL’s stated differentiators center on its public–private model that enables pre‑competitive collaboration, accelerates the translation of innovative manufacturing technologies, and strengthens domestic supply‑chain resilience. The organization aims to enhance U.S. economic security, increase global competitiveness, and bring life‑saving medicines to market faster.
