# Mississippi Silicon

**Old description:** Mississippi Silicon is the first domestic manufacturer of raw silicon metal built in the past 40 years and produces approximately 10% of all silicon metal used in the United States.

## About
Mississippi Silicon operates a silicon metal production facility in Burnsville, Mississippi. The plant launched in late 2015, becoming the first new domestic silicon metal smelter built in the United States in four decades. The company is structured as a partnership between Rima Holding USA, Inc. (the majority owner, linked to Brazilian ferroalloy producer Rima Industrial S/A) and Clean Tech I LLC, a group of strategic investors and financial advisers.

The facility produces raw silicon metal using submerged electric arc furnaces that reduce quartz or gravel (SiO₂) with a carbon source to yield 99% pure molten silicon, which is tapped in batches. Mississippi Silicon invested over $200 million in the Burnsville site and now supplies roughly 10% of the silicon metal consumed in the U.S. The operation emphasizes modern furnace controls, proprietary process techniques, and automated off-gas containment to achieve low-cost, high-quality output.

The company sells silicon metal to customers that serve automotive, electronics, steel, and a broad range of consumer, industrial, and commercial markets across North America and globally. Automotive manufacturing is cited as a key end-use sector, alongside electronics and steel production.

Mississippi Silicon was established to restore domestic silicon metal supply, leveraging low-cost Tennessee Valley Authority electricity, proximity to raw-material sources in Mississippi, Alabama, and Kentucky, and advanced automation to become one of the world’s lowest-cost and highest-quality producers. The venture also aims to create jobs in northeast Mississippi.

It was the first commercial electrical contractor in the state to receive a Specialty Wind & Solar classification. Today the business is owned and operated by John and Virginia Wilbanks, both Mississippi natives, and remains family-owned.

The firm designs and installs turn-key solar photovoltaic and battery-backup systems for residential and commercial customers across Mississippi. It also sells, installs, and services Generac home and business standby generators in more than 20 counties, and fits Level 2 and 3 EV charging stations, energy-management thermostats, and LP-tank monitoring. The company acts as a commercial electrical contractor and residential builder, handling permitting, project management, and ongoing system monitoring.

Mississippi Solar serves homeowners, businesses, farms, and institutional clients. Early projects included a poultry farm, a sports arena, and the state's first commercial utility-scale solar PV system as well as the first grid-tie and grid-tie-with-battery installations under the TVA Generation Partners program. The company also supports generator customers in counties such as Lafayette, Pontotoc, Lee, and Lowndes.

The company emphasizes its local roots and code expertise, NABCEP-certified installers, and status as a Generac Power Pro Elite Plus dealer. It backs installations with a 25-year module warranty through REC Solar, lifetime system monitoring, and a five-year workmanship warranty. Its stated mission is to empower customers with reliable, worry-free energy independence, sustainability, and backup power.
