## About
Up North Plastics, Inc., a subsidiary of Poly-America, operates from Cottage Grove, Minnesota. The company manufactures silage bags, grain bags, and bunker covers designed for agricultural feed storage.

The plant runs on what the company describes as state-of-the-art manufacturing equipment, processing premium-grade polyethylene resins into multi-layer films. An extensively trained quality control team performs continuous testing during production, supplemented by additional analysis in an in-house laboratory and on test farms across the United States. Finished goods move through an international distribution network supported by a fleet of more than 300 semi-trucks.

Primary customers include grain producers, beef and dairy operations, and feed managers who rely on airtight storage to preserve high-value forages and grains. The company’s grain bags are engineered to withstand the stress of dry grain, while its bunker covers combine a 2-mil Silage Shield™ layer with a 5-mil black-and-white outer layer for spoilage reduction.

Up North Plastics emphasizes two differentiators: puncture and tear resistance that exceeds foreign competitors’ products, and a service network of distributors and dealers backed by the large truck fleet to ensure bags arrive when and where they are needed.

## About
Utah Solar Detach & Reset operates from Kearns, Utah, serving homeowners and contractors across the Wasatch Front. The locally owned company grew out of a roofing business, giving its founders firsthand experience with the intersection of roof work and solar arrays.

The company specializes in solar panel removal and reinstallation — often called detach and reset — for roof replacements, system maintenance, or upgrades. Technicians document panel positions and wiring before any removal, then follow structured diagnostic sequences to isolate faults before replacing components. Additional services include solar panel cleaning with output verification, critter guard installation, EV charger installation, battery troubleshooting, and solar monitoring setup. Work is performed from a brick‑and‑mortar office in Kearns, with crews traveling to Sandy, Salt Lake City, West Jordan, Taylorsville, Murray, Draper, and surrounding communities.

Primary customers fall into two groups: homeowners who discover their solar arrays must be temporarily removed for roof repairs, and roofing contractors who need a reliable solar detach partner to keep their projects on schedule. Both receive transparent pricing, coordination with project timelines, and a documented process that protects roof penetrations and electrical connections.

The business was built to fill the gap between roofers who lack solar expertise and solar installers who don’t focus on detach‑and‑reset work. Its methodical, climate‑aware approach — accounting for heavy snow loads, freeze‑thaw cycles, and high‑altitude UV exposure — and its local, physical presence are cited as key differentiators.
