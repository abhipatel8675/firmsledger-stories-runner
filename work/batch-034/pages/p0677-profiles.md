ADAR’s core technology, called ADS, dries, pulverizes, and sanitizes waste in a single pass without fossil fuels, added heat, or harmful chemicals. The process yields a dry, odorless, pathogen-free powder that retains the full nutritional profile of the source material. The company commercializes this output in two ways: it sells spec‑consistent powder ingredients — high‑protein supplements from seaweed and seafood waste, zeolite powder, and nutrient‑rich fertilizer from manure — directly to formulators and blenders in the feed, pet food, fertilizer, fuel, and mineral markets; and through ADAR Environmental Services it assumes waste streams from generators at roughly half their current disposal cost, turning liability into feedstock.

Primary customers include animal‑nutrition formulators, pet‑food manufacturers, fertilizer blenders, and industrial mineral processors. A global agricultural company has already endorsed the technology for converting sargassum seaweed and seafood waste into high‑protein livestock feed. Municipal sewage, agricultural by‑products, and wood‑chip waste are also cited as feedstocks.

The company’s differentiation rests on three claims: zero‑emission processing, 100 % nutrient retention, and a business model that cuts waste‑disposal costs by 50 % while creating salable ingredients. The founders state that no thermal process has previously achieved a shelf‑stable, pathogen‑free powder with complete nutrient integrity.
