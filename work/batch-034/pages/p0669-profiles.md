## About
Windy City Wire operates from Tempe, Arizona, where it has built a business around low-voltage cable manufacturing and cable management solutions. The company describes its history as spanning more than 30 years, though a specific founding year is not disclosed. Ownership details and founder names have not been made public.

The company produces American-made SmartWire cables, which include value-added features such as FastFIND footage markers and SmartWire Glide lubrication. Its patented RackPack system uses a reel-in-the-box design to streamline cable pulls, reduce waste, and improve jobsite safety. Complementary products include RackPack Transporters for mobility, SmartFIBER enclosures for fiber handoffs, and the SmartKITS platform for configuring custom cable kits. The firm also offers DAS/BDA quoting support and online calculators for conduit fill and voltage drop.

Windy City Wire serves security integrators, low-voltage contractors, and procurement teams working on public safety BDA/DAS systems, CCTV and IP camera networks, and general structured cabling projects. Customer testimonials cite labor savings on large-scale installations, with users from firms such as Automated Life Inc, Komar Associates, and Eire Electric reporting faster pulls and reduced setup time.

The company emphasizes that its integrated RackPack Field System lowers total installed cost by cutting labor hours and simplifying logistics. Custom branding on RackPack cartons allows contractors to promote their own identity on the job site, while the SmartKITS tool lets teams create, share, and reorder tailored kits without re-engineering each project.
