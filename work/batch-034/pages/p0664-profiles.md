## About
Warbler Woodworks operates from a shop in Waynesboro, Virginia, where a single craftsman designs and builds custom furniture using traditional joinery. The business began as a one-person workshop focused on handcrafted pieces for the local community and surrounding areas including Charlottesville, Harrisonburg, Richmond, and Washington, D.C.

The shop produces heirloom-quality furniture such as wine cabinets, office desks, settees, dining tables, side tables, and credenzas. Each piece is made one at a time using time-honored techniques including dovetail and mortise-and-tenon joinery, executed with a blend of hand tools and power tools. The maker selects local hardwoods — primarily black walnut and cherry, along with white oak, maple, hickory, and ash — for their grain, color, and strength.

Warbler Woodworks serves residential clients who commission custom pieces for their homes. The shop’s reach extends across the Shenandoah Valley and into the Mid-Atlantic region, with customers in Virginia’s major cities and the Washington, D.C. metro area.

The founder emphasizes that mass-produced furniture lacks the subtlety and durability of handcrafted work. By relying on traditional joinery rather than screws or bolts, and by finishing each piece to highlight the wood’s natural character, the shop aims to create furniture that tells a story and endures for generations.

Under the WEMOCO (Warfield Electric Motor Company) banner it builds custom DC motors for original equipment manufacturers, while its DC Connection division rejuvenates obsolete industrial motors and gearboxes. The company maintains six locations across the United States — Frankfort, Illinois; Upland, California; Conyers, Georgia; Waukesha, Wisconsin; Westland, Michigan; and Dallas, Texas — and stocks an inventory of over 10,000 motors available on an exchange basis. The company serves the wind energy sector with new pitch motors and remanufactured pitch and yaw motors for turbines from GE, AMD, SSB, Moog, Clipper, Adda, VEM, ABB, Lonne, AVN, and Bonfiglioli. In material handling it supplies remanufactured motors and drive units for forklifts and warehouse equipment. Additional markets include ground support equipment, agriculture, mining, airport facilities, cold storage, and brewery applications. Customers range from OEMs to independent repair shops such as Mahaffeys Electric Motor Repair and large fleet operators like Anderson Material Handling.

Warfield Electric emphasizes service, quality, and price as its founding principles.
