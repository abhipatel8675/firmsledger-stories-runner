Its product line includes vector network analyzer, spectrum analyzer, and signal generator extension modules that push microwave measurement tools beyond 50 GHz. The company also produces component‑level devices — detectors, mixers, frequency multipliers, and custom systems — all built around in‑house fabricated GaAs Schottky diodes and microelectronic filter structures. Operations are housed in a 20,000‑square‑foot high‑tech facility staffed by roughly 100 engineers, technicians, and administrative personnel.

VDI serves scientific, military, and commercial markets. Early customers included radio astronomy observatories and radar programs; the portfolio has since expanded to support terahertz transmitter and receiver modules for a range of defense and research applications.

The firm’s stated mission is to make the terahertz region of the electromagnetic spectrum as useful as the microwave and infrared bands. This focus on extending measurement capability into the THz band, combined with vertical integration of diode fabrication, is presented as its core differentiator.
