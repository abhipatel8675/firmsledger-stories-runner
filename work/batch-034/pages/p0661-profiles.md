## About

Vapos serves food manufacturers, spice manufacturers, pet food manufacturers, and nut and grain processors. Its customer base includes participants in the almond, pistachio, and peanut supply chains, and the company regularly exhibits at events such as the ASTA Annual Meeting, the American Pistachio Growers Conference, and The Almond Conference.

The company emphasizes food safety as its mission and holds FSSC 22000 certification through SAI Global, positioning its validated processes as a way for brands to protect product integrity and meet regulatory requirements.

**Old description:** We specialize in the development and deployment of advanced blood flow imagers and perfusion monitoring systems.

## About
Vasoptic Medical Inc. operates from Columbia, Maryland, where it develops and deploys advanced blood flow imagers and perfusion monitoring systems. The company's founding year and ownership structure have not been publicly disclosed.

The company's core products are noninvasive optical imaging systems that capture blood flow dynamics at video rates without the need for contrast agents. These imagers are designed for clinical diagnostics and research applications, providing perfusion data that complements existing angiography methods such as OCT-angiography, fluorescein angiography, and ICG angiography.

Vasoptic's systems serve clinical settings, particularly in ophthalmology, where they assist in diagnosing vascular conditions, as well as research institutions studying blood flow dynamics. The technology generated notable interest at the ARVO 2026 Annual Meeting in Denver, indicating engagement with the vision research community.

The company emphasizes that its video-rate, contrast-free imaging offers a distinct advantage over traditional angiography by revealing real-time blood flow fluctuations over each cardiac cycle, enabling assessments that static imaging cannot provide.

# Vermont Green Printing

**Old description:** None

## About
Vermont Green Printing, operating as VG Printing, is a contract screen-printing and embroidery shop based in Morrisville, Vermont. The business was launched in 2014 by co-owner John Goodhue, who had spent more than three decades producing tie-dyed and screen-printed apparel for concert tours such as the Grateful Dead, Phish, and the HORDE festival, as well as wholesale accounts in the U.S. and Japan. Goodhue’s first commercial sales date back to 1983 at a Tucson street fair, and his move to Vermont marked a shift from touring merchandise to a local, service-oriented operation.

The company provides custom screen printing using water-based and algae-based inks, embroidery on caps, jackets, and fleece, full-color garment printing with a 24-piece minimum, and promotional product sourcing. Its printing process avoids plastisol and solvents, relying instead on discharge and opaque water-based inks that are free of PVC, phthalates, and heavy metals. In 2022 the shop adopted Algae Black ink, a bio-based pigment. The facility on Old Creamery Road handles both small and large runs, though specific headcount and floor space are not disclosed.

VG Printing serves a mix of regional and national clients. References on its site include Vermont Festivals LLC, the gaming brand Gamers Nexus, Riverbend Market, Green Mountain Community Fitness, the rowing-machine manufacturer Concept2, Paul’s Hardware, and the tech retailer Level One Techs. Orders range from event merchandise and corporate apparel to e-commerce fulfillment for online stores.

The company states that its founding purpose was to minimize environmental impact, pay living wages, and create products customers can feel good about. It donates at least 1% of gross sales each month to organizations such as the Vermont Food Bank, Planned Parenthood of Northern New England, The Ocean Cleanup, the Last Prisoner Project, and the Equal Justice Initiative. Goodhue’s own history — including a year in prison for cannabis cultivation — informs the shop’s support for criminal-justice reform.
