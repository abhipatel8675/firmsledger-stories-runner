## About

Ixana designs and ships ultra-low-power Wi-R silicon that uses localized electric fields to create a "wireless wire" for short-range, high-speed data links along a person, robot, or machine. Its production-grade XA-NFE2001 transceiver delivers 5 Mbit/s at 0.2 nJ/bit, with a 20 Mbit/s XA-NFE3001 in sampling. The company handles full-custom mixed-signal IC design, AI-accelerated tapeout cycles, silicon validation, and provides development kits to partners.

The technology targets wearable AI (smart glasses, biosensor patches), medical and biosensing systems, robotics, automotive and industrial equipment, and mission-critical defense applications.
