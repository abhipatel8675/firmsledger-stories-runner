## About
Quality Sheet Metal & Welding operates from Piscataway, New Jersey, where it was founded in 1997. The company has served small and large clients for over 25 years, completing thousands of projects across commercial, industrial, and pharmaceutical sectors.

The shop provides a broad range of fabrication services, including custom stainless steel and carbon steel fabrication, industrial ductwork, HVAC system design and installation, high-definition plasma cutting up to 3/4-inch plate, dust collection systems, and 3D printing in materials such as ABS, polycarbonate, and carbon-fiber-reinforced ABS with a build volume of 14 by 10 by 14 inches and ±0.002-inch accuracy. Additional capabilities cover fume and compressor exhaust systems, DuctSox installation, polished stainless steel finishing (including 316L for pharmaceutical and food-grade work), structural steel platforms and mezzanines, OSHA machine guards, on-site welding, and scheduled shut-down or emergency repair work.

Customers span commercial construction, industrial manufacturing, pharmaceutical and cosmetic production, recycling facilities, woodworking shops, and additive manufacturing users. The company has fabricated ductwork for carpentry cabinet shops, dust collectors for pharmaceutical plants, and custom stainless components for food-grade environments.

Quality Sheet Metal emphasizes hands-on management, creative custom solutions, and a safety-first approach backed by OSHA certifications and a master HVAC license (19HC00288200). It also offers 24/7 communication for time-critical projects.
