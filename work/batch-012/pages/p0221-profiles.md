## About
PDI Duct operates from North Chelmsford, Massachusetts, where it has fabricated PVC and CPVC duct systems since 1976. The business began as Plastic Design Inc. and later became a division of Plastic Design Industries, a company that also encompasses Chemical Systems Services, Inc. No founders are named in the source material.

The company produces custom PVC and CPVC ductwork, pipe fittings, and complete industrial ventilation systems for corrosive environments. Its 30,000-square-foot facility includes a 21,000-square-foot manufacturing floor equipped with nitrogen-purged hot-gas welding stations, two CNC machining centers, a 400-watt CO2 laser, pipe fusion equipment, and deionized-water testing systems. Capabilities span triple-pass hot-gas and extrusion welding, precision machining of flanges and ports, custom assembly of duct runs and manifolds, and engineering support from consultation through delivery. The shop handles orders ranging from single prototypes to full production runs, with standard duct diameters from 6 to 24 inches and larger.

PDI Duct serves chemical processing plants, wastewater treatment facilities, semiconductor manufacturers, pharmaceutical operations, testing laboratories, and general industrial ventilation projects. Its customers include engineers, contractors, and facility teams who specify corrosion-resistant thermoplastic systems for fume exhaust, process ventilation, and air handling.

It also maintains confined-space entry certification for on-site work. All fabrication occurs under one roof in Massachusetts, which the company says keeps quality and lead times under its control.
