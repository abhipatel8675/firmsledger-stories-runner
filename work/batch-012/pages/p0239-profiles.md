## About
Sangamon Solar is a solar installation and service company headquartered in Champaign, Illinois. The company operates as an approved vendor and equity-eligible contractor under Illinois energy programs.

The company designs and installs rooftop, ground-mounted, and ballast-mounted solar arrays for residential and commercial customers. Installations use Ironridge mounting hardware with triple-sealed roof penetrations. Every system includes a workmanship warranty, equipment warranties up to 25 years, and 15 years of performance monitoring. Sangamon Solar also provides permitting, interconnection, and Renewable Energy Credit (REC) processing through the Illinois Shines program, and offers maintenance for systems it did not originally install.

Customers include homeowners seeking to offset electricity costs, commercial building owners looking to reduce overhead, and “stranded customers” who need assistance retrieving REC payments from existing installations. The firm also offers zero-money-down leasing options and energy consulting for both homes and businesses.

Sangamon Solar distinguishes itself by handling the full project lifecycle—from design and permitting to long-term monitoring and REC compliance—and by serving as a primary vendor for the state’s stranded-customer REC recovery efforts.
