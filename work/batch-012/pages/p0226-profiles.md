# Precise Circuit Co

**Old description:** Precise Circuit Company has been providing high quality printed circuit board and electro-mechanical assembly services since 1985. Working closely with manufacturers we provide completed products, on time and at competitive cost.

## About
Precise Circuit Company was founded in 1985 by Thomas Misencik in downtown Shelton, Connecticut. The company began as a provider of quality electronic assemblies for small to mid-sized original equipment manufacturers, specializing in surface-mount, through-hole, and mixed-technology printed circuit board assembly. It remains owned and operated by the Misencik family, with Tom Misencik Sr. and Tom Misencik Jr. serving as the primary owners.

The shop offers a full range of PCB assembly services, including surface-mount placement down to 0402 components, through-hole insertion (both machine and hand), wave soldering (leaded and lead-free), automated optical inspection, functional testing, and final box-build assembly. Equipment includes two Samsung SM482 pick-and-place lines, three Heller reflow ovens, a Mirtec MV-3L automatic inspection system, Universal through-hole insertion machines, and both leaded and lead-free wave solder machines. The facility also maintains a climate-controlled board storage room and provides consignment or turnkey procurement options.

Precise Circuit serves original equipment manufacturers in the electronics sector, typically small to mid-sized companies that require prototype through production-volume assemblies. The company works directly with OEMs across industrial, commercial, and defense-related electronics, though specific industry verticals are not publicly listed beyond the general OEM market.

The company emphasizes direct owner involvement, long-term customer relationships, and a commitment to quality, transparency, and competitive pricing. Community engagement includes mentoring the Shelton High School FIRST Robotics team.
