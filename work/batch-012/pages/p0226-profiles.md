**Old description:** Founded with the strongest work ethic we continue to communicate with honesty and fairness to all interested parties.

## About
Precise Cable Inc was founded in 1988 by Billie Van Ark in Longmont, Colorado. The company began as a home-based business driven by Van Ark's passion for providing fabrication solutions. Over three decades, it has grown from grassroots research into a global supplier of custom cable and wire harnessing products.

Today, Precise Cable operates a 15,000-square-foot facility near Denver and Boulder. The shop specializes in custom cable harnessing and precision electro-mechanical assemblies, offering both turnkey and contract manufacturing services. For projects requiring controlled environments, the company maintains ISO-certified clean rooms for fabrication.

The company serves a broad range of industries, including aerospace (flight and ground support), defense, government, commercial, medical, oil and gas, communications, instrumentation, agriculture, automotive, and test and measurement. Customers range from large aerospace programs to smaller commercial projects.

Precise Cable emphasizes that its people are the key differentiator, citing an experienced management team and talented personnel who compete on complex programs of all sizes. The business has grown largely through word-of-mouth referrals, reflecting a commitment to exceeding customer expectations and continuous improvement.

# Precise Circuit Co

**Old description:** Precise Circuit Company has been providing high quality printed circuit board and electro-mechanical assembly services since 1985. Working closely with manufacturers we provide completed products, on time and at competitive cost.

## About
Precise Circuit Company, Inc. was founded in 1985 in Shelton, Connecticut, by Thomas Misencik Sr. after he completed electronic training at the Connecticut School of Electronics and small business management courses at Southern Connecticut State University. The company operates from a downtown Shelton location with easy access to Route 8. It is owned and managed by the Misencik family, with Tom Misencik Sr. serving as president and Tom Misencik Jr. also an owner.

The company provides printed circuit board assembly and electro-mechanical assembly services, specializing in surface-mount, through-hole, and mixed-technology processes. Its capabilities include prototype and production volumes, automated pick-and-place lines (two Samsung SM482 machines), stencil printing, reflow soldering (three Heller ovens), wave soldering (both leaded and lead-free), automatic optical inspection (Mirtec MV-3L), functional testing, and final box-build assembly. The facility also houses through-hole insertion equipment, a climate-controlled board storage room, and depaneling and cleaning systems.

Precise Circuit serves small to mid-sized original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) across various industries, working directly with manufacturers to deliver completed electronic assemblies. The company emphasizes direct owner involvement and long-term customer relationships, though specific industry verticals are not publicly listed.

The company was started to provide quality electronic assemblies for smaller OEMs that needed responsive, transparent manufacturing partners. According to the founders, the “Precise Difference” lies in working directly with the owners, continuous customer interaction for manufacturing feedback, and a commitment to quality, transparency, and commitment at the lowest cost.
