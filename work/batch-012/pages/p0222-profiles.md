# Pacific Transformer

**Old description:** Custom, distribution, padmount, and PDU transformers (CA plant is secondary to overseas)

## About
Pacific Transformer was founded in 1981 in a garage in Anaheim, California, by three softball teammates who each borrowed $1,300 from their parents. The company remains founder-owned and headquartered in Anaheim, with a domestic production facility that serves as a backup for its international operations in Mexico, Thailand, and China.

The company designs and manufactures custom magnetic components — transformers (1 VA to 1,000 kVA), toroidals, inductors, chokes, and power-supply assemblies — for applications ranging from signal-level to utility-scale. Engineering services include free design, computer modeling, and rapid prototyping; quotes are returned within one business day and wound, tested prototypes ship within two business days from the Anaheim floor. Over 45 million transformers have been shipped to date.

Customers span aerospace (Mars rovers, NASA flight hardware), defense (nuclear submarine systems), medical imaging, EV infrastructure (Tesla Superchargers), professional audio (Trainwreck amplifiers), industrial automation, data centers, and renewable energy. The firm works with both startups needing a single prototype and established manufacturers placing hundreds of units annually.

Pacific Transformer emphasizes “everything around the winding”: free engineering, agency-approval expertise (UL, CSA, CE, ETL, TÜV), a domestic failover facility that mitigates supply-chain risk, and pricing leveraged from its overseas plants. The company holds AS9100D, MIL-PRF-27, ISO 9001, and ITAR registrations.
