# Haf Equipment

**Old description:** HaF Equipment is a dry ingredient handling equipment manufacturer whose systems are designed to focus on partner success and excellence across various verticals and applications, from the food industry to pharmaceuticals and plastics.

## About
Haf Equipment, based in Maplewood, Minnesota, was launched in 1994 by founder Pete Hoefler with a focus on dry material handling. The company remains USA-based and serves businesses worldwide.

The company designs and manufactures custom material handling equipment and systems, including filter receivers, bulk bag unloaders, feeders, pneumatic and mechanical conveyors, blending equipment, dust collection, dry sorbent injection, air pollution control, batching systems, and liquid handling assemblies. It offers system integration, batching, unloading, packaging, controls, and multiple conveying phases. Two product lines serve different needs: the H-Series provides pre-engineered, off-the-shelf equipment for faster delivery, while the M-Series features tool-free, quick take-apart designs customized to specific applications.

Haf serves industries such as food processing (dairy, bakery, snacks, pet food, nutraceuticals), plastics, rubber, chemical, pharmaceuticals, ceramics, steel, air pollution control, and batteries. Customers include food processing plants, pharmaceutical manufacturers, plastics processors, and other industrial facilities requiring powder handling, bulk material handling, dust collection, and batching solutions.

The company differentiates itself through operator-centric designs and a quality control process that inspects every piece of equipment before shipment. Its "HAF Difference" values emphasize team focus, humble confidence, elite performance, hustle, and driven-yet-fun culture. In 2024, Haf acquired the assets of Semi Bulk Systems to expand its product lines into both dry and liquid ingredient handling.
