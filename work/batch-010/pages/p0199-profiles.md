Linear Fine Woodworking operates from Phoenix, Arizona, where it has been designing and building custom furniture, cabinetry, and architectural millwork for over 30 years. The shop serves clients across Phoenix, Scottsdale, and the broader Southwest region.

The company produces custom cabinetry, architectural millwork, and one-of-a-kind furniture pieces such as kitchen cabinets, wall units, home theater systems, and office furniture. A distinguishing capability is the in-house production of veneer panels from rough-cut flitches, a process that yields uniformly even, stable panels and allows greater design flexibility. The workshop works with exotic veneers, solid woods, glass, integrated lighting, cables, and specialty hardware.

Projects span both residential and commercial sectors. Homeowners, interior designers, architects, and business owners commission Linear Fine Woodworking for custom residential woodworking, commercial millwork, and specialized installations including hospitality and office environments.

The firm emphasizes that its in-house veneer manufacturing sets it apart from typical cabinet shops, enabling a level of creativity that blurs the line between functional cabinetry and sculpture. Innovation in design and woodworking is cited as a hallmark of every project.
