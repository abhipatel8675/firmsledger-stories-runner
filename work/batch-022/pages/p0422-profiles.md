Roehm serves the automotive, electronics, construction and medical technology industries. Roehm emphasizes decarbonization through its methacrylate Verbund. The LiMA process reduces the carbon footprint of MMA production. PLEXIGLAS® proTerra grades incorporate mechanically recycled PMMA or sustainably certified raw materials. Mold-in-color manufacturing cuts emissions by avoiding energy-intensive coating. The ACRYLITE® SunResist capstock extends product life in high-exposure outdoor applications.
