# Spectral Products

**Old description:** We offer various spectrometers, monochromators, light sources, integrating spheres, detectors,

## About
Spectral Products is headquartered in Putnam, Connecticut, with additional offices in Albuquerque, New Mexico, and Seoul, South Korea. The company's founding year and ownership structure are not publicly disclosed. It operates as a manufacturer of optical instrumentation.

The company designs and builds spectrometers, monochromators, light sources, integrating spheres, detectors, fiber-optic accessories, adapters, and filter wheels. Its product lines include the SM series of miniature spectrometers—such as the SM301/SM301-EX PbS(e) array spectrometer covering 1.0–3.0 µm and the TE-cooled SM304 InGaAs array spectrometer—the CM110 compact 1/8-meter monochromator with customizable slits, and the ASBN-W high-power tungsten-halogen light source assemblies featuring fused-silica lenses and UV-protected aluminum mirrors. An Optics Division also supplies optical coatings and coated optics for high-energy applications.

Spectral Products serves industrial, laboratory, research, and scientific markets, working directly with system designers and end users. The company maintains international representatives in Europe, Asia, and Australia.

The firm attributes its differentiation to an innovative direct-drive technique introduced more than a decade ago, which it says revolutionized computer-based spectrometers and monochromators by delivering high performance at lower cost. Its SM miniature spectrometers are marketed as combining essential features and optimal performance in a small footprint.

# Stark Mountain Woodworking Co

**Old description:** Blending time honored wood craft with the tech of today in the mountains of Vermont since 1993

## About
Stark Mountain Woodworking Co. was founded in 1993 in New Haven, Vermont, a town of about 1,800 people in the state's mountainous region. The business began as a two-person shop and has grown to a team of more than 20 craftspeople operating from a 24,000-square-foot facility.

The company provides full-service custom woodworking, producing cabinetry for homes and offices, furniture, and architectural millwork for institutions such as museums, libraries, and colleges. Each project moves through design collaboration, 2D and 3D shop drawings created with AutoCAD, DataCAD, AlphaCam, and Cabinet Vision, CNC fabrication on three- and five-axis routers, moulding, sanding, edge banding, finishing in a dedicated spray booth, and final assembly and installation. A bandsaw mill and vacuum kiln give the shop control over lumber from tree to finished product, and a full metal shop handles architectural finishes in stainless steel and bronze.

Clients include architects, interior designers, general contractors, and homeowners. Notable project types range from residential and commercial interiors to institutional work for colleges, libraries, and museums.

Stark Mountain has been FSC-certified since 2001 and sources a large percentage of its lumber within a 30-mile radius. The facility runs on roughly 60 percent solar power, with a 54 kW hillside array and a 34 kW rooftop array, and is heated by a pelletizer that converts shop sawdust into fuel. A bike-to-work program has logged over 145,000 commuter miles since 2006.

# Steadfast Farms

**Old description:** The New Standard in USDA-inspected Poultry Processing in New England

## About
Steadfast Farms is based in Bethlehem, Connecticut. Founded in 2017 by Jared McCool, a U.S. Marine Corps veteran who began farming after his honorable discharge in 2014, the veteran-owned operation started as a continuation of military service, applying principles of discipline and resilience to agriculture.

The company runs Connecticut’s only USDA-inspected poultry slaughter and processing facility, providing fee-for-service processing to more than 180 farms across the Northeast. The plant is certified for USDA Organic slaughter and holds Animal Welfare Approved verification, enabling interstate sales of processed birds. In addition to contract processing, Steadfast Farms raises its own pasture-raised poultry—quail, chicken, turkey, pheasant, and chukar partridge—using regenerative farming practices. The facility also offers parting, grinding, sausage making, and stock production.

Steadfast Farms serves two primary customer groups: independent poultry farmers who need USDA-inspected processing to bring birds to market, and wholesale buyers such as restaurants, retailers, and food-service distributors. The company also works with game-bird clubs and other B2B farm partners throughout the region.

McCool has stated that the business was founded to extend his service to the domestic community, emphasizing regenerative land stewardship, humane animal handling, and transparent production. The company’s stated values—Rooted in Resilience, Integrity in Every Bite, Delivering the Promise, Pioneer Partner Empower—reflect a commitment to consistent quality and ethical farming from hatch to harvest.
