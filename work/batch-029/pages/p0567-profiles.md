**Old description:** Certified recycled polyester, takes 6.5 16 oz. plastic bottles out of oceans and landfills and turns into your new favorite shirt

## About
Kastlfel is based in Lakewood, Colorado. The company was founded with a mission to create sustainable apparel through a lifecycle approach, though the founding year and founders are not publicly disclosed.

The company designs and manufactures apparel including t-shirts, hats, and children's clothing using two proprietary fabric platforms: OrganiSoft™, made from organic and transitional cotton sourced through the Better Cotton Initiative, and RecycledSoft™, a certified recycled polyester that diverts 6.5 sixteen-ounce plastic bottles per garment from oceans and landfills. Kastlfel controls the entire manufacturing process from fiber to finish, employing environmentally conscious screen printing and low-impact certified dyes and finishes.

Kastlfel serves both direct consumers and wholesale buyers through its Kastlfel Wholesale channel, targeting the sustainable apparel and promotional products markets. Its customer base includes brands and organizations seeking verified sustainable garments with transparent supply chains.

What sets Kastlfel apart is its fiber-to-shelf model that integrates material sourcing, production, and environmental accounting under one roof. The company emphasizes transparency, compliance management, and the calculation of water, waste, and energy impacts for each product, alongside award-winning print quality achieved through eco-friendly production methods.
