The shop also runs turret punching, sawing, grinding, roll forming, and tube bending, with finishing and powder coating coordinated through local partners. A team of roughly 35 employees works across robotic welding cells, CNC machining centers, and precision press brakes.

The company serves OEMs in food processing and packaging (stainless-steel trays, molds, washdown-ready weldments), electrical and power distribution (custom enclosures, lugs, welded assemblies), industrial machinery (large welded frames and system components), data centers (server-room cabinets and hardware), material handling and logistics (conveyor supports, lift-system assemblies, brake components), and agriculture (structural weldments for soil-prep and irrigation equipment). LMI emphasizes in-house control from laser cutting through final assembly, which shortens lead times and improves traceability. Certified welds, QA tracking, and ISO 9001:2015 certification support repeatable quality, while real-time shop-floor dashboards enable accurate lead-time commitments.
