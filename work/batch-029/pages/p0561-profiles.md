## About
International Manufacturing Services, Inc. (IMS) is headquartered in Portsmouth, Rhode Island. The company was founded in 1974 by former General Electric executive H. By 1977 they had built their first dedicated facility on one acre in rural Portsmouth, and the operation has expanded continuously over the following decades. IMS remains privately owned and family-run.

IMS designs and manufactures passive electronic components, specializing in thick film and thin film resistors, attenuators, and thermal management products such as its ThermaBridge™ and TheraPlane™ series. Engineering, R&D, manufacturing, and sales operate under one roof, enabling rapid prototyping and custom solutions. The company stocks millions of off-the-shelf chip resistors and attenuators for same-day shipping, while also offering short production lead times for custom orders. Capabilities include high voltage resistors, power resistors, high frequency terminations, 100% value testing, Sn62 leaded solder pre-tinning, and packaging services (bulk, tape and reel, waffle pack).

Aerospace and defense customers use its products for satellite communications, commercial space, and military requirements including 100% value testing and heat management studies. Telecommunications and data systems clients rely on high frequency, high power terminations and attenuators with impedance control. Instrumentation customers select from ultra-high megaohm resistors, precision attenuators, and ultra-low tolerance TCR devices.

IMS emphasizes its integrated structure — engineering, development, manufacturing, and sales in one facility — as the basis for short lead times and innovative products. It claims a history of industry firsts, including aluminum nitride (AlN) components, ThermaBridge™, Teraohm chip resistors, ultra-leach resistant non-magnetic termination conductors, and SZG power terminations for high frequency applications.
