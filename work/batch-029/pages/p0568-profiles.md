## About
Kenwal Steel operates a steel service center in Lebanon, Tennessee, part of a network that has processed flat-rolled steel for over 70 years. The company is led by Chairman and CEO Stephen Eisenberg, who describes the business as a critical part of manufacturing across the nation. Corporate headquarters are in Dearborn, Michigan, where the company also runs a pickling line.

The Tennessee location provides precision processing including pickling, slitting, tension leveling, packaging, and technical support. Kenwal runs seven slitting lines across its facilities and handles hot-rolled, cold-rolled, coated, and advanced high-strength steel (AHSS) grades. Its quality and environmental management systems are certified to IATF 16949 across all processing and distribution sites.

Customers span automotive, appliance, agriculture, construction, heavy equipment, industrial manufacturing, tubing, electronics, and energy generation. The company positions itself as a supplier that delivers the right grade of steel with quick turnaround from strategically placed service centers available 24/7.

Kenwal emphasizes integrity and long-term partnerships, built on decades of steel experience. Its differentiators include the pickling line in Dearborn, the multi-line slitting capacity, and a certified quality system that aims for right-first-time results.
