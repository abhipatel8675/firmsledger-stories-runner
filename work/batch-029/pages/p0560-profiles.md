Industrial Steel & Wire Co. LLC (ISW) is a third-generation, family-owned wire distributor headquartered in Bristol, Connecticut. ISW stocks and distributes a broad range of wire products—including stainless steel, carbon steel, music wire, basic wire, non-ferrous wire, cold-heading quality, and specialty grades—across six North American service centers totaling 400,000 square feet of warehouse space. The company provides value-added processing such as torsion straightening (diameters .0300–.1250 in), cut-to-length, slitting, tension leveling, and multiple packaging options (continuous coils, catch-weight coils, reel-less cores, reels, and spools).

ISW serves manufacturers across diverse industries that require spring wire, strip products, and cold-heading wire. Its customer base includes companies needing fast, flexible supply of wire for applications ranging from automotive and aerospace components to industrial machinery, though the company does not publicly list specific vertical markets.

The company emphasizes its “ISW Pledge” of service, trust, selection, and response—principles dating to Harry Furlong’s original goal of earning customer confidence with every order.
