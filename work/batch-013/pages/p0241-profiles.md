Six B Apparel operates from Henderson, Nevada, producing custom dye-sublimated crew apparel for the racing industry. The company does not disclose its founding year, ownership structure, or the names of its founders in its public materials.

The shop specializes in all-over dye sublimation on lightweight, breathable polyester, delivering full-color, high-resolution prints that cover garments from neck to waist and sleeve to sleeve. Its process uses specially formulated dyes and a heat-transfer application that permanently bonds the image to the fabric, preventing peeling or fading through repeated washing. Products include custom crew shirts and hoodies, with an in-house art department handling design work for every order.

The primary customer base is race teams, from small grassroots outfits to larger operations, all seeking a professional, unified look. The company emphasizes that its no-minimum policy allows teams of any size to access the same quality of custom gear.

Six B Apparel positions its differentiators around three pillars: no minimum order quantities, an award-winning internal design team, and a combination of product quality, pricing, and customer service that it describes as rare in the market.
