## About
Turntech is based in Ephrata, Pennsylvania, and was founded in April 2002 by Curvin and Lucille Horning. The company began in a two-car garage with a single hydraulic copy lathe and a modest vision for custom wood turning.

Today, Turntech produces a wide range of custom wood products including columns, porch posts, mouldings, kitchen island legs, wooden balls, and dowels. The shop operates multiple CNC machines, highlighted by a custom CNC lathe capable of turning pieces up to 17½ inches in diameter and 144 inches long. Processes include reeding, rope twisting, barley twisting, and fluting, applied to wood species such as walnut, cherry, mahogany, maple, oak, cedar, pine, and poplar. The facility has expanded from its garage origins to a dedicated location in Lancaster County, though headcount and annual volume are not disclosed.

The company serves contractors restoring historic properties, homeowners updating staircases and entryways, furniture makers, stairway manufacturers, arts-and-crafts enthusiasts, and even martial-arts practitioners who use Turntech's wooden balls for training. Customers can submit photos, sketches, or CAD drawings for exact replication of existing designs.

Turntech emphasizes domestic production, made-to-order flexibility, and a typical turnaround of as little as five business days. The founders' original commitment to combining old-world craftsmanship with modern technology remains central, and the business stresses employment of local craftsmen and community involvement in Lancaster County.
