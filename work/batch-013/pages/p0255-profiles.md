# Toray Plastics (America), Inc.

**Old description:** At Toray, we carefully design thoughtful materials that surround you; materials that help keep food fresh, provide protection against the sun’s rays, make cars more luxurious to the touch, add value to industrial applications, and much more.

## About
Toray Plastics (America), Inc. is headquartered in North Kingstown, Rhode Island, with a second operation in Front Royal, Virginia housing its PEF Foam Division. The company is a wholly owned subsidiary of Toray Industries, a Tokyo-based conglomerate established in 1926. No founders are named for the U.S. entity, which operates the Torayfan® and Lumirror® film divisions from its Rhode Island campus.

The manufacturer produces polypropylene and cast polypropylene films under the Torayfan® brand, polyester films under Lumirror®, and metallized OPP, PET, and bio-based PET films. Its PEF Division extrudes polyolefin, polypropylene, and polyethylene foams. Processes include extrusion coating, casting, metallizing, and hot stamping/cold foil. The company describes its equipment as state-of-the-art and says both divisions are global leaders in their respective markets, though facility size, headcount, and volumes are not disclosed.

Toray’s films and foams are sold to converters and manufacturers that fabricate finished goods for packaging, labels, solar-control window films, technical applications, housing and engineering, and automotive markets. The company does not name specific customers but notes its materials are used in food packaging, automotive interiors, and industrial applications.

Toray states its corporate philosophy is to contribute to society through the creation of new value with innovative ideas, technologies, and products, and it emphasizes environmental, social, and governance commitments. The group highlights a sustainability vision addressing climate change, water scarcity, and resource depletion, and holds certifications including ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BRC, Sedex, ISCC PLUS, and Rhode Island Green Manufacturing Certification.
