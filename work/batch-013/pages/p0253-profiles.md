## About

TI designs, fabricates, packages, and tests analog and embedded processing chips, offering a portfolio of more than 80,000 products. Its global manufacturing network includes 300 mm wafer fabs that run on 100 % renewable electricity, and the company reports producing tens of billions of chips each year. With roughly 33,000 employees and over 100,000 customers, TI handles everything from process technology and design through final test and delivery.

The company’s components serve automotive, industrial, consumer electronics, communications, data center, personal electronics, and medical equipment markets. Engineers at automakers, industrial automation firms, smartphone makers, and data‑center operators rely on TI’s analog ICs, microcontrollers, and wireless transceivers for applications ranging from vehicle electrification to AI‑driven servers.

TI says its founding purpose was to make electronics more affordable through semiconductors, a mission that still guides its three core ambitions: act like long‑term owners, adapt to a changing world, and build a company its people are proud of.
