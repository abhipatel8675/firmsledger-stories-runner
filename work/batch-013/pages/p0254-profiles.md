# The Plastics Group of America

**Old description:** Offering custom compounding to meet customer’s needs since 1973

## About
The Plastics Group of America (TPG) is headquartered in Woonsocket, Rhode Island. The company traces its roots to 1973 when Bob Lebeaux founded Ralco Industries, Inc., a one-man operation buying and selling scrap plastics. In 1978 the firm added a grinder, baler, extruder and tractor‑trailer to enter recycling, and in 1980 began custom compounding reinforced thermoplastics under the Polifil® trade name. The business was renamed The Plastics Group of America in 1988, consolidating Ralco, Polifil® and the Transcontinental Polymers recycling unit. Today the company remains family‑led, with Lebeaux family members holding key executive and operational roles.

TPG operates more than 300,000 square feet of manufacturing space in Woonsocket, including the former Jules Desurmont Mill complex acquired in 2012. With a workforce of 60‑plus employees and annual revenue exceeding $50 million, the company custom‑compounds mineral‑ and glass‑filled polypropylene resins to precise mechanical, thermal and physical specifications. Its on‑site quality laboratory performs melt index, Izod, flexural, tensile, DSC, moisture, ash, Gardner impact and color testing on every batch. Beyond compounding, TPG provides recycling and toll‑processing services, color matching at no charge, and acts as a resin broker and distributor with stock ready for shipment.

The company serves processors in appliance, automotive, construction, consumer housewares, furniture, industrial/institutional/electronic, medical and personal care, packaging, and pump/pool/spa/irrigation markets. Customers include injection molders, extruders and blow‑molders who rely on TPG’s Polifil® resins for consistent performance in demanding applications.

TPG emphasizes its asset‑based manufacturing model, ISO 9001 certification and an on‑site testing lab as differentiators that enable tight lot‑to‑lot consistency whether the order is 100 pounds or one million pounds. The firm also highlights its recycling expertise, free color compounding, and a technical insights library that helps customers optimize molding processes.
