# The Plastics Group of America

**Old description:** Offering custom compounding to meet customer’s needs since 1973

## About
The Plastics Group of America (TPG) is headquartered in Woonsocket, Rhode Island, where it was founded in 1973 by Bob Lebeaux as Ralco Industries, a one-man scrap trading operation. The company remains family-owned, with second-generation leadership including President and CEO Marti Lebeaux and Executive Vice President Michael Rosenthal, who joined in 1979. The business adopted its current name in 1988 after merging Ralco, the Polifil® compounding line, and the Transcontinental Polymers recycling unit.

TPG operates over 300,000 square feet of manufacturing space in Woonsocket and runs a 24/7 production schedule. Its core business is custom compounding of Polifil® polypropylene-based reinforced resins, using mineral, talc, calcium, glass, and barium additives to meet specific physical, mechanical, and thermal requirements. An on-site quality control laboratory equipped with melt index, Izod, flexural, tensile, DSC, moisture, ash, Gardner impact, and color testing instruments certifies every batch, whether the order is 100 pounds or one million pounds. The company also provides recycling and toll processing services, densifying, guillotining, and extruding scrap into reusable material, and acts as a resin broker and distributor with stocking points across the continental United States.

The compounded resins serve processors in appliance, automotive, construction, consumer housewares and disposables, furniture, industrial/institutional/electronic, medical and personal care, packaging, and pump/pool/spa/irrigation markets. TPG's ISO 9001-certified facility supplies molders, extruders, and blow-molding operations that require consistent, specification-grade thermoplastics.

TPG emphasizes its asset-based manufacturing model combined with a full-service approach: free commercial color matching, technical consulting through its TPGOA Technical Insights library, and a commitment to on-time, on-budget delivery. The company traces its differentiation to the 1980 launch of custom compounding under the Polifil® brand and its early adoption of ISO 9002 certification in 1996, followed by an OSHA SHARP award in 1997.

## About
Based in Arab, Alabama, The Wooden Nickel builds custom live edge furniture from locally sourced hardwood slabs. The company's founding year and ownership structure are not publicly disclosed; it operates as a shop where each piece begins with a slab selected for its natural character.

The shop produces dining tables, desks, adjustable workstations, and contract-grade furniture for commercial spaces. The process is deliberately slow: logs from tree services and loggers are kiln-dried and stabilized, then flattened, sanded, and inspected for cracks or knots that may receive resin filling. Finishes are applied in multiple hand-rubbed coats, and steel bases—fabricated from high-tolerance steel—are fitted to ensure level, wobble-free assembly. The company notes that most production is custom, ranging from single items to larger commercial orders.

Customers include residential clients such as a Marshall County family that commissioned a three-generation dining table, as well as local businesses needing tables and work surfaces for offices, break rooms, and public areas. The firm has supplied several area companies with thicker slabs, reinforced bases, and durable finishes designed for daily wear.

The Wooden Nickel emphasizes careful material selection, honest construction, and an unhurried timeline. As the owners describe it, the goal is furniture that 'doesn't draw attention to itself' but 'just keeps working year after year'—built to be used, built to last, and built for real life.
