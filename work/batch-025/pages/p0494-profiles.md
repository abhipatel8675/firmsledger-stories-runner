## About
Automotive Test Solutions was established in 2001 in Albuquerque, New Mexico, by Bernie C. Thompson and Neal R. Pederson. The company began as a developer of patented diagnostic tools for the automotive sector.

The company manufactures a range of diagnostic equipment including multi-channel oscilloscopes (eSCOPE series), OBD II drivability scan tools (eSCAN), misfire detectors, pressure transducers, vibration analyzers, leak detectors, gas analyzers, borescopes, engine simulators, ignition analyzers, and training products. Electronic and software design is supported through its affiliate VI Control Systems, which has developed controls for lasers, particle accelerators, and nuclear reactors. A mobile diagnostic service, ATS Mobile Diagnostics, also operates under the same ownership.

Products are sold to automotive repair technicians, independent shops, and original equipment manufacturers; the eSCOPE ELITE8 carries a GM-approved tool designation. The company’s tools have been recognized with awards from Motor Magazine, TST, Undercar Digest, Transmission Digest, and the Mobile Air Conditioning Society spanning 2004 through 2021.
