# BIO El Paso - Juarez

**Old description:** The Frontier of Innovation: Biomedical Manufacturing on the Border

## About
BIO El Paso–Juárez operates as a binational industry cluster organization headquartered in El Paso, Texas. Formed as a 501(c)(6) nonprofit under the Medical Center of the Americas Foundation, it was established to strengthen the medical device sector spanning the U.S.–Mexico border. A board led by Chair Julio Chiu and Vice Chair Mylena Walker governs the initiative, which formally links the Medical Center of the Americas Foundation with the Clúster Biomédico de Juárez.

The organization does not manufacture products. Instead, it convenes manufacturers, government agencies, academic institutions, and nonprofits to develop regional capabilities, map supply-chain competencies, and monitor cluster growth. Its programs create collaborative networks, promote the region’s biomedical ecosystem globally, and leverage the strategic El Paso–Juárez location to optimize cost, efficiency, and quality for member companies.

Stakeholders include medical device OEMs, contract manufacturers, suppliers, and research centers on both sides of the border. By connecting these entities across boundaries, the cluster aims to accelerate the formation, expansion, and retention of biomedical enterprises while improving healthcare quality in the region.

The initiative was launched to address fragmentation in the border region’s biomedical base, providing a unified platform that enhances global competitiveness through coordinated innovation, workforce development, and cross-border supply-chain integration.
