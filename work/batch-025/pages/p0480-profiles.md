# Acadiana Plastics Molding

**Old description:** Acadiana Plastics Molding thrives on innovation and unparalleled customer satisfaction.

## About
Acadiana Plastics Molding operates from New Iberia, Louisiana, where it was founded in 1989 by Kent Byron. The company remains locally owned and operated, with Byron serving as president and chief executive officer. What began as a three-person venture aimed at filling a regional gap—Louisiana food producers previously had to source injection-molded closures from out of state—has grown into a nearly 100-employee operation.

The 22-acre facility houses injection molding presses ranging from 60 to 500 tons, capable of producing parts from 0.05 grams to over 1,000 grams. Annual output exceeds 500 million closures in sizes from 10 mm to 48 mm, including continuous-thread and tamper-evident styles with custom lining options. The product line also covers 19 standard PET bottle styles and the patented STAT® Vial, a unit-dose heat-seal package for the respiratory market, supported by an in-house vial printing process. Engineering and design consulting are offered alongside molding.

Primary markets include the pepper sauce and condiment sector, where the company says it dominates closure sales, as well as respiratory pharmaceutical packaging and craft beverage brands seeking custom containers. The original customer base was Louisiana food companies, but the reach has expanded nationally.

Byron started the business after recognizing that no Southern Louisiana shop specialized in injection-molded plastic closures, forcing local food manufacturers to look elsewhere. The company emphasizes a partnership-oriented service model rather than a purely transactional one, a philosophy it traces back to its founding vision.

# Accumold

**Old description:** Bring your ideal micro part design and let Accumold be your key to success.

## About
Accumold operates from a 15-acre campus in Ankeny, Iowa, just north of Des Moines. The company traces its roots to a rented garage where its founders recognized a growing demand for micro injection molded components that exceeded the capabilities of conventional molding systems. While the exact founding year is not publicly stated, the firm notes four decades of exclusive focus on micro molding and tooling.

Accumold provides vertically integrated micro manufacturing, encompassing tooling, injection molding, micro 3D printing, packaging, and metrology under one roof. Its core process is high-precision thermoplastic injection molding for parts with micron-level tolerances, complemented by additive manufacturing for rapid prototyping. The campus houses advanced metrology including a Zygo NewView 8300 optical profiler, OGP multi-sensor systems, and Keyence vision inspection, enabling measurement of features down to tenths of a micron. The company also offers design for micro molding (DfMM), process validation, and Gage R&R studies.

The company serves medical device OEMs producing components for endoscopes, diagnostics, and implantable sensors; micro-electronics manufacturers needing connectors and lead frames; polymer optics firms requiring micro-lenses and fiber arrays; automotive electronics suppliers pursuing high-volume micro-systems; and emerging technology developers in wearables and smart devices. Materials range from medical-grade biocompatible resins (ISO 10993) to engineering thermoplastics such as ABS, PVC, PMMA, and nylon equivalents.

Accumold differentiates itself by embedding metrology into every production stage, asserting that "if you cannot measure it, you cannot make it." Its vertically integrated model ensures that tooling, molding, and inspection are co-located, allowing rapid iteration and consistent quality at micro scale. The firm emphasizes continuous investment in measurement technology and automation to maintain precision as parts shrink further.
