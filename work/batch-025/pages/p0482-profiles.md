## About
Ahner Industrial Fabricating & Sheet Metal operates a 45,000-square-foot fabrication facility in Sandusky, Ohio. The family-owned shop traces its roots to the Ahner Sheet Metal business that has been part of the Sandusky industrial landscape for decades, though a specific founding year is not publicly stated. Today the company runs cutting, forming, welding, and assembly under one roof, supported by 2- to 5-ton overhead cranes and multiple jib cranes for handling large weldments and structural assemblies.

The shop focuses on short-run and repeat production of metal components, from laser-cut and formed brackets to full industrial ductwork packages and large welded frames for equipment such as heater houses. Capabilities span laser cutting, press-brake forming, MIG/TIG welding, finishing, hardware installation, and final assembly. Work can start from a complete CAD model, a 2D drawing, a photograph, a hand sketch, or an existing sample part that needs replication.

Ahner serves a broad industrial base including automotive, electronics, air handling, material handling, lighting, food processing, industrial machinery, amusement parks, steel and foundry operations, construction, general manufacturing, injection molding, chemical, marine, containers, oil and energy, and quarry operations. Typical projects involve replacement parts for legacy equipment, pilot builds for new designs, and ongoing production releases for OEMs and maintenance departments.

The company emphasizes a single-point-of-contact approach: customers work directly with the fabrication team to define process scope, critical fits, material selection, inspection requirements, and delivery logistics before any work is committed. An online Price CAD tool supports instant quoting for production-ready sheet metal parts, while custom requests move through a structured review of manufacturability and timing.
