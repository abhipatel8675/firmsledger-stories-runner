## About
Founded in October 1977 in Gallatin, Tennessee, Associated Packaging, Inc. has grown into a national provider of end-of-line packaging solutions. The company operates 12 branches across the United States with over 140 packaging specialists serving customers from coast to coast.

The firm supplies packaging materials and equipment while providing a suite of technical services that include onsite start-up, equipment integration, consulting, and machine reconditioning to industry standards. A 3:1 ratio of sales staff to field service technicians supports a proactive service model, and the company's automation and integration team combines packaging equipment with material handling expertise. Additional capabilities span continuous improvement resources, wire partitioning, productivity enhancements, and software and hardware controls.

Associated Packaging serves defense contractors and other industrial customers requiring end-of-line packaging for small, medium, and large-scale production. Its specialists apply LEAN/Six Sigma, automation, materials, and machinery knowledge to analyze existing processes and collaborate on custom solutions.

The company emphasizes its unbiased industry expertise and a listen-first methodology—listen, analyze, document, collaborate—as the foundation for delivering specialized programs that add value and support growth. Unparalleled technical service and a national footprint with local impact are cited as key differentiators.

## About
Atico Furniture operates from a studio and showroom in Farmers Branch, Texas, a suburb of Dallas. The company was founded in 2011 when its principals began building handmade industrial furniture from leftover materials from a home reconstruction project. Ownership is held by Sarah Breitkreutz, who serves as Owner and Director of Sales, Marketing, and Client Relations, and William Howard, Operating Owner and President.

The company designs, fabricates, and installs custom furniture for residential and commercial spaces. Its capabilities span woodworking, metal fabrication, powder coating, and on-site installation. Work is performed at the Dallas-area studio, where a team of artisans handcrafts each piece using premium solid wood and steel. The shop also offers powder coating finishes and manages full installation, including field adjustments.

Atico serves interior designers, architects, and direct clients across commercial office and residential markets. Projects have included custom desks, reception desks, conference tables, built-in shelving, freestanding work tables, dining tables, and bookcases for offices, conference rooms, break rooms, and private homes. Wholesale and designer trade programs are available.

The company emphasizes a blend of sustainability and craftsmanship, using reclaimed materials where possible and guaranteeing workmanship for one year. Its differentiators include personalized design consultation, handcrafted construction, and an installation crew that arrives equipped for on-site modifications.
