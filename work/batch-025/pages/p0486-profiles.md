The company began with three Battenfeld injection molding machines and five customers. Now led by third-generation president Wolf Tetzlaff, the family-owned business operates a second facility in Sherwood, Oregon, acquired in 2016.

The company provides turnkey contract manufacturing, combining plastic injection molding, pad printing, tool design, and final assembly under one roof. Its Alabama and Oregon plants run Cincinnati Milacron presses ranging from 33 to 165 tons, including a Roboshot unit, and a large-format six-color pad printer added in 2024. Amdek handles short-run prototypes through high-volume production, with secondary operations and assembly integration.

Amdek serves medical, automotive, electronics, and industrial markets. A noted customer is Wilson Sporting Goods, for which the company has printed custom logos since 2009. The firm also produces its own Earth Friendly Cutlery line of compostable utensils.

Amdek emphasizes its BBB accreditation, dual-location logistics for faster delivery, and a design-first approach that optimizes parts before tooling. The company traces its roots to Robert Tetzlaff’s early career selling molding and pad-printing equipment, a background that shaped its focus on integrated decorating and molding solutions.
