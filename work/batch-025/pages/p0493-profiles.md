**Old description:** Custom switchboards, panelboards, and electrical distribution equipment; largest WBE manufacturer of its kind in NY

## About
Atlas Switch Co., Inc. is headquartered in Garden City, New York. The company is certified as a Women's Business Enterprise (WBE) and identifies as the largest woman-owned manufacturer of electrical service and distribution equipment in New York.

The company custom manufactures panel boards and switchboards to meet project-specific requirements and building space constraints. Its product line includes panel boards with integral metering and integral surge protective devices (SPD), as well as switchboards. Atlas Switch also supplies transformers, disconnect switches, busway, and metering solutions through partnerships with other manufacturers.

Atlas Switch serves residential, commercial, and industrial construction projects across New York State, with extended reach into New Jersey, Connecticut, Pennsylvania, and Massachusetts via partners. Its client roster includes the NYC Port Authority, Mount Sinai Health System, New York University, Northwell Health, JP Morgan Chase, Citibank, Google, Amazon, Facebook, and Bloomberg. The company has contributed to major developments such as World Trade Center Tower 4, Yankee Stadium, Madison Square Garden, the TWA Hotel at JFK, and the Hudson Yards project.

Atlas Switch emphasizes its status as the largest WBE manufacturer of its kind in New York and its ability to deliver custom electrical distribution equipment tailored to unique space constraints. The firm was founded to address the need for specialized, project-specific switchgear in the dense New York City building environment.
