## About
Tri-Mack Plastics Manufacturing Corporation was founded in 1974 in Bristol, Rhode Island. The company began as a supplier of precision thermoplastic components to the aerospace industry.

The company provides vertically integrated manufacturing services including thermoplastic composites processing, injection molding, CNC machining, engineering support, and material selection. Its capabilities span from concept through production, with in-house quality inspection using CMMs, ultrasonic NDT, and in-machine probing on CNC milling centers.

Tri-Mack serves aerospace, defense, industrial, and medical markets. Aerospace OEMs such as GE Aerospace have recognized the company with Best in Class and Supplier Quality Awareness awards. Defense customers rely on the firm for precision components meeting complex regulatory standards. Industrial clients use materials like Victrex PEEK, Torlon PAI, Ultem PEI, and Ryton PPS for equipment assemblies. Medical equipment manufacturers depend on Tri-Mack for components that ensure accuracy and meet rigorous regulatory requirements.

The company differentiates itself through a quality management system certified to AS9100, ISO 9001, and NADCAP, which governs lot traceability, material certifications, and approved vendor requirements. On-staff designated supplier quality representatives (DSQRs) enable direct ship-to-stock for many customers.
