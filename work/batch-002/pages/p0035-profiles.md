The company operates a showroom stocked with solid wood furniture sourced from a rotating list of suppliers selected by the family's staff. Unlike many furniture stores, Bare Wood allows customers to take purchased pieces home the same day, eliminating wait times for special orders. The inventory spans a range of styles and sizes, all priced to remain affordable year-round.

The retailer serves residential customers across the Acadiana region, focusing on households seeking durable, heirloom-quality furnishings. Its customer base includes repeat buyers who return for additional pieces and referrals, reflecting the company's emphasis on long-term relationships.

The business was founded on the belief that high-quality solid wood furniture should be accessible and lasting. The family highlights its commitment to customer satisfaction through a guarantee that extends beyond the sale, offering support for repairs or issues after delivery.
