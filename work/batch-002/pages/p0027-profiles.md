## About
AMP Welding & Metal Fabrication is a privately owned welding and fabrication company founded in 2018 in Daphne, Alabama. The business was started by Alvon Portis, who brought over 20 years of experience in the commercial and residential building industries into a full‑time steel, aluminum, and metallurgical production operation. Today the shop manages projects across Alabama, Mississippi, and parts of Florida.

The company operates as a custom production metal shop, offering a wide range of welding processes — including MIG, TIG, stick, laser, orbital, flux‑core, and specialized techniques for stainless steel, cast iron, pipeline, and underwater applications — alongside fabrication services such as metal fabrication, stainless steel fabrication, industrial and heavy metal fabrication, staircase and handrail fabrication, plasma cutting, and powder coating.
