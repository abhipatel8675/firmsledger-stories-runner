American Leather sells through more than 700 retail partners across the United States and supplies contract-approved collections for commercial and hospitality projects. Its customer base includes independent furniture retailers, interior designers, and contract specifiers who require customizable, domestically produced seating.

The business was started to compress lead times from concept to delivery, and it continues to emphasize speed, quality, and customization. A central U.S. location reduces shipping time and carbon footprint, while a workforce representing 35 countries supports a culture of innovation and sustainable manufacturing.
