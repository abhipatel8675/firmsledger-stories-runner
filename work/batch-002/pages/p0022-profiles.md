## About
TE Wire & Cable LLC operates from Saddle Brook, New Jersey, where it has manufactured thermocouple and specialty wire since 1941. The business originated as the Wire and Cable Division of Thermo Electric Corporation, created to supply high‑quality wire for temperature‑measurement markets spanning cryogenic to 1,300 °C applications. Today it is a subsidiary of Marmon Wire & Cable, a Berkshire Hathaway company.

The company produces a full line of thermocouple wire and extension cable from iron‑ and nickel‑based alloys and copper, with insulation ranging from PVC to CEFIR® ceramic fiber braids. It also provides custom cable assembly, wire assembly, and box‑build services. An in‑house calibration laboratory, directly certified by NIST and accredited to ISO/IEC 17025:2017, calibrates and matches wire from ‑320 °F to 2,400 °F, supporting compliance with AMS2750, ASTM E220, BAC5621 and other aerospace and industrial specifications.

Customers include aerospace composites manufacturers, industrial OEMs, steel and aluminum metallurgy plants, medical device makers, aviation engine test facilities, and power generation operators. The wire serves temperature‑sensing and control circuits in environments from cryogenic furnaces to high‑temperature engine testing.

TE Wire & Cable emphasizes its position as the world’s largest thermocouple wire manufacturer, citing short lead times, competitive pricing, and a calibration program verified against U.S., British, and Dutch national standards as key differentiators.
