Primary customers are wood artists and furniture makers who value unique grain and color consistency across a project. The flitch-drying method allows clients to build entire pieces from matching lumber. The company also seeks partnerships with charitable organizations in need of lumber.

Nashville Wood differentiates itself by avoiding kiln drying to preserve natural wood colors, by keeping flitches intact for grain matching, and by selling exclusively live-edge slabs that let the end user control the final cut.
