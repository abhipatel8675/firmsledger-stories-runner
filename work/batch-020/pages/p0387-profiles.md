Core capabilities include bridgewire and ordnance products, electromechanical assemblies, cable and harness assemblies, and contract manufacturing. Processes encompass multi-conductor instrument cable, coaxial cable, specialty power cables, multi-point harnesses, and molded rubber components for turnkey assemblies.

Mohawk serves the defense, oil and gas, pyrotechnics, instrumentation, mining, and construction industries. Its products are used in military applications such as the M18A1 and M68 Claymore weapon systems, as well as in commercial explosive products for coal mining, dam construction, roads, railways, and other large projects. Mohawk emphasizes a reputation for superior quality and reliability of detonators in military applications. Its quality assurance program includes receiving inspection of all materials, infrared and video inspection technology, statistical process control during production, 100% line and final product inspection, and packing and shipping inspection.
