# OSH Cut

**Old description:** Get 2-day delivery throughout the US for less than the typical price of ground shipping.

## About
OSH Cut operates from Spanish Fork, Utah, as an on-demand metal fabrication shop. The company's founding year and ownership structure are not publicly disclosed, nor are its founders named in available sources.

The shop runs a digital-first production line centered on laser cutting, tube laser cutting, bending, tapping, and flat finishing. Cutting is performed on 10 kW Trumpf 3030 fiber lasers, a 7 kW Trumpf 7000 tube laser, and an 8 kW Mazak Optiplex, all driven by in-house software that ties the online quoting and DFM engine directly to the machine controllers. Deburring uses a 120-grit wide-belt sander and graining a 240-grit belt. Bending capacity reaches 3/4 inch thickness in steel, stainless, aluminum, and copper; tapping covers 6-32 through 1 1/2-6 and M3 through M36. Powder coating is also offered. Standard cutting tolerance is ±0.005 inch.

Customers span aerospace, medical devices, astronomy, and the FIRST Robotics FRC community, alongside general prototyping and low-volume production buyers. The platform accepts a wide range of CAD files—including STEP, SLDPRT, DXF, and SVG—and ships to all 50 states via parcel or LTL freight.

OSH Cut emphasizes its instant quoting app with real-time DFM feedback and forming simulation, which lets users upload complex geometries that other vendors reject. The company advertises two-day delivery across the United States and positions its software-driven workflow as the primary differentiator.
