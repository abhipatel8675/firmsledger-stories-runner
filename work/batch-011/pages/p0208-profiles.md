## About
Micro-Precision Technologies, Inc. (MPT) was founded in 1998 in Salem, New Hampshire. The company focuses on thick-film hybrid microelectronics and semiconductor packaging designed for harsh environments.

MPT produces thick-film circuits screened onto ceramic substrates such as alumina, aluminum nitride, and beryllium oxide, offering a robust alternative to traditional FR-4 printed circuit boards. Its capabilities include FR-4 to ceramic substrate conversion, hybrid assembly with both manual and automated surface-mount technology, wire bonding (ball and wedge), die attach with automated dispensing for epoxy, encapsulant, or optical fluid, and semiconductor die repackaging for improved thermal performance or pinout configuration. All assembly work is performed in a Class 10,000 cleanroom, and the company supports new product introduction as well as consigned or full turnkey assembly.

The company serves industrial, military, and medical markets. Its products are used in applications that demand operation under extreme heat, high vibration, downhole mining conditions, deep-sea wells, and space environments, including power semiconductor packaging challenges.

MPT emphasizes the inherent thermal management of ceramic substrates, which dissipate heat more efficiently than epoxy-based FR-4, and maintains certifications including MIL-PRF-38534 Class H qualification since 1998, QML-38534 listing, DSCC certification, AS9100D, ISO 9001:2015, and ITAR registration.
