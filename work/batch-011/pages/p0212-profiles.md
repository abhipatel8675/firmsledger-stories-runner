**Old description:** Molded Products was founded in 1986, initially focused on providing rubber molded goods for the automotive aftermarket.

## About
Molded Products Inc was founded in 1986 in Broken Arrow, Oklahoma, initially supplying rubber molded goods to the automotive aftermarket. The company began as a focused rubber molding operation and has since expanded its capabilities.

Today the company operates as a diversified custom rubber molder specializing in compression molding, transfer molding, and rubber-to-metal bonding. Its 22 hydraulic presses range up to 600 tons, supported by in-house tool and die machining, preforming equipment (including 60-inch and 50-inch mills, Barwell extruders), finishing lines with cryogenic deflashing, a paint center, and general machining. The facility runs 24 hours a day, five days a week, enabling quick turnaround on urgent jobs.

Molded Products serves a range of industrial markets including oil and gas, construction, fuel-cell seals, electrical, automotive and aftermarket, aerospace, and air filtration. Customers rely on the shop for turn-key solutions that span concept, tooling design, prototype development, and full production runs.

The company emphasizes its ISO 9001:2015 certified quality system, in-house tooling control, and a reputation for delivering quality, timely, and cost-competitive rubber components. Its founding aim was to provide reliable molded rubber parts, and it has maintained that focus while broadening its industrial reach.

## About
Moulstown Woodworks operates from a log yard in the Pigeon Hills of York County, just east of Abbottstown, Pennsylvania. The business is run by Wade, who offers a mobile sawmilling service under the motto “can mill, will travel.” No founding year or ownership structure is disclosed on the company’s website.

The core service is on-site portable sawmilling using a 2021 Woodmizer LT40, a fully hydraulic commercial mill with a 34-inch cutting width and capacity for logs up to 20 feet long. The mill can lift and turn logs up to 4,000 pounds; heavier logs require crane assistance. Typical output runs 250–300 board feet per hour, with milling priced at $75 per hour plus a transportation and setup fee starting at $85 for sites within 10 miles of the yard. The company also provides yield estimates and pre‑job site visits.

Customers include landowners, arborists, excavators, forestry consultants, lumber buyers, kiln operators, furniture makers, carpenters, and luthiers across south‑central Pennsylvania (Adams, Cumberland, Perry, Lancaster, Chester, and York counties) and northern Maryland (Frederick, Carroll, Baltimore, and Hartford counties). Repeat clients are noted in towns such as Gettysburg, Hanover, New Oxford, and Westminster.

The business emphasizes customer preparation—clean, metal‑free logs staged for the mill, adequate labor to clear slabs, and equipment to move heavy logs—as the key to efficient yield. The mobile model eliminates the need for customers to transport logs to a fixed mill.
