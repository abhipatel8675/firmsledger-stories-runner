Typical output runs 250–300 board feet per hour, with service charges around $0.38–$0.42 per board foot for an eight-hour day, plus a transportation and setup fee starting at $85 within 10 miles of the Pigeon Hills.

The company serves a network of arborists, excavators, forestry consultants, lumber distributors, kiln operators, crane services, furniture makers, framing carpenters, cabinet shops, and luthiers across Adams, Cumberland, Perry, Lancaster, Chester, and York counties in Pennsylvania, as well as Frederick, Carroll, Baltimore, and Hartford counties in Maryland.

The company emphasizes a 'can mill, will travel' approach, stressing that proper site preparation—clean, metal-free logs staged for setup, adequate labor to clear slabs and lumber, and equipment to move heavy logs—enables the highest yield and efficiency.
