**Old description:** Professional-grade welding tables, modular booths, and precision tooling engineered for shops that demand more. Every product is designed, cut, and welded at our facility in Texas.

## About
Texas Metal Works operates from a 40,000-square-foot, climate-controlled facility in Conroe, Texas. The company was founded in 2017 by brothers John and Kirk Pozadzides, who started the business after failing to find quality, affordable American-made fabrication tables for their own shop. They built the first tables for themselves, then brought them to market.

The company manufactures professional-grade welding tables, modular welding booths, heavy-duty workbenches, plasma cutting tables, DIY welding kits, and the ModPro modular storage system. Production runs on precision laser cutters, CNC press brakes, and robotic welding cells, with in-house powder coating available in over 200 colors. Texas Metal Works offers more than 40 standard table variants in sizes from 30″×30″ to 70″×140″, all cut from ASTM A572-50 structural steel milled in the U.S. It also produces the only readily available stainless-steel fabrication table line in the domestic market.

Customers range from hobbyist garages and small fabrication shops to large production facilities, as well as school districts, community colleges, and CTE programs nationwide. The stainless tables serve contamination-sensitive sectors such as food-grade, pharmaceutical, aerospace, and high-purity welding. Structural steel fabrication services support commercial, industrial, and institutional projects.

Texas Metal Works emphasizes its ISO 9001 certified quality system, AWS-certified welders, and a 100% debt-free, brother-owned structure that answers only to customers. Manufacturing, fabrication, shipping, and U.S.-based support all operate under one roof, allowing control over tolerances, flatness, and lead times without third-party handoffs.

## About

After working for a local rag merchant, Hyman launched H. Brickle & Son, Inc. to trade recycled fibers between textile mills, becoming the first woolen waste dealer in the South and establishing a foundation built on material recovery.

Today the group operates six divisions. Bouckaert Industrial Textiles custom‑engineers needle‑punch nonwoven felts. Northwest Woolen Mills has produced wool and synthetic blankets for 68 years, supplying army blankets and emergency‑relief blankets for humanitarian crises worldwide. H. Brickle Fiber Trading & Recycling purchases mill waste, recycles it, and spins woolen yarns (including 2‑ to 4‑ply constructions) for other textile manufacturers. F&M Dyeing Corp provides commercial‑quality dyeing and fulling for wool and fur felt hat bodies. Brickle Realty Group manages and develops industrial and commercial properties, while Heat Smart assembles energy‑assistance kits — wool blankets, weatherstripping, and conservation tips — for low‑income households.

Customers span textile mills, hat manufacturers, government agencies (including the U.S. Department of Energy), disaster‑relief organizations, and senior‑energy programs. The group's blankets have been deployed in natural disasters and political conflicts globally, and its fiber‑recycling services feed raw material back into the textile supply chain.

The company credits its longevity to diversification — acquiring Northwest Woolen Mills in 1978 and Bouckaert Industrial Textiles in 2000 — and to a mission of developing tailored products while maintaining a culture rooted in family, quality of life, and performance.
