# O'Donnell Metal Fabricators, Inc.

**Old description:** Since 1981, O’Donnell Metal Fabricators Inc. has served as an all-encompassing design and fabrication partner dedicated to helping individuals and businesses enhance their products from concept to creation. With our fully in-house approach, every process is handled by our specialized departments, all of which adhere to the rigorous standards and procedures set by ODMF. This consistency ensures quality parts, streamlined communication, and outcomes that build client success.

## About
O'Donnell Metal Fabricators, Inc. was founded in 1981 in Norristown, Pennsylvania, as a family-owned, American-made metal fabrication shop. The company began as a design and fabrication partner for OEMs, contractors, and plant managers, and has remained in the same location for over four decades.

The shop provides fully in-house metal fabrication, including CNC fiber laser cutting (up to 1-inch mild steel), CNC plasma cutting (up to 4-inch thick stainless, carbon steel, and aluminum), press brake forming with a 175-ton Cincinnati press brake (12-foot bed, 60-by-144-inch max sheet), shearing, sheet rolling (hot and cold), punch press work, welding (MIG, TIG, stick), and finishing such as powder coating and zinc plating. Production runs from single prototypes to full-scale volumes, with a stated laser tolerance of ±0.005 inch.

The company serves aerospace, automotive, military and defense, manufacturing, oil and energy, medical and pharmaceutical, facility maintenance, agriculture, food processing, construction, food service, hospitality, and commercial kitchen sectors. Customers include OEMs, engineers, architects, and contractors across Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware, and Maryland.

O'Donnell differentiates itself through value engineering to reduce cost and improve function, a single-roof process that eliminates vendor handoffs, dedicated project managers who maintain visibility at every stage, and certifications that include AWS, NSF, and UL. The family-owned structure emphasizes competitive pricing, short lead times, and integrity throughout each build.
