## About

The family-owned business expanded steadily, acquiring plants across the Midwest and South, and by 2022 marked 90 years of continuous operation.

The company manufactures underground and aboveground steel storage tanks, ASME-code pressure vessels, and LPG tanks for flammable and combustible liquids and gases. Its Elizabethtown facility specializes in custom plate work, chute fabrication, wear products, and structural steel fabrication. Modern Welding runs ten tank and vessel fabrication subsidiaries nationwide, two dedicated structural steel plants, and four welding supply stores in Kentucky and Indiana, all linked by a shared information network.

Customers span energy, construction, distillery, agriculture, defense, mining, chemical processing, and petroleum storage.
