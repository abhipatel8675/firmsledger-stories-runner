## About

A move to a new 50,000-square-foot facility in La Vista, a suburb of Omaha, came in 2004, and in 2017 EDM became part of Pivot International, a global product development and manufacturing network.

The La Vista plant provides 50,000 square feet of ESD-protected, temperature- and humidity-controlled production space with 24/7 security monitoring. EDM offers PCB and electromechanical assembly, rapid-turn prototyping, turnkey and box-build solutions, supply-chain management, global sourcing, RoHS and lead-free conversions, rework and modification, engineering and design, service depot support, and encapsulation and coating. The company handles jobs from a single prototype through full turnkey production with direct fulfillment.

EDM serves customers in medical device manufacturing, evidenced by its ISO 13485:2016 certification, and in equipment for potentially explosive environments, supported by ISO 80079-34:2018. Its client base includes original equipment manufacturers seeking scalable electronics production and design support.

Founded to bring high-quality electronics manufacturing to the Midwest, EDM emphasizes a climate-controlled, secure facility near major highways, a global footprint through Pivot International, and a commitment to supporting customers from prototype to volume production.

Wearable and consumer electronics programs leverage the firm's design expertise for miniaturized, high-reliability interconnects.
