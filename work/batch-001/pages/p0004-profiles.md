## About
Calumet Electronics Corporation was founded in 1968 in Calumet, Michigan, in the state's Upper Peninsula. The company has remained 100% American owned and operated since its inception, with no offshoring or brokering of production.

The company designs, engineers, and manufactures high-reliability printed circuit boards from a 160,000-square-foot facility. Its capabilities span conventional, HDI, and HDBU fabrication, including 6- to 30-layer high-density multilayer packages with blind and buried vias, stacked microvias, and buried passives. Processes include sequential lamination (up to five or more cycles), bridge plating, via-in-pad (non-conductive, conductive, and copper-filled), laser and LED direct imaging, digital solder mask and silkscreen, mechanical milling, and edge castellation. The shop handles panel sizes up to 19.5 by 22.5 inches and supports copper weights up to 5 oz plated.

Calumet serves aerospace, defense, medical device, communications, electric power, industrial controls, space, and national security sectors. Its boards are used in U.S. military equipment, electric power grid protection, space applications, and life-saving medical devices.

The company emphasizes its fully domestic supply chain—design, engineering, and manufacturing all occur in the U.S. with no offshoring. It positions itself as a safe harbor for American electronics companies and has received Department of Defense funding to advance PCB technology. Its focus is on zero-defect reliability for mission-critical applications.
