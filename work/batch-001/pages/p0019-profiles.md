## About
Redstar Worldwide was founded in 2007 and is headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah. The company operates as a single-source printed circuit board (PCB) solutions provider, coordinating with both domestic and international manufacturing partners to deliver boards from prototype through production.

The firm offers a range of PCB fabrication services including quick-turn prototyping, high-density interconnect (HDI) technology, rigid-flex and flexible circuits, metal-core boards, and high-temperature materials. Capabilities span single- and double-sided boards up to 36-layer multilayer constructions. Engineering support covers design for manufacturability, specialized coatings, through-hole plating, and coverlay application.

Redstar serves customers in green energy and solar, medical equipment, telecommunications, and automotive sectors. Its client base includes a global audio and infotainment company, an electronics manufacturing services (EMS) provider, and other global OEMs that have relied on the company since its founding year.

The company emphasizes 24/7 communication across domestic and offshore teams, a commitment to matching price and quality, and a hassle-free process from design to destination. These differentiators were highlighted in customer testimonials citing responsiveness, technical support, and on-time delivery.

**Old description:** The Sigma Plastics Group was founded in 1978 by Alfred Teo and is the largest privately owned film extrusion group in North America. ## About
Reliance Packaging operates a 160,000-square-foot manufacturing facility in Aberdeen, North Carolina, as a member of the Sigma Plastics Group. The Sigma Plastics Group was founded in 1978 by Alfred Teo and has grown into the largest privately owned film extrusion group in North America, with over 40 facilities and an annual throughput exceeding 2 billion pounds of resin. Reliance Packaging functions as a product specialist within this network, focusing on heavy-duty bags and printed films for specific market segments.

The company extrudes, prints, and converts heavy-duty bags and printed roll-stock using five blown extrusion lines, wide-web flexographic presses, and bag-converting equipment. Its extrusion capacity reaches 20 million pounds annually with a maximum web width of 90 inches and roll outer diameters up to 30 inches. Printing is handled by a 52-inch Schiavi six-color press, a 50-inch PCMC INFINITI eight-color press, and a 50-inch inline flexographic press. Converting includes three side-weld bag machines. A turnkey in-house graphic design department supports custom artwork and plate preparation.

Reliance Packaging serves the lawn and garden, ice-bag, industrial, food, and agricultural markets. Its product line includes mulch and pine bark bags, soil and compost bags, decorative rock bags, play sand and paver base bags, surface-printed and reverse-printed laminated roll stock, food-grade bags and film, freezer-capable ice bags, bailer bags, bags on wickets, industrial bags and films, furniture bags, box and drum liners, and printed industrial shrink film.

The company emphasizes quick turn-around times and quality products, highlighting non-slip ultra-strong film with UV inhibitors and fade-resistant inks designed to perform in tough outdoor conditions. As part of a certified Minority Business Enterprise (NMSDC), Reliance Packaging operates within Sigma Plastics Group's philosophy of domestic manufacturing, sustainability through the How2Recycle program, and independent facility specialization backed by shared capacity.

# Richmond Auto Parts Technology, Inc

## About
Richmond Auto Parts Technology, Inc. (RAPT) operates in Richmond, Kentucky, roughly 36 miles south of Lexington and 10 miles north of Berea. The company was established in 1998 with a $23 million capital investment from its Japanese parent, Aikitec Co., Ltd., and began mass production of automatic transmission gears in July 1999 with an initial team of 20 associates.

RAPT functions as a Tier I and Tier II supplier to the automotive industry, specializing in automatic transmission gears and component parts. The 58-acre campus encompasses a 213,500-square-foot facility staffed by approximately 170 associates. Production capabilities include forging, machining, and assembly for transmissions ranging from 5-speed to 10-speed units, as well as hybrid-electric vehicle (HEV) transmission components. The company has launched programs for 8-speed and 10-speed automatic transmissions and HEV parts.

Primary customers are major Japanese automakers. RAPT has supplied transmission components to Honda since 2000, including 5-speed, 10-speed, and HEV parts, and to Toyota for 8-speed and 10-speed automatic transmissions. The company has received multiple supplier performance awards from both OEMs, including Honda Excellence in Quality and Delivery awards and a Toyota Excellent Quality Performance Award in 2023.

RAPT attributes its differentiation to an associate-centric philosophy summarized in its slogan: “A company that grows strong and survives with associate ideas and knowledge.” The organization emphasizes safety, quality, and production discipline, and maintains ISO 9001:2015 registration. Environmental compliance and community stewardship are also stated priorities.
