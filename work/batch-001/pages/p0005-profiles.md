# Custom Coils

**Old description:** Welcome to Custom Coils, your go-to location for custom transformers and other specialty magnetic components. Companies all over the world that rely on magnetic components for their own production have trusted in Custom Coils to deliver the parts they need since 1967. Custom Coils not only manufactures parts like custom transformers, it also creates designs specifically to meet the needs of its individual clients.

## About
Custom Coils operates from Alcester, South Dakota, where it was founded in 1967. The company manufactures custom transformers, inductors, and specialty magnetic components for a global customer base.

The 17,000-square-foot facility employs over 100 people and serves more than 140 active accounts, with no single account exceeding 16 percent of sales. Primary winding capabilities include complex hand winding, high-voltage layer winding, multi-spindle high-speed bobbin winding, and heavy-wire winding; the secondary area adds universal (pi) winding and toroid winding. Production test equipment comprises inductance analyzers, hipot testers, automated testers, and micro-ohm meters. The product range spans high-voltage, switchmode, flyback, trigger pulse, step-up/down, power, toroidal, and surface-mount transformers, as well as air-core, ferrite, and power inductors, antennas, air coils, DC igniters, and hydrogen coils.

Customers come from agriculture technology, heavy industrial, bio-medical devices, oil exploration and downhole, plasma generation, communications, aerospace and military, seismic exploration, high-tech mining, power distribution, semiconductor and electronics manufacturing, renewable energy and power generation, robotics and industrial automation, and automotive and electric vehicles.

The company emphasizes personalized service: engineers build their own samples, a key manager handles customer service, and top management visits every client. Custom Coils also offers reverse engineering to replace end-of-life components, custom prototyping, assisted design, and through-hole PCB assembly for up to 15 components. All products are manufactured in the United States.
