# Arkansas Poly & Printing

**Old description:** Nearly 50 years ago, APP began as a manufacturer of clear polyethylene bags, primarily in the poultry industry. As we grew, we recognized an opportunity to serve other industries with our expertise. Today, we engage in the extrusion, printing and converting of our own raw materials. The vertical integration of our production process allows us greater control over our scheduling, shortens lead times, and affords us opportunities to expand our market area.

## About
Arkansas Poly & Printing (APP) was established in 2001 in Van Buren, Arkansas, when partners Sarah Sparks Diebold and R. Trent Goins acquired a legacy polyethylene bag business that traced its roots to the early 1970s. The original founder, Ben Gosey, started with a single used bag machine, selling by day and producing by night, and later expanded into printing and extrusion operations in Fort Smith before consolidating in Van Buren.

Today APP operates as a vertically integrated custom flexible packaging manufacturer on a 10‑acre campus with 68,000 square feet of production space and a 5,000‑square‑foot office. The company extrudes its own film — offering LDPE, LLDPE, MDO, multi‑layer, PCR, biodegradable and compostable grades — prints up to 10 colors on flexographic presses equipped with AVT scan and ColorCert technology, and converts the printed web into bags, pouches, liners, sleeves and wicketed products through slitting, rewinding, folding, laminating, zip‑lock and gusseting. Additional in‑house services include graphic design, plate making, inventory management, warehousing and a private fleet for shipping.

APP serves the poultry industry — its original market — as well as broader food and beverage processors that require SQF and Global Food Safe certified packaging. Its customer base spans regional, national and international accounts seeking custom poly bags, films, liners and retail merchandise bags.

The company emphasizes vertical integration as its core differentiator, citing control over scheduling, shorter lead times and the ability to recycle 100 % of ink, scrap film and corrugated material. Quality systems include a HACCP‑based process, internal testing, and a reported product return rate below 1 %.
