## About
Electro Soft, Inc. (ESI) was founded in 1986 by Jim and Sheila Wallace in Montgomeryville, Pennsylvania, just outside Philadelphia. The company remains family-owned and operated; their daughter Karla Trotman, who started cutting wire by hand as a child, now serves as president and CEO. From its start as a small wire-harness shop, ESI has grown into one of the largest independent electronics contract manufacturers in the Philadelphia Tri-State area.

ESI builds custom wiring harnesses, cable assemblies, printed circuit boards, cabinet assemblies, and military electronics. Its 32 full-time skilled professionals work in over 16,000 square feet of office and warehouse space, performing all assembly in-house—including wave and surface-mount soldering, panel assembly, and box build. The shop runs no-minimum prototypes through large-volume batches, maintains records and drawings for three years, and offers repair, rework, and upgrade services.

The company serves aviation, rail, transit, OEM, and defense customers. A noted partnership includes a 20-year relationship supplying components for autonomous guided vehicles (AGVs), and ESI’s ITAR registration supports defense work. Every engagement includes on-staff engineering support, flexible run sizes, and just-in-time delivery.

ESI positions itself as the “value leader” in electronics contract manufacturing, emphasizing quality and service at competitive prices. The founders’ original focus on linked component product lines, quality, and service remains the stated driver of growth, reinforced by ISO 9001 certification and a commitment to never outsourcing production.
