## About
Keco Inc was founded in 1963 by the Keister brothers as an injection molding shop in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. Chris White joined the company as an engineer in 1994, became general manager in 2000, and purchased the business in 2004; he remains owner and president. The company operates as a single, engineering-led facility where molding, assembly, engineering, and fulfillment share the same shop floor.

The company provides full-service contract manufacturing centered on plastic injection molding across a 28- to 1,100-ton press range, supporting prototype, short-run, and high-volume production. In-house capabilities extend to tooling sourcing and validation, product engineering review and manufacturability analysis, mechanical and low-voltage electro-mechanical assembly, functional testing, packaging, kitting, inventory management, and direct-to-customer fulfillment. Over the past decade Keco has shipped more than 100,000 orders from its Oklahoma City plant.

Keco serves OEMs in industrial equipment, oil and gas, automotive aftermarket, foodservice equipment, architectural and electrical products, and consumer goods. Documented programs include collision repair products (SEMA Award 2022), oilfield spill containment systems in continuous production since 1992, architectural lighting assemblies since 1973, consumer plumbing tools fulfilled through Amazon FBA, and plant saucers for direct-to-consumer and wholesale channels.

Keco differentiates itself through an engineering-led operating model where decisions are made by staff who understand the product, tooling, and process. It offers a single accountable team from prototype through customer delivery, a broad tonnage range on one floor, and U.S.-based production in Oklahoma City. The company emphasizes total program cost over piece price, combining domestic molding with assembly, inventory, and fulfillment to reduce risk and ensure repeatability, fast problem solving, and dependable supply.
