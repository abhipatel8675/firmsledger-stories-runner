## About
HLC Industries, Inc. was founded in 1916 in Bala Cynwyd, Pennsylvania. The company began as a woven nylon manufacturer and has since specialized in producing fabrics that meet U.S. military specifications and Berry Amendment compliance.

The company manufactures woven nylon fabrics for military, tactical, parachute, and commercial/industrial applications. Its product lines include Mil-Spec fabrics, tactical nylon fabrics, parachute fabrics, commercial fabrics, and branded materials such as CORDURA® Truelock™ and Multicam®. HLC also provides fabric finishing services and value-added offerings like an in-stock program for CORDURA® fabrics.

HLC serves contractors and manufacturers that supply the U.S. Armed Forces as well as militaries worldwide. It also supports commercial and industrial customers. The company is a sustaining member of the Parachute Industry Association (PIA), reflecting its focus on parachute-grade textiles.

HLC emphasizes continual innovation in processes, emerging technologies, and advanced materials to deliver longer-lasting textiles. Its stated advantage lies in Berry Amendment-compliant, U.S.-manufactured fabrics and an experienced sales team.
