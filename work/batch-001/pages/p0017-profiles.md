Its facility includes high-speed electronic fruit graders, automated bagging machines, and a large multilevel cooler. Peace River serves the fresh citrus market, supplying fruit to customers globally through the Florida’s Natural Growers cooperative. Its primary industries are citrus production, fresh fruit packing, and agricultural cooperatives.

The founders established the packinghouse to give local growers collective control over packing and marketing, a structure that continues as a cooperative today.

Our range of material and processing technologies allow us to provide optimum solutions for innovation-driven companies, ranging from entrepreneurial start-ups to major medical device manufacturers. From prototype development through full-scale production, we take pride in our quality, technical expertise, and customer service, and we strive to provide the best value throughout the product life cycle.

## About
Polyzen LLC was founded in 1991 by polymer chemist Tilak M. Shah in Apex, North Carolina, to serve as a contract developer and supplier for medical device OEMs and start-ups. Now part of the Solesis family of companies—alongside Secant Group, SanaVita Medical, and Charter Medical—Polyzen operates a 75,000-square-foot facility near the Raleigh-Durham research corridor.

The company develops and manufactures customized polymer-based materials, films, components, and finished assemblies for medical devices. Its processing capabilities span elastomer and silicone formulation, specialty film extrusion, barrier films and laminates, dip molding and coating, stent and wire coatings, low- and high-pressure balloon production, multi-layer balloons, catheter fabrication and assembly, lay-flat sheathes and tubing, thin-film welding, and thermoforming. Polyzen supports projects from prototype through full-scale production, offering validation, packaging, and sterilization management under one roof.

Polyzen serves the medical device and life sciences sectors, working with entrepreneurial start-ups as well as major OEMs developing Class I, II, and III devices. Its quality system complies with FDA cGMP (21 CFR Part 820), ISO 13485:2016, ISO 9001:2015, and Japanese MHLW requirements, enabling global supply.

The firm’s stated mission is to accelerate product realization by acting as an extension of customers’ engineering and manufacturing teams. Core principles emphasize urgency in turning ideas into solutions, attention to detail in quality and sustainability, and creating value for patients. Integrated R&D with sister companies further expands its material science and processing expertise.

# Powell Industries

**Old description:** Powell Industries is a manufacturer and integrator of electrical power equipment. Product line includes low- and medium-voltage switchgear, motor control centers, transformers and power control rooms.

## About
Powell Industries was founded in 1947 by William E. Powell in Houston, Texas, initially as a metal-working shop serving the local petrochemical industry. The company developed the Power Control Room concept in 1968 for a Union Carbide project in Puerto Rico, marking an early move into integrated electrical infrastructure.

Today Powell operates globally with over 1,000 employees, designing and manufacturing low- and medium-voltage switchgear, motor control centers, transformers, and power control rooms. Its portfolio includes ANSI metal-clad and metal-enclosed switchgear, IEC-rated switchgear, and the PowlVac Series 5 circuit breaker, supported by custom engineering, centralized project management, and integrated package solutions.

The company serves oil and gas refining, offshore production, petrochemical plants, pipelines, data centers, electric utilities, traction power, renewables, mining and metals, commercial construction, pulp and paper, and government agencies. It also lists power utilities and general industrial markets among its core sectors.

Powell distinguishes itself through a single-source model that combines centralized engineering and project management with global factory and service locations, an approach rooted in its original mission to support complex electrical infrastructure needs. The company emphasizes a safety-first culture and a "can-do" attitude toward solving customer challenges.
