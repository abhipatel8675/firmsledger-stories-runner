## About
Woodworks Distilling Co. operates from a facility in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. The company was founded by Dave Wood and David Alexander to continue the craft-distilling tradition of small batches, local ingredients, and artisanal attention. Wood serves as founder and CEO, drawing on a background in retail, warehousing, and distribution, while Alexander serves as distiller, bringing expertise in chemistry, regulatory compliance, and cocktail design.

The distillery produces a range of spirits including corn-based vodka distilled six times, a four-grain vodka, dry and barrel-aged gins, bourbon and rye whiskeys sourced through its Preface Series, and an apple-pie corn liquor under the partner brand Horns Down Fine Spirits. Liqueurs such as Encheva and Nocino are in development. Operations cover distilling, barrel aging, blending, and bottling on-site, and the facility also functions as an event space for private functions and live music.

Woodworks sells directly to consumers through its tasting room and online, and distributes to bars, restaurants, and retailers in Oklahoma. The Horns Down partnership extends the portfolio into sports-oriented occasions. The company also hosts weddings, corporate events, and chef dinners, managed by front-of-house manager Abbey Elliot, who has nine years of beverage-industry experience.

The founders state that wood influences every facet of production, literally and figuratively, so that each spirit tells a distinct story. They launched the business to write the next chapter in craft distilling, aiming to earn the trial and ongoing support of drinkers who seek distinctive flavors and provenance.
