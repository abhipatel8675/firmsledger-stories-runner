**Old description:** The ZDC Tech’s patented hybrid dielectric sensors are the world’s most advanced humidity/dew point sensors manufactured using the latest nanotechnology, featuring with long-term stability, fast response, and durability in extremely high/low humidity levels. It can be recovered even after exposure to liquid water! It can monitor both dew point and temperature with a temperature sensor provided. Proper use of the meter can provide lifelong reliability without recalibration. The superior performance of the sensor makes it an ideal choice for many industrial applications.

## About
ZDC Tech is based in Lexington, Kentucky. The company traces its roots to 2011 when Advanced Semiconductor Processing Technology (ASPT) was established to commercialize a novel humidity sensor invented by Dr. David Chen, a professor of electrical engineering at the University of Kentucky. ZDC Tech continues that research and development lineage today.

The company designs and manufactures dew point transmitters (models E80 and E100) and portable dew point meters, all built on its patented hybrid dielectric sensor platform produced with nanotechnology methods. It also operates a dew point sensor calibration service. Facility size, headcount, and annual volumes are not disclosed.

ZDC Tech serves industrial customers needing humidity and dew point measurement, though specific sectors are not named in its public materials.

The company emphasizes that its sensors offer long-term stability, fast response, and durability across extreme humidity levels, can recover after liquid water exposure, and provide lifelong reliability without recalibration. A newer generation of sensors is said to respond five times faster than the previous version.
