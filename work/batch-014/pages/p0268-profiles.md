Wood Components & Technologies operates from Cedar Falls, Iowa. The business evolved from a producer of wood bearings for agricultural equipment and washing machines into a manufacturer of wood and plastic OEM parts for a range of industries.

The company produces curved and arched moldings, lineal trim, and specialty millwork for commercial contractors and home builders.
