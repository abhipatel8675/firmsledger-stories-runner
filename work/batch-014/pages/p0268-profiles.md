## About
Wood Components & Technologies operates from Cedar Falls, Iowa. The company's history notes a shift toward manufacturing wood OEM parts for various industries, after earlier production of wood bearings for agricultural equipment and washing machines. No founding year or founder names are publicly disclosed.

The shop produces curved and arched moldings, lineal trim, and specialty millwork for commercial contractors and home builders, as well as wood and plastic OEM components for machinery equipment manufacturers. Core processes include CNC machining, arched and curved millwork, and lineal millwork. The facility also turns out custom arch windows, custom doors, and machined handrails. Headcount, facility size, and annual volume are not published.

Customers span commercial construction (architects, building centers, contractors, homeowners) and industrial OEMs in machinery, agriculture, and appliance sectors. A featured project for Jokers Comedy Club in Cedar Falls included custom arch windows, custom doors, and machined handrail.
