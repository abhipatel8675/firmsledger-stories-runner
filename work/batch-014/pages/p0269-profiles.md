## About
American Woods operates from Grand Forks, North Dakota, where it was founded in 1982 by Lowell and Georgia Innes. The business traces its roots to 1979, when Wood Products Inc. began as a cabinet shop serving Innes Construction, the founders' home-building company. As commercial projects grew, the shop expanded its capabilities, and in 1982 American Woods was launched to meet customer demand for hardwood accessories. The two entities merged under the American Woods name in 2003, combining custom cabinetry and accessory lines under one roof.

The company runs a furniture manufacturing facility of approximately 160,000 square feet staffed by a 100-person team. Production covers the full sequence from milling, machining, sanding and sawing through sub-assembly, finishing and final assembly. Daily truck shipments move finished goods to distribution points nationwide. The operation is equipped to produce a wide range of home furnishing products, including indoor and outdoor collections, using real hardwoods and other premium materials.

American Woods supplies finished furniture to multiple nationally recognized brands. A long-standing partnership with Room & Board dates to 2001 and includes the Berkeley, Copenhagen and Danbury lines. Earlier, the workshop produced cedar outdoor pieces for major retailers such as Target, Costco and Lowe's. The customer base consists primarily of resellers and national brands seeking American-made, high-end furniture for retail distribution.

The founders' original vision — to craft timeless, quality, real wood products made in America and built to last — remains the stated mission. The company emphasizes craftsmanship, consistency and efficiency across every production stage, attributing its differentiation to the dedication of its team and a culture where integrity and pride guide each step.

## About
Based in Colfax, Wisconsin, Woods to Workshop LLC began when its founder, a hobby woodworker and engineer by trade, decided to build a timber-frame workshop and saw his own lumber. Recognizing that many quality logs were being wasted as firewood or left to rot, he started offering custom sawing services to others in the region.

The company provides mobile custom sawing within a 100-mile radius of Menomonie, Wisconsin, operating a band sawmill capable of plane (dimensional) sawing for construction-grade lumber and grade sawing to maximize high-quality, knot-free boards. Additional services include band saw blade sharpening and setting using a Wood Mizer CBN profile sharpener and dual tooth setter, chainsaw chain sharpening with an Oregon 520-120 grinder, and woodworking 3D modeling for concept evaluation and CNC routing.

Customers range from weekend hobbyists and serious enthusiasts to timber-frame builders and small-scale construction projects. The service area covers western Wisconsin, with the founder noting that clients trust him to maximize the value of their limited log resources for projects such as custom furniture, structural timbers, and specialty lumber.

The founder’s engineering background drives a focus on precision and detail, aiming to save customers time and money not only during sawing but throughout their subsequent projects. This commitment to maximizing log value and reducing waste is cited as the core reason the business was started.
