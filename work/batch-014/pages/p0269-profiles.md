## About
American Woods traces its roots to 1979 when Wood Products Inc. began as a cabinet shop serving Innes Construction in Grand Forks, North Dakota. The business expanded into commercial cabinetry, and in 1982 Lowell and Georgia Innes founded American Woods to meet customer demand for matching hardwood accessories. The two entities merged under the American Woods name in 2003, combining custom cabinetry with a growing line of wood goods under single ownership.

Today the company operates a roughly 160,000-square-foot furniture manufacturing facility in Grand Forks with a workforce of about 100. The plant runs milling, machining, sanding, sawing, sub-assembly, finishing, and final assembly lines, producing a wide range of home furnishing products from real hardwoods and premium materials. Daily truck shipments move finished goods to distribution points across the country.

American Woods supplies finished furniture to multiple nationally recognized brands, including long-standing partner Room & Board (producing the Berkeley, Copenhagen, and Danbury lines) and former mass-market programs for Target, Costco, and Lowe's. The company also works directly with resellers seeking luxury furniture solutions and maintains its own branded collections.

The business emphasizes American-made craftsmanship, consistency, and efficiency, framing its differentiation around enduring partnerships, a made-in-America promise, and a team culture built on pride and integrity.

# Woods, To, Workshop, LLC,.

**Old description:** Let's turn your logs into valuable lumber the way you want it, where you want it. Whether you're a weekend warrior or a serious enthusiast, we provide the fuel for your woodworking desire at a cost you can afford.

## About
Woods to Workshop LLC operates from Colfax, Wisconsin, offering mobile custom sawing services within a 100-mile radius of Menomonie. The business began when its founder, a hobby woodworker and engineer by trade, built a timber frame workshop and decided to saw his own lumber to save money and apply his knowledge of wood. Seeing usable logs turned into firewood or left to rot, he started sawing for others to put that resource to work.

The company provides plane (dimensional) sawing for construction-grade lumber and grade sawing to maximize high-quality, knot-free boards. It also sharpens and sets band saw blades using a Wood Mizer CBN profile sharpener and a Woodmizer Dual Tooth Setter, and sharpens chainsaw chains with an Oregon 520-120 grinder. In addition, it offers woodworking 3D modeling from concept evaluation through CNC routing.

Customers range from weekend warriors to serious enthusiasts who need custom sawn lumber for personal projects. The service area covers hobbyists and small-scale builders across western Wisconsin who bring their own logs for processing.

The goal is to save clients time and money not only at the sawmill but throughout their subsequent projects.
